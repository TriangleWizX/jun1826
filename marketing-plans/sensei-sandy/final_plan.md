## 1. Executive Summary

### Goal Focus
This roadmap establishes a $0 budget, organic-led acquisition engine to enroll kids, teens, and adults in Sensei Sandy BJJ. It focuses on local SEO search authority and local government youth bureau alignments.

### Three Big Bets
1. **Greene County Youth Bureau Partnership (OCFS):** Align our youth programs with local county youth advocacy and prevention frameworks (Pre-PINS). This provides direct, free channels to mountaintop families.
2. **Local Search Engine Domination:** Optimize Tannersville and surrounding Catskills town landing pages to capture mountaintop searches for family sports and fitness.
3. **Structured Word-of-Mouth (WOM) Flywheels:** Use parent-to-parent referrals and bring-a-friend passes to turn active students into our primary organic customer acquisition channel.

### Plausible Twelve-Month Outcomes
- 25 new core-term youth enrollments.
- 15 new core-term adult enrollments.
- 1 active youth program partnership with the Greene County Youth Bureau.
- Top-three search results for BJJ and martial arts in Tannersville, Hunter, and Windham.

3. Deploy parent-to-parent referral cards and bring-a-friend passes at the studio.

## 2. Strategic Frame

### One-Sentence Definition
Sensei Sandy BJJ provides calm, coach-led, small-group Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu classes capped at 12 students in Tannersville, NY.

### Category Claim
We operate in the local youth development and adult fitness category. We claim the "Beginner Lane" positioning, focusing on calm instruction, safety walkthroughs, and injury prevention, in contrast to high-ego fight gyms.

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Mountaintop Parents:** Greene County families who want a safe, structured, screen-free after-school sport for kids (5-9) and teens (10-17).
- **Adult Beginners:** Adults aged 18 to 60 who want to learn self-defense and build mobility without fear of injury.
- **First Responders / LEOs:** Local public service workers seeking restraint techniques and tactical durability.

### Business Model Logic
We monetize through 12-week core packages (from $425 to $715), private coaching ($195 per session), and visitor passes ($35). Our $0 acquisition budget requires organic channels to fuel core package renewals and new enrollments.

### Brand Voice Non-Negotiables
- **Tone:** Calm, safe, welcoming, expert, community-oriented.
- **Rules:** Avoid aggressive fight language, combat-sports hype, and terms like "sparring" or "contracts" in our primary customer-facing copy.

### The Plan

- **Move 2 — Beginner Lane Pacing:** Limit class sizes to 12 students to guarantee personal attention for new starts.
- **Move 3 — Show-Up Kit Delivery:** Text the arrival instructions and waiver links immediately after booking a Free Intro.

## 6. Retention

### Current State
Rescheduling text options help families keep their class routines.

### The Plan
- **Move 1 — Open-Seat Rescheduling by Text:** Allow parents and adults to adjust training days by text message to reduce dropout rates.
- **Move 2 — Monthly Progress Checks:** Coach Sandy conducts brief, verbal check-ins with youth parents and adults during training week 4 and week 8 to gauge progress.
- **Move 3 — Fit Guarantee Promotion:** Highlight the 30-day fit guarantee to build confidence during the initial term.

### 90-Day Retention Moves
- **Weeks 1–4:** Add the text-rescheduling instructions to the welcome packet.
- **Weeks 5–8:** Set up a simple spreadsheet to track core term renewal dates.
- **Weeks 9–12:** Implement check-in prompts in our weekly schedule reminders.

### 12-Month Retention Outlook
