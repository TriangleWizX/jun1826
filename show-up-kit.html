Your First Visit

A calm sequence from door to mat.

Nothing here is rushed. The first class is built to help you settle in, understand the room, and start with clear direction.

  1. 01

    Room tour

    You'll know where to put your things, where to stand, and how the room works.

  2. 02

    Safety walkthrough

    We cover a few basics and ensure skill-based resistance activities begin at the right pace from day one.

  3. 03

    Beginner-paced movement

    You'll move, learn, and settle in at a comfortable pace with clear coaching.

  4. 04

    Next-step clarity

    We'll help you choose the next step that fits you best without pressure.
