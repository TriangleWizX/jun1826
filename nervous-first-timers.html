The Difference

Calm beginner-friendly martial arts means more than a soft slogan.

It means the first visit is organized, we teach grappling without striking, we welcome questions, and the coach gives you one clear next step instead of leaving you to decode the room alone.

If you are an adult beginner in the Catskills, start with the adult program. If you are choosing for a child, use the kids program. If you want one-on-one support first, see private coaching.

Questions

First-Timer FAQ

How is the first class paced for first-timers?

The first visit is built around a room tour, safety walkthrough, Beginner Lane orientation, and a clear next step.

What happens in my first Jiu-Jitsu class?

You meet Sandy, see the room, learn how tapping works, get paired carefully, and move through beginner-paced instruction before any intensity increases.

Do I have to spar hard on day one?

No. We focus on the gentle art of control and leverage, ensuring smaller grapplers feel safe and welcomed. The first small-group class is coached. Beginners start with safe, coached, beginner-appropriate partner activities where Sandy adjusts the position, partner, pace, and resistance.

What if I want a quieter first step?

Private classes are available for beginners who want one-on-one coaching before or alongside Classes.
