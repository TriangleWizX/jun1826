5-Class Reward Terms

Five-Class Reward Terms

Members who log at least five attended classes during the month of December unlock a complimentary January Guest Pass.

  • Reward is attendance-based, not tuition promotion.
  • Triggered automatically upon completion of class 5 in December.
  • Nontransferable for cash value or tuition discounts.
January Guest Pass

January Guest-Pass Rules

Use your earned guest pass to invite a local friend, family member, or coworker to a coached Free Intro in January.

  • Guest must be a first-time visitor to Sensei Sandy BJJ.
  • Requires advance Goal Mapping booking by the guest.
  • Valid through January 31, 2027.
