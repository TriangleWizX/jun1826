Community Partners

Referral pages for hospitality, wellness, camps, and schools.

Sensei Sandy BJJ partners with local hotels, restaurants, wellness providers, camps, and schools around Tannersville, Hunter, Windham, Haines Falls, and the Catskills. Use these pages when a guest, parent, student, or staff member wants a calm first step into Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Each referral path points people toward the right Free Intro, directions to the second-floor studio, and beginner-friendly class options for kids, teens, and adults.

Use this page as the crawlable directory for the partner pages that explain how referrals work, who each offer fits, and where families can find the right local entry point.

Hospitality and Tourism

For hotels, guest guides, and visitor planning around Hunter, Windham, and nearby Catskills stays.

Hospitality Partners A private, beginner-friendly Jiu-Jitsu experience guests can book while they are in town. Scribner’s Guide The Scribner’s-specific landing page for a calm first visit and room-tour questions.

Wellness and Professional Referrals

Wellness and PT Referrals Private coaching and trackable referrals for cautious beginners and movement-focused clients. Camp Clinics Rain-proof indoor movement blocks for camps that need reliable schedule relief.

Youth Organizations

For schools, youth leaders, and family-facing organizations who want a calm on-ramp into the room.

School Families Beginner-lane guidance for kids, teens, and parents who want a clear first step. Youth Camp Planning Indoor clinic options for groups that need safe movement, teamwork, and structure.
