Who We Are

A small studio built around steady progress.

Sensei Sandy BJJ is a coach-led studio in Tannersville, New York. Sandy is a BJJ Black Belt who competes actively. He teaches with clear language and patient pacing so you always know where to begin.

We teach foundational grappling principles. You will make steady progress in a room that supports you.

Our Standard of Care

  • We keep semi-private class sizes capped so every student receives direct coaching.
  • We remember our seasonal students; you return to a room that knows you.
  • We notice extended absences and reach out, not to pressure, but to check in.
  • We make scheduling frictionless. Students reschedule by texting the coach.
