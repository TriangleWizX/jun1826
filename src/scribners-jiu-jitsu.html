Private Coaching

What is the visit like?

A coach greets you personally, shows you the room, and guides each step. You set the pace and ask questions whenever you like.

Step 1

Meet the coach

Discuss what you want to learn, past athletic background, and session focus.

Step 2

See the room

Tour our light-filled second floor studio, changing area, and clean mat space.

Step 3

Learn the basics

Try simple, mechanical movements that emphasize posture and pure leverage.

Step 4

Flexible times

Coordinate directly with Sandy if mountain hikes, weather, or dining times shift.
