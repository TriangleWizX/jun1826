Keep Your Sport. Try Jiu-Jitsu.

Add a coached Jiu-Jitsu class alongside your main sport. Your coach guides each step and sets the pace.

Kids and teens are ages 5–17. Adults are ages 18 and up. Sandy can help you choose a class.

Who can ask about it

Kids, teens, and adults in sports like soccer, skiing, running, cycling, and hiking can ask if a Jiu-Jitsu class fits their goals and schedule. Your main sport stays first.

What happens during class

Class starts with a coach-led warm-up and skill practice. Students learn balance, how to fall and stand with coach guidance, and how to make choices with a partner at a pace set by the coach.

Find a class that fits

Kids and teens ages 5–17 have class at 5:00 PM Monday through Friday. Adults ages 18 and up have class at 6:00 PM Monday through Friday and Saturday at 10:30 AM. Formats vary by day. Ask Sandy which class fits your sport and week.

Choose your next step

Start with a free 15-minute visit to talk with Sandy about your goals and plan a class. Kids and teens follow their own first-class plan. Adults may ask about flexible options after their visit.

Evidence and limitations

This is complementary movement practice, not medical care or a performance guarantee. Sensei Sandy focuses on motor control, balance, and leverage rather than health claims, speed records, or competitive results. Read the parent evidence and limitations handout.

What should I wear?

For the visit, wear your day clothes. If you train, wear clean sports clothes and follow the class plan Sandy sends you.
