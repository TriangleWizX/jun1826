Your Free First Visit and First Class

Starting Jiu-Jitsu should feel clear before you choose a paid program.

1. BJJ first visit

Adults meet Sandy for a 15-minute First Visit at the studio. Kids and teens arrive 20 minutes before their selected youth class.

You will tour the room, discuss goals, review schedule needs, ask questions, and choose the right first small-group class. Parents can discuss their child’s experience, comfort, and learning needs.

2. Coached First Class

Adults schedule their coached first class with Sandy. Kids and teens review safety and may train that day.

Experience the coaching, training partners, safety structure, and class format before deciding whether to continue.
