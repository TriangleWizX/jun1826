Dojo Standards

Four Core Class Safety Rules

Our room culture prioritizes partner protection, mutual respect, and emotional calm.

Immediate Tap Respect

When someone taps, let go at once. The tap is an absolute stop signal that preserves safety for everyone.

Controlled Movement

Move with care and follow the coach. Leverage, balance, and technique always take precedence over speed.

Disciplined Restraint

Keep hands to yourself outside drills. Practice begins and pauses only when directed by your coach.

Calm & Respectful Words

Use kind, encouraging language with teammates. We build each other up through every training round.

Zero tolerance for aggression: Threats, bullying, roughhousing, or using a skill to intimidate someone will immediately conclude class time.

Studio Health Standards

Clean Mats & Personal Hygiene

We maintain hospital-grade sanitation to keep our training environment healthy year-round.

  • Wear clean training clothes
  • Keep fingernails and toenails short
  • Clean and cover any minor cuts
  • Stay home and rest when sick
  • Always wear shoes off the mat
Student Care

If Someone Needs a Pause

Students are always encouraged to take breaks whenever needed. We stop the drill, check in, and allow time to rest, hydrate, and reset.

For youth classes, staff speak directly with parents. Parents are always invited and encouraged to watch training from our seating area.

Coaching Philosophy

Ask for Help at Any Time

Sensei Sandy is on the mat for every minute of class. If you ever feel uncertain about a technique, partner pace, or physical sensation, simply signal Sandy.

Our beginner lane adapts to your physical baseline so you progress safely week by week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Beginner Safety Questions

Clear, direct answers for first-time students and parents.

Is Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu safe for beginners?
Yes. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu at Sensei Sandy BJJ is safe for beginners. Training begins with a studio tour, a safety walkthrough, and clear rules. Every drill is coach-led with carefully matched partners, controlled movement, and mutual tapping so you set your own pace from day one.
Do beginners spar live or get thrown on their first day?
No. First-time students participate in cooperative positional movement and coached resistance games. Live, unrestricted sparring is reserved for experienced practitioners who have demonstrated consistent defensive control and tap respect.
What if I am out of shape or have old injuries?
You do not need to be in shape before starting. BJJ develops functional endurance and mobility naturally. Tell Sandy about any pre-existing injuries during your first visit so drills can be modified to keep your joints protected.
Can parents watch youth and teen classes?
Yes, absolutely. We maintain an open, transparent dojo. Parents and family members are always welcome to observe classes from our comfortable seating area.
