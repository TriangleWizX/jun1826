Inclusion at Sensei Sandy BJJ

Everyone is welcome

I teach youth and adults. I welcome students of every race, color, ethnicity, and national origin. I welcome all cultures, languages, and faiths.

I welcome every sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, and gender expression. I welcome people of every body size and income, people with disabilities or neurodivergence, and people at every level of martial-arts experience.

Ask me which class fits your age and needs.

Coaching standards

  • I explain each drill and give you time to practice.
  • I use your name and pronouns. I respect your limits and starting point.
  • I consider size and experience when I choose partners. I set the pace for each drill.
  • I coach each student and give feedback. Students in the same program use the same advancement standards.
  • I act when someone’s behavior affects safety or respect.
  • We talk about changes you ask for before you train.

Conduct rules

Discrimination, slurs, harassment, bullying, sexual misconduct, retaliation, and unsafe training are not allowed in the studio or in messages about the academy.

Every student and guest must follow these rules:

  • Follow my safety directions.
  • Ask your partner before you raise the pace or effort.
  • Let go right away when a partner taps or asks to pause.
  • Use kind words and respectful touch.
  • Tell me about unsafe behavior, harassment, or an access barrier.

Read the class rules for partner safety, respect, cleanliness, and health.

Access and accommodations

Before class, tell me what helps you learn. Share any limits on movement or touch. Tell me what help you or your child may need.

You choose whether to share a diagnosis. Please tell me enough about your needs to plan your class and choose suitable partners.

We can talk about changes before you train. I may let you watch first or shorten your first class. I can repeat directions or send notes after class. I may change a movement, choose partners, set the pace, or suggest another class.

Ask me before your visit about the entrance, restroom, sensory conditions, or service animals. I will share the current details I have and answer your questions.

Safety and class fit

Class uses partner drills and close contact. Some drills include pressure or a partner’s weight. Ask me to explain a drill before you try it.

Tell me when you need to pause. Follow the coach’s safety directions. Let go right away if a partner taps or asks to pause.

Ask me before class about any change you need. Ask about medical care or one-to-one help before you count on it in a group class. I will tell you what I can offer.

I may suggest a private session or another class if it is a better fit for your needs.

Review the safety walkthrough before your first visit.

Reporting concerns

Tell me in person, by text, or by email about discrimination, harassment, bullying, retaliation, or an access barrier. A parent or caregiver may report a concern for a child.

I will review the concern and speak with the people involved. I share details only with people who need them. I protect people from retaliation when they report a concern in good faith.
