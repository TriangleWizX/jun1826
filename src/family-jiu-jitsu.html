Comparing Family Martial Arts

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu vs. Karate & Taekwondo for Mountaintop Families

Many parents in Tannersville, Hunter, and Windham search for karate or taekwondo when looking for martial arts classes for their children. Here is how Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu helps your family:

Training Focus
Sensei Sandy BJJ Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu
Traditional Striking Arts (Karate / Taekwondo)
Core Movement
Grappling & Ground Balance Students learn to use body weight, mechanical leverage, and gentle holds to stay in control on the mats. Punches & High Kicks Focuses on kicking and punching motions in open space and practicing solo forms (katas).
Partner Safety
Pure Leverage & Early Tapping Movement is trained cooperatively with controlled holds, slow pacing, and immediate tap resets. Reaction Speed & Impact Movement relies on quick reflex blocks and timed pad striking.
Realistic Control
Gentle Ground Control Students learn to hold a partner still on the ground while staying calm until help arrives. Distance & Space Requires open room to kick and step back on the feet.
Class Pacing
Small-Group Care (Max 12) Classes are capped at 12 students with tailored partner pairing and close coaching from Sandy. Large Group Formations Often taught in larger lines with synchronized group counts.
