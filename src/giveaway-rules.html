The Mountain Mat Giveaway: Official Rules

Run by Sensei Sandy BJJ, 6045 Main Street, 2nd Floor Studio, Tannersville, NY 12485.
Text: (917) 736-8649. Email: me@senseisandy.com.

The prizes.

Two prizes. One Adult Core Culture term of 12 weeks, two classes each week (24 classes). One Youth Core Culture term of 12 weeks, two classes each week (24 classes), in the Youth and Teen class. Each prize has every class taught by Sandy, a free academy gi, and text makeups. Full program terms are at senseisandy.com/options-pricing. Class times are at senseisandy.com/schedule. Sensei Sandy BJJ also runs a small thank-you draw for current members for one free private session, drawn the same day. If you join during the contest, you enter a draw for a free private lesson. That draw is Tuesday, November 10, 2026.

Who can enter.

Adults age 18 and up. A parent or guardian age 18 and up enters for a child age 5 to 17 in the youth draw. The main prizes are for people who have never visited the studio. Winners train in person at the studio in Tannersville, each week of the term. One entry per person in each draw. One prize per home.

How to enter.

Entry is free. Text ADULT or YOUTH to (917) 736-8649 and reply with your first name, your town, and whether you have ever visited the studio. Entries count only for people who have never visited the studio. You can also enter on paper at the studio Halloween Spooktacular on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Social media posts share the news. Your text or your paper form is your entry. When you enter by text, you agree that Sandy may text you about your entry and a free first visit. Reply STOP to end texts.

Dates.

Entries open Monday, October 26, 2026 at 7:30 AM ET and close Saturday, November 7, 2026 at 2:00 PM ET.

The draw.

Winners are picked by random draw on camera on Tuesday, November 10, 2026. One backup name is drawn for each prize. Entry counts for each draw are posted before the draw. If a winner does not fit the rules in this page, the backup name takes the prize.

Claim and start.

Each winner claims the prize by booking a Free First Visit by Friday, November 13, 2026. The winner fills in the standard class waiver before the first class. The first class takes place by Saturday, November 28, 2026. The free academy gi is given at the first class. If a winner misses the claim date, the backup name is offered the prize the next day, with a first class date 7 days after the first winner's date.

The term clock.

The 12 weeks start at the winner's first class. Days when the studio is closed for a holiday add the same count of days to the end of the term. The prize is for the named winner. Prizes hold no cash value.

Join during contest week.

A person who enrolls as a student during contest week stays in the draw. If they win, the free term starts right after the paid term. They also enter the draw for a free private lesson, drawn Tuesday, November 10, 2026.

Names in public.

Winner posts use first name and town only. The youth winner is named with a parent's okay, or as a town family. Photos and video of winners are taken only with a clear yes (a parent's yes for youth).

Platform note.

This giveaway is run by Sensei Sandy BJJ. It is not sponsored, endorsed, or run by Instagram, Facebook, or Meta.
