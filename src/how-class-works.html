Timeline · Step by Step

How the 45 Minutes Unfold

Sandy coordinates every partner pairing, task constraint, and resistance pace to ensure learning is smooth and supportive.

03 min Step 1

Arrival & Partner Match

Students arrive, change into clean gear, and step onto the mat. Sandy thoughtfully pairs students by age, size, and experience level.

05 min Step 2

Useful Movement

Students explore 2 to 3 essential mat movements. Practice bridges, hip escapes, and safe technical stand-ups with personal coaching.

06 min Step 3

Chest-to-Chest Connection

A focused mini-game. The bottom student works to recover guard or stand up. The top student focuses on base, balance, and gentle control.

06 min Step 4

Chest-to-Back Control

Learn back control mechanics. One student practices safe turns to recover facing position; the other maintains calm, seatbelt control.

06 min Step 5

Neutral Guard Game

Learn distance management with your legs. One player works to pass guard while the other maintains space, sweeps, or gets up.

15 min Step 6

Calibrated Resistance Rounds

Short, live rounds linking the day's skills. Partners work together at a safe, cooperative pace with frequent pauses and resets.

04 min

Review, Reflection & Mat Reset

Students review what worked, thank their partners, celebrate progress, and tidy the mats together.

The Four-Part Mat Map

Clear Positions Every Beginner Understands

Rather than memorizing dozens of complex steps, students learn where they are on the mat and what their clear job is.

1

Standing

Maintain good posture and balance. Guide the movement safely down to the mat.

2

Top Position

Establish steady base, use body weight smoothly, and improve positional control.

3

Bottom Guard

Use your legs as a shield. Create frames, protect your head, and safely stand back up.

4

Back Safety

Protect your neck, maintain awareness, and turn safely back to face your partner.

Safety Safeguards

The Beginner Lane Experience

First-time students start with calibrated partner pairing, one safety rule, one useful movement, and guided practice.

  • Hand-selected experienced partners for all beginners
  • Immediate tapping and reset protocols practiced first
  • Grounded in published sports medicine and safety research
