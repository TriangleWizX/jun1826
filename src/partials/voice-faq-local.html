Questions people ask before booking

Where do I park?

Check where to park before you leave. Arrive 5 minutes early for a visit. For class, follow the time Sandy gives you.

What should I wear?

For a visit, wear your day clothes. To train, wear clean sports clothes. Follow the class plan Sandy sends you.

How do I start?

Meet Sandy for a free visit. See the room and learn the rules. Talk about your goals. Sandy helps you plan your first class.
