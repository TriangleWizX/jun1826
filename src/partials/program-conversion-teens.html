First visit walkthrough

What happens at a teen Free First Visit?

Your teen starts with a Goal Mapping conversation and a coached class. A parent or guardian stays on-site.

Step 1

Tour & Meet Coach Sandy

Arrive in athletic clothes. We introduce you to the space, answer any questions, and walk through the layout before class starts.

Step 2

Safety Rules & Matched Partner

Learn how to tap, protect your neck and joints, and practice with a calm partner who matches your pace.

Step 3

Coached Positional Practice

Test simple answers against controlled resistance. Enjoy cooperative partner drills and calm guidance. Coached problem solving from day one.

Questions parents & teens ask

Before the first class

Are beginners welcome?

Yes! Every student starts with fundamental movement, safe falling, and matched partner pacing.

What style is practiced?

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu focuses purely on leverage, balance, and positional control.

Can parents watch?

Yes. The studio is open and parents are welcome to observe.
