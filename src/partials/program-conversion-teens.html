What happens at a teen Free First Visit?

Your teen meets Sandy, sees the room, and tries a coached class. A parent or guardian stays on-site. Sandy confirms the class and what to wear.

Plan 60–75 minutes. Come 20 minutes early. Wear clean sports clothes or the gear Sandy asks for. Send the waiver before class so Sandy can confirm the plan.

Step 1.

Tour & Meet Coach Sandy.

Meet Sandy, see the room, and ask your questions.

Step 2.

Safety Rules & Matched Partner.

Learn how to tap and reset. Train with a calm partner at your own pace.

Step 3.

Coached Positional Practice.

Test simple answers with friendly partners. Enjoy calm guidance and cooperative drills from day one.

Questions parents & teens ask.

Before the first class.

Are beginners welcome?

Yes. Every student starts with simple movement, safe falling, and matched partner pacing.

What style is practiced?

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu focuses on leverage, balance, and positional control.

Can parents watch?

Yes. The studio is open and parents are always welcome to observe.
