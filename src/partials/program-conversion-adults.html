First visit walkthrough

How adult beginners start on the mats

Starting martial arts as an adult feels intimidating when you don't know what to expect. Here is how your first visit works:

Step 1

15-Min Consultation & Tour

Come in normal clothes. Meet Coach Sandy, view the studio, and talk through any previous injuries or physical goals with zero pressure.

Step 2

Matched Partner

You are paired with a calm, seasoned partner whose job is to keep you safe and help you learn at your pace.

Step 3

Coached Positional Grappling

Practice fundamental escapes and positional control with controlled resistance. Coach Sandy guides you through every step.

What adults practice

Skills for real resistance

Escape

Get out of uncomfortable positions safely.

Control

Hold positions without hurting a partner.

Adapt

Solve physical problems under pressure.

Ask questions
