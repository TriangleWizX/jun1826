Your child’s first visit.

What happens at a youth Free First Visit?

Your child meets Sandy, sees the room, and tries a coached class. A parent or guardian stays on-site. Sandy confirms the class and gear.

Plan 60–75 minutes. Come 20 minutes early. Wear clean sports clothes or the gear Sandy asks for. Send the waiver first. Sandy will then confirm the class.

Step 1.

Room Tour & Warm Welcome.

Meet Sandy, see the room, and ask your questions.

Step 2.

Safety & Clear Boundaries.

We teach how tapping works and match your child with a helpful partner.

Step 3.

Coached Movement Practice.

Kids practice basic games and balance drills. Parents sit matside and watch the whole time.

What happens in class.

Coached movement and partner practice.

Kids learn movement, balance, safe falling, and control through fun drills. Sandy adjusts the pace so students can learn safely and try their own answer before asking for one.
