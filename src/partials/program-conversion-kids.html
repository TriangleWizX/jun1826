What happens in class

Coached movement and partner practice

You move, learn position, practice with a partner, and review. Class includes movement, control and escapes, guard work, short live rounds, and a review. Sandy changes the partner, pace, and starting spot so the challenge fits the student.

Research and real experience

Research says

Studies of open-skill sports found gains in some thinking skills in children. Jiu-Jitsu also asks kids to react as a partner moves. Results vary, so this is context—not a promise for every child.
