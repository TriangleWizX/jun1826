Your child’s first visit

What happens at a youth Free First Visit?

Kids and teens meet Sandy, then try a class. A parent or guardian stays with them. Sandy confirms the class and what to wear.

Step 1

Room Tour & Arrival

Arrive in clean athletic clothing. Coach Sandy welcomes your family, walks you through the room, and lets your child explore the mats with friendly encouragement.

Step 2

Safety & Boundary Talk

We explain clear boundaries, how tapping works, and match your child with a gentle partner who helps them feel comfortable.

Step 3

Paced Movement Practice

Kids try basic games, safe falling, and balance drills at their own pace. Parents are welcome to sit matside and watch the entire session.

What happens in class

Coached movement and partner practice

Kids learn movement, position, safe falling, and control through age-appropriate activities. Sandy adjusts the pace and challenge so students can learn safely.
