Know the room before your first class

Mats, viewing, and cleanliness

The training area is built around clean mats, clear coaching, and enough space for partners to work safely. A small waiting area sits immediately outside the training room, and students learn the room’s safety expectations before live resistance begins.

Mats and high-touch surfaces are cleaned as part of the studio routine. Class sizes stay intentionally small so Sandy can see the room and adjust instruction to the students present.

Kids, teens, and adults

Kids and teens share the scheduled Youth + Teen class, with partners and coaching adjusted to the student. Adults can start without prior experience, athletic credentials, or a competition goal. The first visit is paced as a coached learning experience.
