Facility Details & Arrival Instructions

Location & Parking: We are located at 6045 Main Street, Studio 2 (Second Floor), Tannersville, NY 12485 inside the Studios on Main building (Tannersville Antique & Artisan Center). Free street parking is available directly on Main Street or in the designated lot behind the building.

Entrance & Arrival: Enter through the front doors on Main Street and take the interior stairs up to Studio 2 on the second floor. A door code and arrival guide are sent via text prior to your first scheduled visit.

Parent Viewing: Parents are welcome to watch youth classes from our dedicated observation seating area with clear sightlines into the room.

Cleaning Standards & Class Cap: Mats are disinfected and sanitized daily using non-toxic antibacterial cleaner. Clean footwear is required off the mat, and high-airflow ventilation maintains a fresh environment. All small-group classes are strictly capped at 12 students to ensure focused, individualized coaching.

The Atmosphere

Sensei Sandy’s studio is a calm, technical space inside the Studios on Main creative campus. Staff clean every surface daily and rotate the mats. Students focus on quiet intent, pacing themselves to learn proper movement. Warm lighting and tall windows fill the room. The space holds enough room for kids, teens, and adults to train in dedicated areas.

Instructors cue positional priorities, breathing, and tempo. Managing stress builds progress. Kids learn to respect their peers’ safety and use their limbs as tools. Teens practice composure under pressure. Adults learn to move while protecting their joints. Students share tips on the whiteboard. Parents watching from the hallway see consistent instruction: clear coaching and a focus on curiosity.

The Expectations during your first month

Your first week includes a free intro tour. You walk through the studio, meet the coach, and train with your age group. The coach explains safety, tapping, and partnering. Weeks two through four introduce foundational drilling: base, movement, posture, and escapes. Sessions end with controlled rounds and resets to ensure measurable practice.

Students build momentum by attending twice a week. We keep the schedule consistent. You can practice at home with our mobility circuits, flow warm-ups, and short positional sequences to stay in rhythm between classes.

Coaching philosophy and lineage

Sensei Sandy brings decades of grappling, tournament, and teaching experience. The curriculum blends Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu traditions with modern movement science. Coaches teach 'safety-grade pressure'. You learn to apply weight, finish techniques, and manage your adrenaline.

The studio belt structure rewards consistency, character, and technical understanding. Students earn promotions through practice. Each stripe sets new expectations. We share requirements before tests. Parents track student progress and practice needs.

More than grappling: community and local roots

The studio sits in the heart of Tannersville’s creative corridor. Students explore the local cafés, galleries, and trails in Hunter, Windham, and Haines Falls. We coordinate with nearby schools for after-school transportation, host community meals, and partner with neighbors for events. Training connects to the town's rhythm.

Adults working remotely train at 6 PM and grab dinner on Main Street. Parents observe quietly while children train independently. Main Street provides step-free elevator access. We send a door code via text before class. The restroom accommodates everyone. You can observe or participate using adaptive instruction. We welcome service animals. We share clear updates in writing before each new session.

Sensei Studio remains small by design. Coaches personalize instruction. Students know each other. We celebrate belt promotions. We highlight better balance, calmer breathing, and maintaining respect under pressure.
