Know the room before your first class

Mats, viewing, and cleanliness

The training area is built around clean mats, clear coaching, and enough space for partners to work safely. Parents can watch youth classes from the viewing area, and students learn the room’s safety expectations before live resistance begins.

Mats and high-touch surfaces are cleaned as part of the studio routine. Class sizes stay intentionally small so Sandy can see the room and adjust instruction to the students present.

Kids, teens, and adults

Kids and teens train in structured age-appropriate groups. Adults can start without prior experience, athletic credentials, or a competition goal. The first visit is paced as a coached learning experience.

Entrance, parking, and arrival

The studio is at 6045 Main Street, 2nd Floor, Tannersville, NY 12485, inside Studios on Main. Plan for street parking on Main Street or the designated lot behind the building, then use the front entrance and interior stairs.

Arrival details and any door instructions are shared before a scheduled first visit. If you need help planning the route, text Sandy before you drive.
