The experience

Seen. Tested. Becoming.

Training here should feel personal without becoming soft: you are met with a problem you can enter, challenged by a partner who is genuinely trying, and noticed as your decisions change. A hard round can still leave you with a clear next attempt.

Seen

Sandy knows what gave you trouble, what improved, and when the difficulty needs changing.

Tested

Resistance makes the learning real without turning the round into a test of whether you belong.

Becoming

Failure becomes specific information, and the next problem gives you somewhere useful to go.
