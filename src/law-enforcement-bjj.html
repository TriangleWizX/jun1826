FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to simple questions. Sandy is glad to help.

Can I join as one person?

Yes. You can join the adult class. You pay our low community service rate. We welcome you on the mats. You will feel at home right away.

Do I need prior martial arts experience?

Meet Sandy for 15 minutes. Talk about your goals. Adults can choose a class after the visit that day. Sandy confirms the class before you train. You can choose a later day.

Do I train in a private class or group?

You train in our regular adult group. We also run private classes for whole teams. Both paths work well. You pick what fits.

Will I train with duty belt gear?

Adult classes focus on clear body balance. Squad teams can add duty gear after review. We keep every drill calm and safe. You move with care. Learn with ease.

Does this supplement departmental training?

This is extra skill practice. Your department policy guides duty actions. We help you stay calm and move well. Safety comes first. We honor your service. Sandy is here to help.

Can I train around changing shifts?

Yes. You can pick from evening and weekend classes each week. You can swap days by text with Sandy.
