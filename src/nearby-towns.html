Travel & Planning

Visiting From Neighboring Towns

Helpful details on parking, family class schedules, and mountain road conditions for your trip to Tannersville.

Where should I park when visiting Studios on Main in Tannersville?

Free street parking is available along Main Street directly in front of the studio entrance. In addition, a dedicated free municipal parking lot is located directly behind Studios on Main, with an easy 30-second walkway straight to our front entrance.

Can parents and children train on the same evening?

Yes. Our schedule is designed with mountain families in mind. Youth classes (Kids and Teens) run in the late afternoon from 5:00 PM, followed directly by adult fundamental sessions at 6:00 PM. Parents can relax in our comfortable seating area during youth class, or join the mats right afterward.

What happens if mountain weather or winter road conditions change?

Catskills mountain weather can shift quickly. If winter road conditions, snow, or travel advisories affect your drive, simply text Sandy at +1 (917) 736-8649. We make it completely stress-free to reschedule your visit to any upcoming session that fits your family.
