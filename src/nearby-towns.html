First visit questions

Common questions before your first visit.

Everything you need to know before stepping into the studio for the first time.

What should I wear to my first class?

Wear simple, comfortable athletic clothes such as a t-shirt or rashguard and athletic shorts or sweatpants. We train barefoot on clean, sanitised mats.

Are complete beginners welcome?

Yes. Most of our students started as complete beginners. Every class includes beginner lane pacing with cooperative, coached partner drills.

Can parents stay and watch?

Parents are always welcome to stay, relax, and watch. We have comfortable seating right alongside the mats with a clear view of the entire class.

How do I reschedule if mountain weather changes?

Text Sandy directly at +1 (917) 736-8649. We understand mountain life and make it easy to choose another class date that fits your family.
