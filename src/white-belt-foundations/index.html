The 24-class path

Six skills, then start again.

Each six-class round has one of each skill type.

Lesson 1 · Tue, Oct 13

Escape: side and headlock

Make space. Find guard. Free your head.

Chest to chest: Turn in and find guard.

Chest to back: Face your partner and free your head.

Neutral guard: Make space and stand up.

Lesson 2 · Thu, Oct 15

Takedown: body lock trip

Trip the near or far leg.

Chest to chest: Hold close and turn to the side.

Chest to back: Turn, step behind, and hold.

Neutral guard: Step in and reach for one leg.

Lesson 3 · Tue, Oct 20

Submit: armbar from guard

Use your legs to hold the arm.

Chest to chest: Frame, turn, and find guard.

Chest to back: Turn in and clear the arm.

Neutral guard: Set up the armbar with control.

Lesson 4 · Thu, Oct 22

Sweep: hip bump to Kimura

Tip your partner, then hold the arm.

Chest to chest: Make space, then sit up.

Chest to back: Turn in and face your partner.

Neutral guard: Sit up and tip your partner.

Lesson 5 · Tue, Oct 27

Guard pass: X-pass

Open the guard and move past the legs.

Chest to chest: Hold side control as they turn in.

Chest to back: Follow as they turn to their side.

Neutral guard: Open guard and move past the legs.

Lesson 6 · Thu, Oct 29

Guard attack: arm drag

Pull the arm and take the back.

Chest to chest: Make space and find guard.

Chest to back: Keep hooks as your partner turns in.

Neutral guard: Pull the arm and take the back.

Lesson 7 · Tue, Nov 3

Escape: mount

Chest to chest: Bridge, turn, and find guard.

Chest to back: Turn your shoulders to the mat.

Neutral guard: Frame and stand up.

Lesson 8 · Thu, Nov 5

Takedown: hip throws

Turn your hips for o-goshi and uki-goshi.

Chest to chest: Hold close and turn your hips.

Chest to back: Turn to face your partner.

Neutral guard: Step in and turn for the throw.

Lesson 9 · Tue, Nov 10

Submit: triangle

Block the arm and use your legs.

Chest to chest: Frame and turn to guard.

Chest to back: Turn in and face your partner.

Neutral guard: Block the arm and set your legs.

Lesson 10 · Thu, Nov 12

Sweep: old-school roll

Tip your partner and roll through.

Chest to chest: Frame, turn, and find guard.

Chest to back: Turn under and face your partner.

Neutral guard: Pull the leg and roll through.

Lesson 11 · Tue, Nov 17

Guard pass: stack pass

Open the guard with your knee in.

Chest to chest: Hold close as they frame.

Chest to back: Follow their turn to the side.

Neutral guard: Open the guard and move past.

Lesson 12 · Thu, Nov 19

Guard attack: knee shield

Use an arm drag to take the back.

Chest to chest: Make space with your knee shield.

Chest to back: Keep your back hooks as they turn.

Neutral guard: Frame, arm drag, and take the back.

Lesson 13 · Tue, Nov 24

Escape: back control

Chest to chest: Turn in and face your partner.

Chest to back: Clear a hook and turn to the mat.

Lesson 14 · Tue, Dec 1

Chest to chest: Keep a close hold and turn.

Chest to back: Turn to face and clear the hold.

Lesson 15 · Thu, Dec 3

Submit: back choke

Chest to chest: Turn in and find a safe frame.

Chest to back: Keep hooks as your partner clears them.

Lesson 16 · Tue, Dec 8

Chest to chest: Make space and sit up.

Chest to back: Turn in and face your partner.

Neutral guard: Lift with a hook and tip them.

Chest to chest: Hold side control as they turn.

Lesson 18 · Tue, Dec 15

Chest to back: Keep hooks as they turn in.

The learning path

Four steps. One skill at a time.

Get safe

Learn the mat rules. Learn how to tap and fall.

Work to make space and stand up.

Get free

Learn ways to get free from a pin.

Work to find guard or a safe spot.

Gain control

Learn guard, passes, and top spots.

Work to hold a spot as your partner moves.

Put it together

Review the skills in guided rounds.

Find the next step for your training.

This plan does not promise a belt rank. Sandy will set the pace for each student.

Each class

Learn with care.

Play skill games with a partner. Sandy helps each pair move at a good pace.

  • Tap to end a hold. Let go at once.
  • Move with care and follow the class rules.
  • Match your partner's pace.
  • Ask Sandy when you need help.
Your first step

Meet Sandy first.

Take 15 minutes to meet Sandy, see the room, and plan your first adult class.
