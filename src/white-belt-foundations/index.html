The learning path

Get safe. Get free. Gain control. Put it together.

Proposed White Belt Foundations curriculum
StagePractice focusWhat students work toward
Get safeMat rules, tapping, falling, and safe movementStay balanced, make space, and get up
Get freeMount, side-control, and back-control escapesRecover guard or reach a safer position
Gain controlGuard, passing, side control, mount, and back controlHold position while following a partner’s movement
Put it togetherStanding balance, safe entries, review, and guided roundsChoose a steady next step in regular adult class

This is a proposed curriculum, not a belt-promotion promise. Sandy sets the pace and confirms what is appropriate for each student.

Every class

Practice with clear rules.

Classes use guided games, partner feedback, and controlled resistance. Students can tap early, pause a round, or end a round. Partners match pace, and Sandy coaches the room.

  • Tap early and release right away.
  • Use controlled movement and agreed rules.
  • Keep resistance matched to your partner.
  • Ask Sandy when a position or pace feels unclear.
