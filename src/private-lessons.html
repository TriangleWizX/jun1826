Private Coaching FAQ

How much is a private coaching session?

A single Tannersville private lesson is $195. Text Sandy to ask about dates and package options.

What is the best-value option?

The Tannersville Studio Private is our best-value option. It offers direct mat time at our home studio.

How much are Kingston private coaching?

Kingston Add-On Privates start at $275 near scheduled class times. Kingston Travel Privates start at $450. Times are set by request.

Are private coaching good for beginners?

Yes! Private lessons are great for beginners. You receive a calm introduction to basic movements. Skill-based resistance activities begin at the right pace from day one. Sandy sets the pace. You learn safely at your own speed.

Can kids book private coaching?

Yes! Private lessons help kids build confidence and focus. They learn safe movement and class flow.

Can visitors book a private coaching session?

Yes! Visitors can book a private session at our studio. We welcome grapplers staying across the Catskills.

Can I text before booking?

Yes! Text Sandy anytime. Sandy helps you pick the right session or pack.
