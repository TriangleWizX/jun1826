A clear place to start

What can we work on?

Private coaching gives you time to ask questions and work on one skill with Sandy. You can be new to BJJ or bring a class question. Tell him your goal. Then pick a first task.

  • Learn basic BJJ positions and movements.
  • Practice a move or solve a question from class.
  • Ask about a session for a child, teen, or adult.
  • Request a private session while visiting the Catskills.

For a youth session, a parent or guardian must contact Sandy to talk about the student and plan.
