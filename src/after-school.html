A structured hour between school and home

School ends. Energy is high. The evening still needs a repeatable rhythm.

Jiu-Jitsu gives kids a useful problem to enter, a safe partner to work with, and a clear ending before they head home.

The parent-facing outcome is simple: a productive physical challenge at 5:00 PM, coached work on the mat, a clear ending, and home.

Recognize

Students notice the problem, their position, and what their partner is changing.

Protect

They learn to tap, stop, control intensity, and protect themselves and their partner.

Solve

They look for a useful way to improve the situation instead of freezing.

Adapt → Express

They try another answer when needed, then gradually choose solutions without waiting for every answer.

How progress becomes visible

You won’t have to guess whether they’re progressing.

What Happens in Class

1. Movement connected to the day’s problem

Students start moving in a way that makes the class problem understandable.

2. Chest-to-chest control and escapes

They learn how to create space, stay safe, and improve a close position.

3. Chest-to-back control and escapes

They practice protecting position and finding a useful way out.

4. A neutral guard game

Students solve a changing problem with a partner under coach guidance.

5. Coach-controlled resistance rounds

The partner can respond, while Sandy keeps the task safe and appropriately sized.

6. A brief review

Students name what they noticed and what they can try next.
