Calm After-School Cadence

After School Can Become the Best Hour of Their Day

School ends. Energy is high. The evening still needs rhythm.

Jiu-Jitsu gives kids a simple pattern: arrive, breathe, move, listen, practice, partner safely, review, and leave proud.

The goal is calm confidence: clear focus, gentle body control, and a strong sense of belonging in a supportive room.

Habit

Focus

Kids listen and follow clear steps. They practice one skill at a time. They learn to move with calm care.

Growth

Confidence

Kids build genuine pride through coached reps, cooperative partners, and steady progress every week.

Balance

Self-Control

Kids learn gentle body control. They share the mat respectfully and match pace with their training partners.

Family Routine

Better Evenings

Active mat time before dinner helps kids reset. They return home happy, settled, and ready for family meals.

Class Flow

What Happens in Class

Every youth class follows five clear, predictable steps so kids always know what comes next.

Coached Small Groups
Predictable Routine

Structured From Hello to Bow

Kids thrive on clear routines. Every class follows the same five calm steps so young minds settle quickly and train with joyful focus.

Step 01 0–10 min

Arrival and Reset

Students place shoes on the rack, put water on the shelf, take a breath, and shift smoothly out of school mode.

Step 02 10–20 min

Warm-Up

Gentle movement drills and animal rolls loosen hips, backs, and shoulders for safe, supple coordination.

Step 03 · Core Focus 20–35 min

Skill of the Day

Sandy teaches one clear idea at a time in simple steps. Kids practice positioning, leverage, and balance with attentive coaching.

Step 04 35–45 min

Partner Practice

Students pair up with partners of matched size. They practice the skill cooperatively under close coaching with kindness first.

Step 05 45–50 min

Cool Down

We end with a simple review circle so students celebrate what they learned and head home proud.
