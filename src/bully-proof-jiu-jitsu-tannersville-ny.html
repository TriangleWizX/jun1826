Questions

Is this a striking class?

No. Students practice grappling, balance, and safe movement.

What should my child do if there is peer conflict?

Use clear words, move away, and tell a trusted adult.

Does BJJ replace help from school?

No. Work with trusted adults and school staff. Class is practice.

Can I watch?

Yes. Parents can watch the class.
