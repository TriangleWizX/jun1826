The Sensei Sandy Standard

How We Protect the Beginner Experience

Every class operates under four clear safeguards to ensure calm, progressive martial arts learning.

Safety Walkthrough

Every student learns tapping and reset rules before participating in partner drills.

Cooperative Partners

Beginners are paired with experienced, calm practitioners who emphasize controlled technique.

Beginner Lane Pacing

Resistance is calibrated gradually. You focus on positions and escapes at a manageable pace.

Text Coordination

Need to adjust a session? Reschedule your class easily by sending Sandy a direct text message.
