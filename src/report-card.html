What the stages mean
Not Yet Observed
I have yet to see this in live practice. Every skill has its starting moment.
Emerging
You start smoothly with coach guidance or in a calm partner drill.
Developing
You apply the skill more often during regular safe practice.
Contextual
You perform the skill well in familiar spots, and we expand it into new situations.
Reliable
You apply the skill with calm control and balance across changing rounds.

Every milestone marks real progress. Each step shows what you can do on the mats.

Stage updates reflect steady, repeatable practice across multiple partner sessions rather than one try or one easy round. I look for enough signs from class. A new partner helps show what to practice next.

For parents and caregivers: Ask, listen, and cheer your child on. Let the coach teach the moves. Ask, “What felt easy today?” or “What did you try?” For a young child, say: “I saw you try. Your coach can help you try again.”

The 4-Step Progress & Enrollment Flow
1. Day 4 Milestone:

Whether visiting on The Mountain Tap Card or starting your first term, Class 4 gives you observable proof of balance, base, and safe partner practice. Build your baseline here, then confirm your 12-week Core Culture term.

2. Weekly Rhythm:

Log mat reflections on the Daily Note and track habits on the Weekly Check-In. Skills develop steadily from Emerging to Developing.

3. Mid-Cycle Check:

At Week 6 (Class 18), review your 9 skill areas and adjust your focus for the second half of the term.

4. Term Completion & Annual Track:

At Week 12 (Class 36), complete your Core Culture Review. Ascend to the 12-month Annual Track for continuous growth and locked tuition.
