The Rural Mat Experience

In large metropolitan gyms, classes can feel crowded and impersonal. Training in the Catskills offers a refreshing alternative. Class sizes are intentionally kept comfortable so every student receives direct coaching, feedback, and encouragement from Sensei Sandy.

Our students include local tradespeople, business owners, healthcare workers, teachers, weekend cabin visitors, and whole families training together. This diverse blend creates a respectful, ego-free room where safety and personal progress come first.

First Visit Questions

Do I need prior martial arts experience?

No experience is required. Your first class is a coached introduction designed specifically for complete beginners.

Can parents watch youth classes?

Yes. Parents are always welcome to sit comfortably in our viewing area and watch their children train.

What should I wear for my first class?

Wear clean, comfortable athletic clothing such as a t-shirt and sweatpants or shorts. We provide everything else you need.
