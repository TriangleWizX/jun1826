Saturday morning Adult No-Gi is our most popular class for visitors. See the full week on /schedule. Text us to save your spot. Classes are capped at 12 students.

Bring the family

Kids ages 5 to 17 train in calm, coached classes. A parent stays to watch. The first visit is free.

Questions (FAQ)

I train in the city. Can I drop in?

Yes. You are welcome here. Text us before you come. We match you with a good partner.

I have never tried BJJ. Can I come?

Yes. Your first visit is free. A coach guides you. You train with a calm partner. You move at your pace.

Can I come just once?

Yes. Book one class. Come back any time.

Do you have a gi I can borrow?

Yes. Borrow a gi for your visit at no charge. We hold a deposit until it is returned. We wash every gi after use. Ask us about sizes when you text to book.
