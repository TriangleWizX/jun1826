RANDOMIZED CONTROLLED TRIAL · RELATED MARTIAL ART

Youth Martial Arts and Attention

Population
240 children ages 7–11.
Intervention
Taekwondo compared with active physical education over 11 weeks and 16.5 total hours.
Reported outcome
Executive-attention effect approximately d=.28; conduct-problem IRR .79. Not every measured outcome improved.
Supports
Cautious context for structured martial-arts training.
Does not support
Calling Taekwondo BJJ evidence or guaranteeing an individual outcome.
