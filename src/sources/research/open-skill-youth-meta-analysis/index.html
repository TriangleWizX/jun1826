SYSTEMATIC REVIEW · RELATED SPORT RESEARCH

Open-Skill Youth Exercise Meta-Analysis

Publication date
Published online 2025-02-04; volume 18 (2024).
Population
1,298 children and adolescents ages 5–16 across 17 studies, including 11 randomized trials.
Intervention
Open-skill exercise in changing environments.
Reported outcome
Selected pooled executive-function outcomes; heterogeneity was high.
Supports
Changing-environment decision demands as a broader training-category mechanism.
Does not support
An SSBJJ-specific effect, superiority to soccer, or an individual guarantee.

2026-10-08 · review again 2027-10-08
