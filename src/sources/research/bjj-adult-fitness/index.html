SMALL INTERVENTION · DIRECT BJJ

BJJ Adult Fitness Study

Population
16 previously untrained adults: 8 men and 8 women.
Intervention
BJJ training twice weekly for one hour over six weeks.
Reported outcome
Significant changes in VO₂max, time to exhaustion, lower-body strength, and several anaerobic measures.
Supports
Describing regular BJJ practice as physical training.
Does not support
BJJ superiority to Pilates, body-composition guarantees, or individual fitness guarantees.
Verified
