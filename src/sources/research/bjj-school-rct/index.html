RANDOMIZED CONTROLLED TRIAL · DIRECT BJJ

School-Based BJJ Randomized Trial

Population
88 sixth-grade boys enrolled; 80 analyzed.
Intervention
Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu compared with regular physical education, 12 weeks, two classes per week.
Reported outcome
Teacher-rated emotional symptoms p=.007; hyperactivity/inattention p<.001; total difficulties p=.002; externalizing p=.001, η²=.07.
Supports
One specific school-program result on selected behavioral measures.
Does not support
ADHD treatment, all children, SSBJJ-specific effectiveness, or individual outcome guarantees.
Verified
