Fall registration is closed

The official Fall Athletics 2026 registration page says registration is closed. The school athletics page currently says it has no programs published. The public pages do not show a soccer game calendar, so contact the school to confirm team dates or the next registration period.

Student clearance

What the school asks families to provide

  • Complete the FamilyID form, including health information, school policies, and required agreements.
  • Provide a current physical. The school says a physical expires 12 months after the exam. Families can upload it with the form or bring a copy to the Health Office.

The registration page says students need these items before they can play or try out. Check with the school for current requirements.

Contact Hunter-Tannersville Athletics

Ask about soccer team schedules, future registration, and current clearance steps.
