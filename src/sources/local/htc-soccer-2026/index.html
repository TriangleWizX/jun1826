How This Source Is Used

This record is maintained strictly to confirm the local availability of fall youth soccer through the Hunter-Tannersville school system. It is referenced in cross-training discussions to recognize that many Catskills student-athletes participate in seasonal team sports. It does not support any claim that Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is superior, safer, or more cognitively demanding than soccer.
