Children’s ski programs

Choose by age and skill

Mighty Mights · ages 5–6

Children need to ride a chairlift, make and stop a wedge, and turn with control. Sessions are Saturday and Sunday, 9 a.m.–noon.

All Mountain Devo · ages 7–14

For intermediate and advanced skiers who can ski parallel on blue terrain. Sessions are Saturday and Sunday, 9 a.m.–2 p.m. The lunch break is unsupervised and lunch is not included.

Competition Devo · ages 7–14

For intermediate and advanced skiers. Sessions are Saturday and Sunday, 8:30 a.m.–2:30 p.m.

Teen and snowboard programs

More development options

Teen Pro · ages 12–17

For advanced skiers and riders who are comfortable on black-diamond terrain. Sessions are Saturday and Sunday, 9 a.m.–noon.

Snowboard Development · ages 7–12

For beginner, intermediate, and advanced riders who can link turns on both edges. Sessions are Saturday and Sunday, 9 a.m.–noon.

Dates and availability · checked October 21, 2026

Check the program before you register

The listed season runs December 12, 2026, through March 14, 2027. The official page gives no-program dates of December 26–27, January 16–17, and February 13–14 for Mighty Mights, All Mountain Devo, Teen Pro, and Snowboard Development.

Registration opened September 1. Returning participants received an early registration period. The resort says remaining places are first come, first served and a “Sold Out” label means a program is at capacity. Check the official page for current availability, requirements, and registration.
