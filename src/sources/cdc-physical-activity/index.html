CDC Adult Guide

The CDC shares weekly activity goals for adults. Read the CDC guide.

Week goals

Do 150 minutes of moderate activity each week. Or do 75 minutes of vigorous activity. Aim for strength work on two or more days.

The CDC says this can help. Fit the plan to your own health.

Jiu-jitsu class

A jiu-jitsu class has drills with a partner. Class time may not meet all CDC goals. Each class can ask for more or less work.
