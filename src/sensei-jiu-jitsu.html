How Sandy teaches

Sandy leads each class with a clear plan. Students learn one task at a time. The coach shows a move, then gives people a chance to try it. A partner adds a simple task to solve. Both people take turns. The coach can change the task as the group learns. Skill-based resistance grows at a pace that fits each student. This lets people learn by moving, thinking, and trying again.

Kids, teens, and adults have their own class paths. Each group has goals that fit its stage. New students can start with simple steps. People can ask a question and get help from Sandy. Class is a coached learning time. The team works on skill, balance, and care for a partner.

Plan your first class

Your first visit starts with a talk. Tell Sandy what you hope to learn. You can see the room and learn the class rules. Sandy can walk you through the safety steps. You can ask what to wear and what to bring. Then you can plan a class time that fits the group and your goals.

At class, the coach shows the task. You work with a partner who fits the drill. The group moves at a calm pace. You learn one step and try it. Your partner gives you a chance to solve the task. You can pause, tap, or ask the coach to show the move again. Sandy can help set the right pace from day one.

Coach and lineage

Sandy trained under Josh Griffiths at Clockwork Jiu-Jitsu. Coach lineage links a student to the teachers who came before. Sandy shares those roots through his work at Sensei Sandy BJJ in Tannersville, NY. You can read our BJJ sensei lineage tree, the Clockwork instructor page, or the coach bio.

Each class builds on shared mat time. Students learn to work with a partner and listen to the coach. The aim is to gain skill, one class at a time. You can ask Sandy how the class path fits your needs. He can help you plan the next step.
