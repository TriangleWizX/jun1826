What a Police BJJ Report Says

This page sums up one police report and one state class guide. It shows what each source says and what it leaves out.

What the Report Says

  • Marietta shared results from its 2020 police BJJ program.
  • Read its case report for the figures and study limits.
  • The report names a state guide for one BJJ class level.

What It Does Not Prove

  • It does not show that BJJ caused these changes.
  • It does not show what another police team will see.
  • It does not show that training alone meets police rules.
  • It does not replace state or police use-of-force training.
  • It does not show how sport BJJ works with belts, weapons, or more than one person.
