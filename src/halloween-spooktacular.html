Meet Sasquatch on Main Street

Celebrate the annual Halloween Spooktacular with your mountain neighbors! Trick or treat through the Village, see haunted cars, and visit Sensei Sandy BJJ for warm Sasquatch cider, hot coffee, and photos with Sasquatch.

SPTO Trick or Truck & Village Fun

What is happening along Main Street

The Student Parent Teacher Organization (SPTO) and Village neighbors host this annual tradition for local kids and families from Tannersville, Hunter, Windham, and Haines Falls.

Haunted Car Trick or Truck

Creative, spooky decorated cars line Main Street. Kids can trick or treat safely from trunk to trunk right in the heart of the Village.

Costume Parade & Contests

Show off your family costumes in the costume parade! Enjoy the annual pie eating contest, festive Jack o’lantern display, and crafts for the kids.

Meet Sasquatch at SSBJJ

Head up to Studios on Main on the 2nd Floor. Meet our mountain Sasquatch in person and take friendly family photos together on your own phones!

Sasquatch Cider & Coffee

Warm up with complimentary hot spiced apple cider for kids and fresh hot coffee for parents in our clean family room.

Warm Room & First-Visit Plan

Take a restful break from the cold autumn air. Step inside our clean, calm studio, see the mats, and pick up your Free First-Visit Plan.

Catskills Community Warmth

Connect with local families, teachers, and neighbors. Sensei Sandy and the academy team are here to celebrate our mountain village.

Come say hello on Main Street!

Admission is completely free for all community members. Visit us at 6045 Main Street between 5:00 PM and 8:00 PM while trick-or-treating.

Location & Safety

How to find us and plan your visit

Studio Address

Studios on Main (2nd Floor)
6045 Main Street, Tannersville, NY 12485
Located directly on the Main Street parade route.

Parking Advice

Street parking is available on side streets and nearby municipal lots. Portions of Main Street will feature pedestrian-safe trunk setups.

What to Bring

Costumes, candy bags, and your camera for photos with Sasquatch! We supply the cider, coffee, warmth, and friendly smiles.

Family Privacy & Stairs

Photos are opt-in. Parents use their own phones. Our 2nd-floor stairway is well-lit with handrails. Please leave outdoor boots at our entry cubbies.

New to Jiu-Jitsu?

Curious about martial arts for your family?

Sensei Sandy BJJ offers calm, structured small-group classes for kids, teens, and adults. Every newcomer starts with a coached, private learning experience.
