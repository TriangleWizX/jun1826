After a Core Culture term

Core Culture Review

A first term has a defined ending. Before anyone discusses another product, Sandy reviews what became more reliable, what problem should come next, and whether the weekly rhythm still fits.

Observe → Review → Adjust → Choose. Continuing is one possible outcome. Pausing or stopping is also a valid outcome; a successful term does not require another purchase.

1 · Training first

What did Sandy observe?

Use the existing Progress Report Card rather than creating a second assessment.

  • What is working?
  • What is the next problem?
  • What is beginning to become yours?
  • Which Development Map states have enough live evidence?

2 · Schedule second

Does the rhythm fit?

  • Which home classes were easiest to maintain?
  • Were missed classes random or tied to a recurring conflict?
  • Did partner placement work well?
  • Would different weekly days or a different access model help?

3 · Choice last

What arrangement fits now?

Here is what Sandy is seeing. If you want to keep going, now you can decide what schedule and enrollment structure make sense.

Choose the next useful arrangement

Different structures solve different problems.

Renew Core Culture

Choose another defined 12-week term when a recurring schedule and continued progress feedback remain useful. Confirm the next home classes during the review. The first-term gi does not repeat, and the Training Fit Guarantee does not reset.

Annual Track

Choose this when the weekly routine is already working and you expect to continue for the year. It is a purchasing structure, not a rank or commitment reward.

Elite Concierge

Eligible adults can choose additional logistical and private-coaching support. It remains continuation-only, subject to application, schedule review, capacity, and placement.

Flexible Access

When recurring home-class reservations no longer fit, seasonal or irregular-schedule students can ask about open-access class packs where appropriate.

Pause

If school, work, health, transport, or family needs make continuing unreasonable right now, discuss a pause and the next practical step.

Stop

Stopping is allowed. The completed term already delivered its coaching value; no explanation or replacement purchase is required.

Student voice matters

For children and teens, Sandy’s observations, the parent’s practical view, and the student’s own willingness should all inform the conversation. A paid term does not by itself decide that a student should continue.

For adults, the conversation can stay simple: What is working? What are you trying to solve? Does three-times-weekly training still fit? Do you want another 12 weeks, a longer plan, more support, something flexible, a pause, or a stop?
