Your First Visit,Then Your First Class

Your first-visit checklist depends on who is starting. Adults begin with a short First Visit; kids and teens arrive early for safety and may train that day.

Not booked yet? Reserve Free Intro. Already booked? Follow the arrival and clothing instructions in your confirmation, then use the checklist below.

Step 1

Adult First Visit

15 minutes. No workout required.

Adults: wear normal clothes No workout during this appointment.
Bring your calendar Sandy will recommend and schedule the coached first class afterward. Youth and teen visitors use the combined-visit checklist below.
Bring your questions Parents and visitors can ask about fit, comfort, and schedule.
Arrive 5 minutes early Give yourself a quiet couple of minutes before we start.

What to wear

Check the class format

No-GiWear a clean fitted athletic shirt or rashguard with pocketless shorts, compression shorts or leggings. No exposed zippers, buttons or hardware; remove jewelry. Students do not wear belts during live No-Gi practice.
GiWear a clean jiu-jitsu gi and belt. First-time 12-week program enrollment includes an uniform when eligible. Ask Sandy about a loaner for a scheduled first class when inventory permits.
BeginnerYou do not need special equipment for a No-Gi first class. Clean athletic clothing is enough. Sandy will tell you whether the scheduled class is Gi or No-Gi before you arrive.

Step 2

First Class

Experience a real Sensei Sandy BJJ class.

Wear clean athletic clothes Follow the class-specific Gi or No-Gi instructions. Remove jewelry.
Bring water A bottle is enough.
Complete the waiver Complete it before training or on-site as Sandy confirms; it is not a workout requirement for first visit.
Arrive 10 minutes early Meet your coach and training partners before small-group class.
Finish the waiver Text Sandy

Your First Visit

A calm sequence from door to next step.

Adult First Visits are not training. Youth First Visits may continue directly into class. Sandy helps you understand the room, your goals, and the class that fits.

  1. 01

    Meet Sandy

    Arrive, settle in, and ask the questions that matter to your goals and schedule.

  2. 02

    See the room

    Sandy shows you where class happens and explains the basic safety expectations in plain language.

  3. 03

    Map the starting plan

    Discuss goals, weekly rhythm, comfort, and any training limitation that should shape the first class.

  4. 04

    Choose the next step

    Sandy recommends and schedules the coached first class afterward at your pace.
