Start Calm & Safe

Welcome to Jiu-Jitsu for Everyday People. If you are reading this, you might be curious about training but unsure if it is safe, welcoming, or the right fit for you.

This short course is designed to answer those questions. Your first class is a coached learning experience, not a test. We prioritize a respectful room and cooperative pacing.

What you will learn in this course:

  • How our cooperative, coached movement keeps you safe from day one.
  • What to expect when you first walk into the academy.
  • How pure mechanical leverage works for smaller or older students.
  • Exactly what to bring and how to prepare for your first intro class.

The Room Walkthrough

Video: Academy Walkthrough

Walking into a martial arts academy for the first time can feel intimidating. We have built our space in Tannersville specifically to feel calm, structured, and organized.

Your First 15 Minutes

  • The Greeting: You will be welcomed at the door and shown where to place your shoes and bags.
  • The Tour: We will walk you around the mats, show you the changing areas, and explain the class format.
  • The Warm-up: Classes start with simple, structured movement drills. You follow along at your own pace.

You are never thrown into the deep end. Everything is guided step-by-step.

Pure Leverage: How technique works without strength.

Pure Leverage

Video: Mechanical Leverage

You do not need to be strong, fast, or flexible to start jiu-jitsu. The art is built entirely on pure mechanical leverage.

In our beginner classes, we focus on cooperative partner drills. You will learn how to use your body structure, frames, and balance to move effectively, even against someone larger.

The Focus for Beginners

  • Frames: Using your bones to support weight instead of your muscles.
  • Base: Keeping quiet confidence and balance.
  • Cooperation: Working with your partner to learn the movement smoothly.

First Class Checklist

You are ready. Here is everything you need to know before you arrive for your first free intro class.

What to Bring

  • A comfortable t-shirt or rash guard.
  • Athletic shorts (without zippers or metal) or leggings.
  • A water bottle.
  • Slip-on sandals or flip-flops for walking off the mat.

Your first class is completely free, and we have loaner gear if you need it. We look forward to meeting you.
