Kids Summer Academy in Tannersville

A six-week, no-gi BJJ program for kids ages 6 to 12.

Future dates and prices are not set.

The 2026 plan

This plan was for 2026. Future programs may differ.

  • Dates: July 14 to August 20, 2026.
  • Class days: Tuesday and Thursday.
  • Class time: 9:00 to 11:30 a.m.
  • Age group: Ages 6 to 12.
  • Group size: Up to {{ temporalPrograms.summerAcademy.capacity }} kids.
  • Training: No-gi BJJ. Beginners were welcome.

Twelve sessions. Two and a half hours each. Thirty hours total.

It was a class program. It was not all-day care.

Who could take part?

  • Kids ages 6 to 12.
  • New BJJ students were welcome.
  • Kids could try games and partner drills.
  • Parents could watch from the seating area.

How a morning was set up

  1. 9:00: Arrive and get set.
  2. 9:10: Play a movement game.
  3. 9:30: Learn BJJ.
  4. 10:05: Take a snack break.
  5. 10:20: Try a partner game with skill-based resistance.
  6. 10:55: Work as a team.
  7. 11:20: Cool down and add to the Progress Passport.
  8. 11:30: Meet the adult set for pickup.

Parents could watch from the seating area.

Six week themes

These were the 2026 themes. A future program may change.

  • Week 1: Ready body and class rules.
  • Week 2: Balance and takedowns.
  • Week 3: Escapes and clear boundaries.
  • Week 4: Guard and problem solving.
  • Week 5: Top control and team roles.
  • Week 6: Safe work around submissions.

Kids practiced movement, safe falls, partner choices, and team tasks.

They could use words to set a boundary or ask for help.

Skills were taught at a pace that fit the child and the task.

What the full program included

  • Parent orientation.
  • Coached BJJ sessions and movement games.
  • Safe-fall practice and partner games.
  • Team challenges and youth leadership tasks.
  • A Summer Progress Passport.
  • A shirt for kids who finished the full program.
  • A family showcase and written progress review.
  • Loaner gi access.
  • Two make-up credits for regular youth classes.

The program was led by Sensei Sandy and an assistant coach.
