Try. Learn. Try Again.

Why We Teach BJJ With Games

In BJJ, your partner can move. You watch, think, and act. Then the game can change. At Sensei Sandy BJJ, we use safe games to help you learn. We make the game fit you.

See how class works

How we learn

Try a goal. Play. Make a change.

Some studies look at BJJ. Some look at other sports. They can help us plan class. But they cannot tell us what each child will gain.

  1. Start with a goal. You may need to get free from a hold or stay on top.
  2. Play the game. Your partner can move. Find a way to meet your goal.
  3. Make a change. Sandy can change the game. Then you can try again.
  4. See what sticks. Can you do it with a new partner, new rules, or less help?
