About these studies

One study is one view.

In Jiu-Jitsu, your partner moves. You choose what to do next. Some studies look at this sport. They cannot tell us what each person will gain.

Sandy watches each person learn and work with a partner. The Report Card tracks class skills. It does not test the brain.

Study rules

  • Say what the group did.
  • Say when a study is about a different sport.
  • Keep the limits clear.
  • These studies cannot rank sports.

What each study found

BJJ in one school study

BJJ and school behavior

Who
88 boys in grade six; 80 finished.
What they did
BJJ or PE for 12 weeks, twice each week. Groups were picked at random.
What teachers saw
Some scores changed more in the BJJ group.

Shows: what teachers saw in this study.

Limit: this cannot show that BJJ helps each child focus.

Small BJJ study

BJJ as exercise

Who
16 adults new to training.
What they did
BJJ for one hour, twice a week for six weeks.
What it found
Some fitness scores changed in this small group.

Limit: this cannot show that BJJ is better than Pilates or change each person’s fitness.

BJJ and MMA over five months

Self-control and kind acts

Who
113 people who chose BJJ or MMA.
What the study found
Both groups said they had more self-control and did more kind acts. Aggression scores fell in BJJ and rose in MMA.

Limit: people chose their sport. The study cannot show what caused change.
