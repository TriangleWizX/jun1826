SANDY JOSÉ NÚÑEZ · BLACK BELT COACH · TANNERSVILLE, NY

I'm Sandy Nunez. I coach jiu-jitsu in Tannersville.

I started out heavy and out of shape. Walking into my first martial arts gym felt intimidating. Today, I coach kids, teens, and adults on Main Street with a calm, welcoming approach. Sandy teaches 17 classes a week between Tannersville and Kingston. Active competitors and lifelong martial artists train alongside first-time students in an environment centered on safe, cooperative learning, clear mechanical leverage, and beginner-first onboarding.

Sandy guides your first class at your pace. He sets the task and matches your partner. You can ask for help or take a breather whenever you like.

My Story

I Was Once a Hesitant Beginner Too

I was heavy and out of shape before jiu-jitsu. I felt shy and had trouble making eye contact during conversations.

Walking into a martial arts school felt intimidating. I even called academies ahead of time just to hear a coach answer before I found the courage to walk inside.

In 2010, I took my first class at Lionheart Fitness in State College, Pennsylvania. Later that year, I moved to Manhattan and trained in the gi at Ronin Athletics. By 2011, I committed to Clockwork Jiu-Jitsu in Manhattan, training two to three times a day.

I worked as a bartender in New York City to support my mat time. I won cocktail competitions and learned the true art of hospitality: greeting guests warmly, listening, and making everyone feel at home.

Competition taught me an enduring lesson: showing up each week matters far more than natural athletic gifts. Jiu-jitsu gave me health, balance, and quiet confidence. It became a lifelong anchor.

Lineage & Mentorship

Mountain Roots

Why I Built Sensei Sandy BJJ in Tannersville

My wife grew up in Prattsville. We moved upstate after 2020 to build our family life in the Catskills. Today, we live in East Jewett year-round.

Before opening our permanent school, I taught wherever the mats fit. We practiced on garage mats, on lawns, and in community rooms. In August 2025, our studio opened at 6045 Main Street in Tannersville.

The Reverse Classroom

I love coaching on the mountain because I learn from my students in a reverse classroom style. Small kids teach me balance, timing, and finesse. Adult students bring focus and life perspective. Every class is a shared learning experience where coach and student rise together.

My own jiu-jitsu has grown sharper since moving to the Catskills.

Coaching Philosophy

Mutual Welfare & Shared Progress (Jita Kyoei)

My coaching energy is chill, firm, and fair. I protect the safety of the room with high standards, and I invite students to discover skills through clear parameters. In our room, every student is coached, not tested.

Our studio embraces the classic principle of Jita Kyoei, which means mutual prosperity:

Partners Need Each Other

Martial arts requires cooperation. Your partner helps you grow, and you help your partner grow. We succeed as a team.

Safety and Respect First

We value partner well-being above winning drills. Cooperative pacing builds confidence and long-term skill.

Social Harmony

The patience and problem-solving skills learned on the mat help kids and adults solve challenges in everyday life.

Efficient Energy (Seiryoku Zenko)

We use mechanical leverage, balance, and frames rather than brute muscle. Smaller students redirect force with ease.
