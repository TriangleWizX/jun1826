How the intro works

  1. We learn about your child. Sandy asks about your child’s goals, needs, and days that fit.
  2. Your child joins the class. Sandy picks a partner. Your child gets one task, one goal, and one rule.
  3. We review the experience. Sandy shares what your child tried. Ask your questions. Plan the next step.
