Core Culture Schedule & Rescheduling

Each standard enrollment includes three planned classes per week (up to 36 scheduled classes over 12 weeks). Students select three recurring home-class reservations to keep training consistent and manage room capacity.

If your availability changes during a week, we offer open-seat rescheduling by text. Rescheduling depends on space and partner fit in that session. Text Sandy directly to check open seats.
