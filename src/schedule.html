A Year of Growth

Plan Your Three Classes Each Week

Pick three days each week to train. Three classes a week is the sweet spot. It helps you learn moves and remember them. It gives your body time to rest between classes. Over one full year, three classes a week adds up to big gains. You build calm focus. You gain real balance and true skill.

Step 1

Pick Your Three Days

Look at your week. Pick three days that fit your life. You can keep the same days each week. This builds a strong habit that lasts all year long. A steady routine makes training fun and easy to keep.

Step 2

Choose Your Training Path

Pick the path that fits you. Youth train at 5:00 PM. Adults train at 6:00 PM or Saturday morning. Morning private lessons are open by request. Mix Gi and No-Gi to feel the fun of both styles. You can start this week.

Step 3

Easy Changes by Text

Life happens. If you need to miss a class, text Sandy. You can make up your class on another open day that week. We keep it simple and calm. You stay on track with your yearly goal.

Youth and Teens · Ages 5 to 17

5:00–6:00 PM

Youth classes meet at 5:00 PM Monday through Friday. Pick any three days. Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday are No-Gi. Tuesday and Friday are Gi. Here are two easy ways to set your week:

Option A: Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. You get two No-Gi classes and one Gi class. You have a rest day between each class.

Option B: Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday. You get two Gi classes and one No-Gi class. This path gives you great balance.

Adults · Ages 18 and Up

6:00 PM & Sat 10:30 AM

Adults train at 6:00 PM Monday through Friday. Saturday class is at 10:30 AM. Weekday nights are in Gi. Saturday is No-Gi. Here are two paths to try. Both paths help you grow.

Path A: Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Train Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 6:00 PM. Wear your Gi each night. Rest on the days between. This gives you a strong rhythm.

Path B: Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday. Train at 6:00 PM on Tuesday and Thursday. Add Saturday at 10:30 AM. You get two Gi classes and one No-Gi class. It fits your work week well. Come train with us.

Morning Private Lessons by Request

Weekday morning private lessons are open by request. Text Sandy to set a time. You get one-on-one help. It is a calm way to start. It fits your work week.
