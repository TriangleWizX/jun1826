Your First Lesson

Here is a sample lesson plan. Ask Sandy what fits your goals:

  1. Share your goals: Tell Sandy what you want to learn.
  2. Warm up: Move at a pace that fits your needs.
  3. Safety first: Learn to tap, release holds, and move with care.
  4. Ground positions: Learn to use your balance and body weight.
  5. One escape: Work on a way out of a hold.
  6. One hold: Learn to keep position with a partner.
  7. Partner drills: Ask your coach which drills fit your level.

Ask which skills to try next. If you want another lesson, talk about a plan with Sandy.

FAQ

Do I need to be in shape?

Your coach helps you choose a pace. Share your current needs and goals before class.

Is it safe for first-timers?

Start with a safety walk-through. Ask Sandy how he guides the pace. Have a health limit? Ask a health care pro before you train.

Can I do this as a couple or with a friend?

Ask Sandy about lessons for each person. Ask him to set the class type and time before you book.

What if I am nervous?

You can ask questions before you start. Tour the room and learn the safety rules. Share the pace that feels right for you.
