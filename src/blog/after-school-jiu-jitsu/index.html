What kids learn

Kids practice balance and ways to move. They learn to tap to end a hold. They release a hold when a partner taps.

Sandy guides their drills and sets the pace. Kids learn to listen and care for their partners. They can ask for help as they learn.

A first class at their pace

Your child can tour the room and meet Sandy. Ask about the safety rules and the class plan. Share what helps your child feel at ease.

Sandy guides partner practice from day one. Ask him how he sets tasks for new kids. A clear first step can help you choose a class.
