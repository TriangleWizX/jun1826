Care for the tree as well as the fruit

Think of a win as fruit on a tree. The roots are practice, timing, and patience. Care for those roots each week. A round can teach you a skill even when you tap.

A good guillotine is one skill. You may also need guard passes or escapes. Ask which part of your game needs work. Choose a drill for that task.

Use rounds to learn

Pick one clear goal with your coach. Tell your partner the task. Use a pace that lets you learn.

If you use the same strong move each round, try a new task. Work on a pass, guard, or escape. Notice where you get stuck. Ask the coach about that step. Learn the class words
