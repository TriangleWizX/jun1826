Critical Safety Check

Use padded mats and train with a coach. Start from sitting before you try a higher fall. Have a past concussion or neck issue? Ask a health care pro before you drill.

A coach can check your head, arms, and landing angle. Learn the drill on mats before adding height or speed.

Four Rules to Check With Your Coach

  • 1. Tuck your chin: Ask your coach how to keep your head off the mat.
  • 2. Round your back: Start low and practice the roll with your coach.
  • 3. Arm position: Ask your coach how your palm and forearm meet the mat.
  • 4. Keep arms soft: Let your coach check the arm angle before you land.
"The ground is hard. You must be soft."

1) Backward Breakfall (Ushiro Ukemi)

Scenario: You get tripped, shoved, or swept backward.

Level 1: From Sitting (The Foundation)
  1. Sit on the mat with arms extended forward.
  2. Tuck your chin firmly to your chest. Look at your belt knot.
  3. Roll backward smoothly along the curve of your spine.
  4. Right before your shoulder blades touch, SLAP the mat at a 45-degree angle.
  5. Keep your feet clear of your head. Let your coach check the range.
Level 2: From a Squat
  1. Get into a deep squat (catcher's stance).
  2. Roll back like a rocking chair.
  3. Explode into the slap as you land.
  4. Verify: Is your head off the mat? Good.

Ask your coach to check your chin and arm position. Keep your head off the mat.

2) Side Breakfall (Yoko Ukemi)

This drill covers landing on your side. Practice on mats with a coach.

Step-by-Step Mechanics
  1. Identify the side you are falling toward.
  2. Tuck your chin.
  3. Land on the side of your back as your coach guides you.
  4. Ask your coach to set the leg position for your side fall.
  5. Slap the mat firmly with the falling-side arm.

Ask your coach to check your chin and arm position. Keep your head off the mat.

3) Forward Breakfall (Mae Ukemi)

This drill covers a forward landing. Ask your coach to show the start position and how your arms meet the mat.

The "Cat Catch"

  1. Start from your knees (or standing stance for advanced).
  2. Start low and follow your coach's landing cues.
  3. Let your coach check your head position.
  4. Land on your Forearms and Palms simultaneously.
  5. Breathe out as you land.

Ask your coach to check how you bend your arms and spread contact on the mat.

Sample Mat Warm-Up With a Coach

Use these as sample class drills with a coach. Choose the height and reps that fit your needs.

  • 10x Rocking Chairs: Hug knees, rock back and forth (massages spine).
  • 5x Backward Slaps: From sitting. Focus on timing.
  • 5x Side Falls: Alternating left and right from knees.
  • 5x Forward Drops: From knees. Focus on the exhale.
