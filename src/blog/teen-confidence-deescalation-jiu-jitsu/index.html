A new friend at a meet

Sandy told us about a 12-year-old student. The child felt social fear. At a Jiu-Jitsu meet, he met a stranger his own age.

The other child trained at a different gym. They met on the practice mat. They made friends face to face, eye to eye.

This is the account Sandy shared. We leave both children's names out.

A challenge from another school

A student from another school challenged one of Sandy's students. His student used his skills to defend himself. He kept the other child safe from harm.

Sandy shared that outcome. Each real event is different. A teen facing a threat needs adult support.

Support your teen

Ask how your teen feels at school. Let them tell you what happened. Help them choose an adult they can trust.
