Why skill-based training can hold your attention

For adults in Tannersville, Hunter, Windham, and the wider Catskills, BJJ offers a different kind of workout. Safety-First FAQ for Catskills Gym Seekers

I'm looking for a gym with weights - is this for me?

Yes. We use bodyweight mastery, mechanics, and resistance to build functional strength that carries into daily life. You do more than lift; you learn to move with purpose.

I have zero coordination or I am old.

We designed the Beginner class for complete newcomers. We start slow, scale every drill, and prioritize longevity so you can grow safely at any age. Browse the beginner BJJ glossary.

Is it clean?

Absolutely. We sanitize mats after every small-group class, disinfect common-touch surfaces daily, and enforce clean-gear and footwear rules to keep the space fresh and safe.
