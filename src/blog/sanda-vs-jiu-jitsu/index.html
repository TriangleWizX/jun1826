Rules create the style (with real numbers)

Sanda competition basics (IWUF)

The cited Sanda rules use three rounds of two minutes. Rest lasts one minute. Winning two rounds wins the bout. A knockout can end it too. [1]

The cited platform is 0.8 meters high. It measures eight by eight meters. The surrounding pads are 0.3 meters high and two meters wide. [1]

The cited gear includes a headguard, chest guard, and gloves. It also lists a mouthguard and groin guard. [1]

The IWUF overview lists eleven men’s weight groups and seven women’s groups. [1]

Jiu Jitsu competition basics (IBJJF rules example)

The cited IBJJF adult matches last five minutes at white. Blue lasts six, purple seven, and brown eight. Black lasts ten minutes. Check current rules before a contest. [6] Learn the class words

  • Takedown: 2 points [6]
  • Sweep: 2 points [6]
  • Knee on belly: 2 points [6]
  • Guard pass: 3 points [6]
  • Mount and back mount: 4 points [6]
  • Back control: four points when the required holds are met. [6]

The 2021 update allowed heel hooks and knee reaping in adult no-gi. It applies to brown and black belts. The start date was January 1, 2021. Check current rules before you compete. [7]

What a new student may feel

Starting Sanda

  • Learn how to read distance.
  • Understand the risk from strikes.
  • Practice entries with head protection in mind.

Coached drills let you study timing. Live tasks add pressure. Ask which tasks fit your experience and health.

Starting BJJ

  • Staying calm in tight contact
  • Learn frames and escapes.
  • Learn to work through pressure in small steps.

A BJJ coach can set limits on partner tasks. Holds and contact still carry risk. Ask how the class sets its pace.

A 2025 study used survey data from February 15 to March 17, 2024. It reported 5.5 cases of harm per 1,000 training hours. It reported 55.9 per 1,000 contest matches. Of reported cases, 89% happened in training. Of those, 79% happened during sparring. These units and groups differ. [8] Learn the class words

Self-defense skills and limits

  • Sanda teaches strike distance, movement, and standing throws. [1]
  • BJJ teaches close holds, pin escapes, and ground control. Start with stance and space in the linked guide. seated guard basics [6]

MMA needs practice moving between ranges. Start with basic skills and clear limits.

Self-defense also needs awareness, calm words, and ways to leave. Sport skills cannot guarantee safety.

Reasons to study Sanda

  1. You want to learn strikes and safe distance.
  2. You like short rounds and resets. The cited rules use three rounds of two minutes. [1]
  3. You want to study throws with strikes. [1]

Reasons to study BJJ

  1. You want to study close contact at a coached pace.
  2. You want to learn holds and ground escapes. [6]
  3. You want to study positions with clear sport points. The cited adult black-belt match lasts ten minutes. [6]
