What the state law says

The law covers certain before and after school care sites. Care must be on a set plan and less than twenty-four hours a day. The site must be inside the school district. The usual written request date is April 1 for the next school year. The law also covers late requests with a sound reason. Ask the district how it applies to your case. State law
