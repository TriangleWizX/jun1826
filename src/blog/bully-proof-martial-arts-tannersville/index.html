What a Bully-Proof Class Teaches

A bully-proof class teaches good skills:

  • Kids learn to set clear boundaries.
  • They practice speaking in a firm voice.
  • They learn to ask adults for help.
  • They learn good balance.
  • They learn body control.

Sensei Sandy BJJ in Tannersville

Classes by Age Group

Kids classes welcome children ages five to nine. The class uses fun games and movement drills. Coaches guide each partner pair. Kids practice staying calm. They learn to hold their personal space.

Youth and teen classes welcome students ages ten to seventeen. They learn practical self-defense. They learn how to escape a hold. Then they stand back up tall.

What to Look For When You Visit a Gym

Look for good signs on your visit:

  • Look for coaches who listen.
  • Look for clean mats.
  • Look for small groups.
  • Look for children who smile and help each other.
  • Look for classes that teach words and balance.

Signs the Program Works for Your Child

Watch for positive shifts at home:

  • Your child stands tall.
  • Your child speaks with a clear voice.
  • Your child shares good news from school.
  • Your child looks forward to each practice.
