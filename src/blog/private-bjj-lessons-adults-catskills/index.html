Who Chooses Adult Private Coaching?

Adults come to Sensei Sandy BJJ for many reasons:

  • Complete Beginners: Start with calm, guided drills. You learn basic positions before joining groups.
  • Busy Mountain Schedules: Mountain business owners and remote workers pick lesson times by request.
  • Body Longevity: Adult practitioners protect their joints through mechanical leverage and smooth technique.

  • Specific Skill Focus: Master a single position like the back escape or half guard.

What Happens During a 60-Minute Adult Session?

Each private lesson follows a clear structure:

  • Movement Check (10 Minutes): Gentle mobility drills warm your back and hips. Sandy checks your balance and range of motion.
  • Core Technique (20 Minutes): Sandy breaks down one practical move. You study angles, head position, and weight distribution.
  • Coached Resistance (20 Minutes): You practice the technique against steady, cooperative resistance. Sandy guides your grips and timing. Skill-based resistance activities begin at the right pace from day one.
  • Review and Key Takeaways (10 Minutes): Sandy recaps the mechanical details. You leave with one clear problem solved.

Pure Leverage Replaces Brute Force

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu uses leverage to manage weight and pressure. A smaller person controls a larger partner with smart body placement.

In private lessons, Sandy shows you how small shifts make a big difference:

  • Use bone frames to carry weight.
  • Use hip turns to create power.
  • Keep safe angles that hold balance.
  • Breathe steady to stay calm under pressure.
