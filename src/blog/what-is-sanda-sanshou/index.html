What Is Sanda? Strikes and Throws

Sanda is striking plus throws. Jiu jitsu is grappling plus control.

Updated February 22, 2026

Home → Blog → What Is Sanda Sanshou

Sanda uses strikes and throws. BJJ uses holds, escapes, and ground control. This guide can help you choose which skills to learn first. Reserve Free Intro.

Sanda in a Few Words

  • Sanda is also called Sanshou. It uses full-contact punches and kicks.
  • Clinch and throws are a big part of the sport format.
  • In many events, action resets after a clean throw.

What Sanda training looks like (for a grappler)

In Sanda, you work on range and timing. You can move from strikes to a clinch and throw. Balance and posture matter at each step. Reserve Free Intro.

The parts that overlap with jiu jitsu

  • Balance and base: if your hips stay under you, you are hard to throw.
  • Clinch awareness and hand fighting: inside control still matters.
  • Off-balancing into takedown entries: same principle, different range.
  • Calm under pressure: contact tolerance transfers directly.

Skills That Differ From BJJ

  • You practice strike timing and range.
  • Practice seeing kicks with a coach.
  • You learn to cover strikes and move your feet.
  • Each sport has rules that shape your choices.

Who should try Sanda first

  • You love striking and movement.
  • You want a fast, upright sport with frequent reset points.
  • You want takedowns paired with standing striking.

Who should try jiu jitsu first

  • You want beginner-safe control skills and pacing.
  • You want to learn clinch and ground skills.
  • You want to practice holds and escapes.

Can Sanda beat jiu jitsu? Honest answer

The result depends on the people, range, and rules. Strikes matter at long range. Holds and escapes matter on the ground.

FAQs

What is Sanda vs Sanshou

Both names refer to the striking and throwing art.

Is Sanda good for self defense

You can learn range, timing, and throws. Personal safety also means spotting risks, making space, and finding an exit.

Is Sanda like Muay Thai

Both use strikes. Sanda also uses throws and quick clinch entries. The rules shape each sport.

Can Sanda beat jiu jitsu

The result depends on the people and rules. Range and ground skills both matter.

Should I start with Sanda or jiu jitsu first

Choose BJJ for holds and ground skills. Choose Sanda for strikes and throws. Ask a coach which class fits your goals.
