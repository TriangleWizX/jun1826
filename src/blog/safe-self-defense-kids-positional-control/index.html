A child faced years of bullying

Sandy shared this story about a student. The child had faced years of bullying from one peer. This began before he started Jiu-Jitsu.

The peer threw a basketball at his face. The student took the peer's feet off the floor. He held the peer with a knee on the chest. He kept frames on both sides of the peer's body. Sandy said the student felt safe and sure.

We leave names out of this account. These are the facts Sandy shared.

Learn holds with a coach

A chest pin puts pressure on the body. Learn these skills on the mat with a coach. Ask the coach how to protect your partner.

This story reports one event. A child facing a threat needs adult help. Work with school staff on a plan to keep your child safe.
