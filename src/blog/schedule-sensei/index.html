Common scheduling traps (and how to fix them)

Trap 1: “I’ll start when things slow down.”

Pick a small first step. Put the class on your calendar. Leave time for the trip.

Trap 2: “I missed a week, so I’m behind.”

Text Sandy and choose a new date. Read the reset guide for more help. Schedule Sensei reset protocol

Trap 3: “I’m too busy to train.”

Choose a fixed day when you can. It gives you one less choice each week.

Trap 4: “I want the perfect plan.”

Make a plan that fits real life. Leave room to reset after a change.

Put the plan on your phone

  1. Add your class days to your phone calendar.
  2. Set an alarm for when to leave home.
  3. Share the plan with your family if useful.
  4. Save Sandy’s text contact for plan changes.
