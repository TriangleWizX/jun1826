BJJ vs Taekwondo for Family Martial Arts

By Sandy Jose Nunez · Updated October 16, 2026

{{ freeIntro.primaryCtaLabel }}

Catskills families often look for martial arts. Parents want an activity for kids and adults. Both sports build focus and balance.

Taekwondo focuses on fast kicks. Students practice high kicks and spin kicks. They also break wooden boards with their feet.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu focuses on balance and leverage. Students train on thick padded mats. They learn gentle holds with a partner.

Joint-friendly movement on the mat

Taekwondo uses fast jump kicks. Jiu-Jitsu is gentle on your joints. You use leverage and steady balance.

Smaller students guide larger partners to the mat. Everyone moves at a calm pace.

Coached movement starts on day one. You practice safe partner drills with kind help.

Train under one roof with your family

Families enjoy training under one roof. Kids build body control in youth classes. Adults build real strength in adult classes.

Parents and children share a common sport. You talk about new moves on the ride home.

Youths also join adult classes with coach approval. Our academy welcomes everyone as a team.
