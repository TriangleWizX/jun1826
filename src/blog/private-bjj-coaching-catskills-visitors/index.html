Ideal for Travelers and Second-Home Owners

Private coaching fits many mountain travelers:

  • Skiers and Snowboarders: Build hip mobility and balance for mountain slopes.
  • Resort and Lodge Guests: Staying at Scribner's Lodge or Deer Mountain Inn? The studio is only minutes away on Main Street.
  • City Practitioners: Visiting grapplers can keep up their routine while on vacation.
  • Couples and Friends: Share a private training hour with a friend or partner.
  • First-Time Visitors: Try Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in a calm, supportive room with a black belt coach.

A Clean, Welcoming Studio Space

Sensei Sandy BJJ maintains high standards for every visitor:

  • Spotless Room: High-grade tatami mats are cleaned before every session.
  • Calm Atmosphere: Training is friendly, cooperative, and guided at your pace.
  • Authentic Lineage: Coach Sandy Jose Nunez earned his black belt from Josh Griffiths at Clockwork Jiu-Jitsu in New York City.
  • Simple Clothes: Soft gym clothes and a water bottle are all you bring.

Inside a 60-Minute Visitor Session

Each session delivers high value:

  • Warm-Up (10 Minutes): Light joint mobility and core movement.
  • Key Concepts (20 Minutes): Learn mechanical leverage, frames, and safe balance.
  • Partner Drills (20 Minutes): Practice moves with Sandy guiding your grips. Skill-based resistance activities begin at the right pace from day one.
  • Debrief (10 Minutes): Review your progress and take home practical safety tips.
