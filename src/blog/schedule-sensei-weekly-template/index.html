What the sources say

  • CDC advises at least 150 minutes of moderate activity each week for adults. For ages six to seventeen, it advises at least sixty minutes daily. Class is one part of a full activity plan. CDC adults CDC children
  • CDC advises ages six to seventeen to move at least an hour daily. Include both moderate and vigorous activity. CDC
  • A habit study found a midpoint of 66 days. This was the time for habits to feel more natural. People varied. It is a study result, not a fixed clock. Lally et al., Wiley
  • A review found times from four to 335 days. People and tasks vary. PMC
  • If-then plans link a cue to a task. When work ends, you could pack the class bag. Gollwitzer & Brandstatter, 1997
  • The cited paper links if-then plans with starting action. It covers general planning, rather than a BJJ class test. Cancer Control

Why a weekly plan may help

  • CDC gives an hour-a-day movement goal for ages six to seventeen. A class can be part of that day. CDC
  • An archived CDC page reports 24% met the daily goal. It covers ages six to seventeen. The page was last reviewed July 26, 2022. CDC Archive
  • A 2021 media study found daily screen use of five hours, thirty-three minutes for ages eight to twelve. For ages thirteen to eighteen, it found eight hours, thirty-nine minutes. The report was published March 22, 2022. School and homework use were excluded. Common Sense Media
  • The cited study found a midpoint of 66 days for habits to feel more natural. Its range was eighteen to 254 days. That is a study result, rather than a personal deadline. Wiley Online Library
