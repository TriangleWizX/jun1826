The short answer

IBJJF sets minimum times for black belt degrees. The first three steps take at least three years each. Degrees four to six add five years each. Seven and eight add seven years each. Nine adds ten years. Proven activity and approval also matter. [1] Reserve Your Free First Visit

What black belt stripes mean

Black belt stripes mark formal degrees in IBJJF rules. Degrees one to six use a black belt. Seven uses red and black. Eight uses red and white. Nine uses red. IBJJF checks that degrees meet its own rules. [1]

When does the clock start?

For belt ranks, IBJJF counts from its registration date. The coach still decides when to award a belt. Degree rules also use certificate dates and proof of activity. Read the linked rules to check which date applies. [1]

What counts as proven activity?

Proof can include active membership or a listed teaching role. Approving student requests may count too. Prior degree records and IBJJF titles are other examples. A year needs qualifying activity to count. Read the rules for the full list. [1]

What paperwork do you need?

The December 2025 rules require current affiliation. The rank must be fully approved. You need First Aid or CPR proof. You also need a rules or referee course certificate from the last twelve months. Review time can vary. [1] Learn the class words

Before black belt

For adults, IBJJF lists two years at blue. Purple takes at least 1.5 years. Brown takes at least one year. Exceptions apply. Adult world champions at those ranks may qualify for an exception. The athlete must be at least eighteen. Check the linked rule. [1] [2] Learn the class words

Is it a stripe every 3 years?

The first three steps add three years each. Four through six add five years each. Seven and eight add seven years each. Nine adds ten years. These are minimum times. [1]
