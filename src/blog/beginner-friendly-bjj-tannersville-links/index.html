Learn the room and the rules

Sandy gives you a room tour and a safety walkthrough. You learn how to tap to end a hold. Your partner must release the hold when you tap.

You can tap your partner or the mat. You can also say “tap.” Ask for help when you need it.

Practice at your pace

Sandy sets the task and matches your partner. He guides the drill and watches you practice. You can ask for a break.

Learn a move in small steps. Try it with the coach's help. Ask how to keep both you and your partner safe.

Choose the right class

Adults can read the adult guide. Families can use the kids guide or teen guide.

Use the schedule for current times. Ask Sandy which class fits your needs.

Plan what to wear

You can wear day clothes for a visit. To train, wear clean sports clothes. Choose clothes with smooth seams and covered skin.

Keep sharp zip pulls and hard buttons off the mat. Follow the class plan Sandy sends you. Read the Show-Up Kit before you come.

Start from your current level

Tell Sandy about past training and your health needs. Ask how he can adjust the pace. If you have health concerns, speak with your health care team.

You can learn the class words as you go. Read the first-class guide for more detail.

Build a habit that fits

Choose a time you can make. Talk with Sandy after class about the next step. Keep your goals clear as you learn.
