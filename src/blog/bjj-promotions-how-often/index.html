What counts as a promotion in BJJ?

1) Stripes or degrees inside a belt

Some schools use tape stripes to mark progress. IBJJF calls them degrees. For ages sixteen and up, it lists four at white, blue, purple, and brown. Black belt has six before the next belt colors. [1]

2) Belt promotions

For athletes 16 and up, IBJJF lists the belt ranks as white, blue, purple, brown, and black. [1]

3) Black belt degrees and coral belts

IBJJF uses red and black for degree seven. Red and white is eight. Red is nine. [1]

Your coach and IBJJF have different roles

The coach decides when to award a belt. IBJJF checks its minimum rules before it records that rank. [1]

  • Coach decides when you are ready.
  • IBJJF sets minimum rules for its records.

IBJJF minimum ages for adult belts

  • Blue belt: 16+
  • Purple belt: 16+
  • Brown belt: 18+
  • Black belt: eighteen under the adult brown-belt world-title exception.

[1]

IBJJF minimum time in grade for adults (18+)

  • White belt: the coach sets the time.
  • Blue belt: 2 years minimum, with listed exceptions
  • Purple belt: 1.5 years minimum, with listed exceptions
  • Brown belt: 1 year minimum, with a listed exception

[1]

What the times add up to

Two years, 1.5 years, and one year add to 4.5. That is the usual IBJJF minimum from blue to black. Time at white comes before that. Exceptions apply. [1]

A key update to know

Adult world champions at blue, purple, or brown may qualify for an exception. The athlete must be eighteen or older. The exception removes the minimum time at that belt. [2] Learn the class words

Kids promotions can be more frequent

IBJJF suggests several stripe plans for ages four to fifteen. Your school decides which plan to use.

Monthly system (every month)

  • 11 degrees per belt
  • 12th degree is the move to the next belt color
  • Degree colors: 4 white, 4 red, 3 in the next belt color

[3] Learn the class words.

Quarterly system (every 3 months)

  • 3 white degrees per belt
  • 4th degree is the move to the next belt color

[4]

Every four months, three times a year

  • 2 degrees per belt
  • 3rd degree is the move to the next belt color

[5]

What shapes your own progress?

  1. Partner care: train at a pace that protects others.
  2. Regular practice: keep showing up to learn.
  3. Skill: use the moves against an active partner.

These totals assume practice for all 52 weeks:

  • Two classes a week adds up to 104 a year.
  • Three classes a week adds up to 156 a year.
  • Four classes a week adds up to 208 a year.
