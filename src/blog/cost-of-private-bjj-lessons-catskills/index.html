What Is Included in Your 60 Minutes?

Your session covers focused care. Every minute gives you real value:

  • Movement Check: Sandy checks your balance and joints.
  • Personal Plan: Sandy picks moves that fit your body.
  • Coached Partner Drills: You train with a black belt. Skill-based resistance activities begin at the right pace from day one.

Why Private Coaching Speeds Up Progress

Group classes build friendships and stamina. Private lessons give you direct mechanical feedback. One private session matches weeks of group mat practice.

Students gain clear steps:

  • Quick answers to your questions.
  • Custom tips for your grip and hips.
  • Full focus on your goals.
  • Safe, calm pacing.

Comparing Private Lessons and Group Classes

Both paths help you grow:

  • Private Coaching: Best for beginners who want a quiet room, students with specific goals, or busy guests.
  • Group Classes: Best for making friends, working with new partners, and steady weekly routines.
  • Both Paths: Many students take one or two private lessons first. Then they join regular group classes.
