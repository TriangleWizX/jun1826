What You Learn in Private Self-Defense Lessons

Sandy teaches practical, repeatable skills:

  • Distance Management: Keep safe space with tall posture, open hands, and clear eyes.
  • Grip Breaks: Release wrist grabs and sleeve holds using body geometry and leverage.
  • Balance and Base: Stand with rooted balance so a sudden push leaves you steady on your feet.
  • Ground Escapes: Learn safe escapes to your feet if you fall to the floor.
  • Voice and Boundaries: Practice using a clear, calm voice to set firm physical limits.

Why One-on-One Coaching Works Best

Personal safety matters. Private lessons give you clear benefits:

  • Quiet Environment: Learn at your own pace in a peaceful setting.
  • Direct Partner Feedback: Coach Sandy acts as your practice partner, ensuring every drill feels calm and controlled.
  • Customized to Your Body: Sandy tailors techniques to your height, mobility, and everyday routines.
  • Immediate Mastery: You repeat each move until the mechanics feel natural and effortless.

Inside a 60-Minute Lesson

Each private session is organized and thoughtful:

  • Warm-Up and Posture (10 Minutes): Gentle stretches, balance checks, and breathing exercises.
  • Concept Focus (20 Minutes): Break down one specific hold escape or boundary drill.
  • Coached Partner Drills (20 Minutes): Practice with Sandy guiding every movement. Skill-based resistance activities begin at the right pace from day one.
  • Debrief and Review (10 Minutes): Recap key angles, leverage points, and personal safety habits.
