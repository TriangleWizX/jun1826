Gi vs No-Gi for Self-Defense

In no-gi, you use body grips to hold and escape. In gi, you can use cloth grips too. Both let you practice balance and control.

Ask your coach which format fits your goals. You can learn from both. Practice how to hold position and move with care.

Is No-Gi Good for Beginners?

Beginners can learn no-gi with a coach. You can also try gi as you learn.

Start with simple drills. Practice balance and grips. Let your coach guide the pace.
