The goal: Keep your body feeling great.

  • Keep movement clean.
  • Keep joints warm and mobile.
  • Return to class ready to train.

Step 0: Safety Check on the Floor

  • Clear space: Make room on your floor. Move tables and toys.
  • Floor surface: Use a thick rug or yoga mat.
  • Golden rule: Move slow and smooth at home.
  • Listen to your body: Rest when you need a pause.

Option A: The Busy Day (20 minutes)

Best for: Travel days or gentle recovery.

Warm-up: Neck, shoulder, and hip circles with gentle squats.

Movement base: 45 seconds work, 15 seconds rest.

  • Shrimps on the floor.
  • Bridges with a shoulder roll.
  • Technical stand-up with steady base.
  • Sit-through hip heists.
  • Gentle sprawls with soft landings.

Mind practice: Picture clean bridge timing. Learn class words.

Note down: Write one question for your coach.

Option B: Deep Practice (45 minutes)

Best for: Deep winter snow days.

Warm-up: Full joint circles and light jumping jacks.

Movement: Three rounds of mat drills.

Skill focus: Pick one move to drill solo.

Fitness: A brisk indoor circuit to keep your heart healthy.

Option C: The Mat Snack (5 minutes)

Quick and easy while tea brews.

Mat Snack

5 MIN
  • 10 Shrimps (each side)
  • 10 Bridges (look back)
  • 5 Tech Stand-ups (each side)
  • 30 sec Plank (core tight)
  • 5 Deep Breaths
