What does family martial arts mean?

It means a school has a way for more than one age group to train. A young child may learn in a kids class. An older child may join a teen class. A parent may take an adult class. Each person learns with people in a like age group.

Some families take turns on the mat. Some may come in for class at the same time. Ask how the class plan works. Check the class schedule for current days and times.

What can a first class look like?

A first class is a chance to meet the coach and see the room. You can ask how kids learn and how the coach sets the pace. You can share what helps your child feel at ease.

Students learn how to move with care. They may practice balance, safe falls, and simple ways to move on the mat. The coach shows each step. A partner drill lets students try a skill with help.

Kids learn to listen, take turns, and work with a team. Teens learn the same core skills with a group for their age. Adults can learn the moves one step at a time. Each person can ask for help as they learn.

Pick the right class for each person

Kids ages five to nine have a class made for young learners. They play games, build balance, and practice body control. Read the kids class guide to learn more.

Teens ages ten to seventeen train with other teens. They learn skills, clear rules, and ways to use their voice. See the teen class guide.

Adults can start with basic moves and build from there. New students can learn each move in class. Read the adult class guide.

Questions to ask before you start

A visit can help you learn if the school feels right. Here are a few things to ask:

  • Which class fits my child’s age?
  • How does a new student join a class?
  • What will the coach teach on day one?
  • How do students ask to pause or get help?
  • Where can I see class times and plan a visit?

Good answers should be clear. You should know who will teach, what the first class will be like, and what you need to bring.

How to start as a family

First, think about who wants to learn. Then look at the class for each age group. You can book a Free Intro to meet the coach and tour the room. One visit can help you see how class works.

Each person can start at their own pace. One family may have a child who loves to move and a parent who is new to the mat. The coach can help each person take the next step.

Bring your questions. Share what each person hopes to learn. You can make a plan that fits your family and your week.
