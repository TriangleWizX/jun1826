Community Partners

Referral pages for hospitality, wellness, camps, and schools.

Sensei Sandy BJJ partners with local hotels, restaurants, wellness providers, camps, and schools around Tannersville, Hunter, Windham, Haines Falls, and the Catskills. Use these pages when a guest, parent, student, or staff member wants a calm first step into Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Each referral path points people toward the right Free Intro, directions to the second-floor studio, and beginner-friendly class options for kids, teens, and adults.

Use this page as the crawlable directory for the partner pages that explain how referrals work, who each offer fits, and where families can find the right local entry point.
