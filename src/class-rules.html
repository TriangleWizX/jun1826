Five Safety Rules

If someone is mean or tries to hurt you, leave if you can. Find an adult you trust.

1

Avoid the Fight

Leave before a fight. Move to a safe place. Find an adult.

2

Protect Yourself and Leave

If you are hurt, cover your head. Move to a safe place. Find an adult.

3

Use the Three T Steps

  1. Talk: Use a calm voice. Say, “Please stop and step back.”
  2. Tell: Use a firm voice. Say, “Back up now.”
  3. Tie Up: Cover your head. Make space. Leave. Find an adult.
4

Grappling Focus in Class

Use the moves Sandy teaches. Tell your partner if you need less force or a break.

5

Use Submissions with Control

Use light force. Let go right away when your partner taps. Move with care.

Partner Safety

  • Use the grips and moves Sandy gives you.
  • Keep your body in control. Keep your partner safe.
  • Ask before you move faster or use more force.
  • Tap to ask for a break. Let go right away when your partner taps.
  • Stop when Sandy calls, “Time.”
  • Tell Sandy if you need a break or help. Ask to change a move if you need to.

Respect and Behavior

  • Listen to Sandy. Follow his steps or ask for help.
  • Use each person’s name and pronouns. Treat students and guests with respect.
  • Line up at the start and end of class. Say hello and thank your partners.
  • Ask Sandy when you have a question.
  • Be kind. Help your team learn.

Read our inclusion page to learn how we treat people and help them take part.

Cleanliness and Health

  • Wash your gi or training clothes after each class.
  • Take off your shoes before you step on the mat. Keep them by the seats.
  • Keep your fingernails and toenails short and clean.
  • Remove jewelry before class.
  • Tell Sandy if you feel tired, sick, or dizzy, or need a break.
