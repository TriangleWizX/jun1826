Common Questions

Is this appropriate for complete beginners?
Yes. Every beginner class is built for first-timers with a reserved spot, clear instruction, and grappling-only training. You start with controlled movements and coached partner practice at your own pace.
Can sessions be customized for individual mobility goals?
Yes. Pace and focus adapt to each client. Private coaching allows tailor-made progression for clients rebuilding mobility and coordination.
How long is a private session?
Private coaching appointments are 45 to 60 minutes long. This allows ample time for a calm warm-up, skill instruction, and controlled drilling at a comfortable pace.
What style of martial arts is taught?
Our program is purely Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and technical grappling. Training focuses entirely on positioning, leverage, body mechanics, balance, and clean technical control.
Where is the studio located?
We are located at 6045 Main Street, 2nd Floor Studio, Tannersville, NY 12485. Centrally located on the mountaintop, close to Hunter, Windham, and Haines Falls.
