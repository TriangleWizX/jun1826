Clinical Scope & Safety Guidelines

Joint-Sparing Mechanics

Techniques emphasize mechanical leverage, neutral spine posture, and smooth floor transitions that protect joints and connective tissue.

Kinetic Chain Loading

Coached ground engagement gently integrates core stabilization, pelvic stability, and full-body rotational mobility at a gradual pace.

Controlled Movement Pacing

Beginners practice slow, predictable movements with attentive partners. All drills maintain steady control and cooperative pacing.

Tap-Early Culture

Students learn early physical and verbal signaling habits from day one. Every partner respects comfortable boundaries and mutual care.

Scope of Practice Disclaimer

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is functional movement and exercise, not physical therapy or medical rehabilitation. Healthcare providers and clients make their own health decisions. We welcome collaborative movement guidelines from licensed providers to support your client's active lifestyle.

Provider FAQ

How do you accommodate past injuries or movement limitations?
Every new student begins with a 15-minute consult. Sandy reviews notes on joint comfort, range of motion, and past rehabilitation. Drills are modified so students work strictly within comfortable movement arcs.
How is pacing controlled for patients returning to physical activity?
Beginner classes emphasize structured positional drills rather than high-intensity sparring. Students partner with experienced, calm teammates who match their pace and practice mutual care.
Can physical therapists communicate directly with Coach Sandy?
Yes. Healthcare providers and physical therapists are welcome to text Sandy directly at (917) 736-8649 or share written movement precautions. We welcome your recommendations.
Is martial arts experience needed for my patient to start?
Martial arts experience is optional. Most adult students start as complete beginners looking for enjoyable, joint-friendly ways to stay active and mobile.
How does the Free Intro consultation work?
The first visit includes a tour of our studio, a discussion of personal movement goals, and a gentle, one-on-one introduction to fundamental balance and mobility.
