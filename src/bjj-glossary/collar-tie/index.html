Collar Tie

Quick meaning

In BJJ, a collar tie is a hand hold near the back of a partner’s head. It can guide head position and connect to a snap down.

In plain words

You place one hand near the back of the head. This helps guide your partner’s head and posture.

Why it helps

A collar tie can help you guide a partner, make space, or set up a snap down. Head position and footwork also matter.

Start here

Learn the grip with a coach. Use a light touch and follow the class cue.

Learn it in class

A coach can show where the hand rests. The grip sits near the back of the head. Your hand can guide the head, while your feet help you stay set. Keep your arms loose and your eyes on the coach. Start with a slow turn. Then take a short step. A small move lets you learn the feel of the tie.

You can learn with a partner who moves at the same pace. Take turns with the grip. One person guides. The other person turns or steps. The coach can say when to switch. Each turn has one clear task. This helps both people learn the cue and feel the change in space.

A collar tie can lead to a snap down. In that move, the hand guides the head down. The coach shows when and how to use it. Follow the coach’s cue and use a light touch. Your coach can show each step. Try one small step at a time.

Coach says

Ask your coach to show you.

Stay safe

Move with care.

FAQ

What does collar tie mean in BJJ?

It is a hand hold near the back of a partner’s head. It can guide head position and link to a snap down.

What is a single collar tie?

A single collar tie uses one hand near the back of the head. Ask your coach to show the grip and a safe way to practice it.
