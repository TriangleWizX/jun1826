Collar Tie

Quick meaning

A collar tie is a hand hold near the back of a partner’s head.

In plain words

Use the hand to guide the head and body.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how collar tie connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It can help you guide your partner.

Collar ties help with posture control. angle changes. snap-down setups. and safer entries when standing exchanges become dynamic.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Keep a light hand. Ask your coach to show you.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working collar tie.

Coach says

You see collar ties in hand fighting. neutral standing exchanges. and transitions into sprawls

Stay safe

Move with care. Do not pull the neck.

Coached rounds begin at a controlled pace so everyone trains safely.
