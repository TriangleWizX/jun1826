Inside Trip

Quick meaning

An inside trip blocks or hooks a partner’s leg from the inside.

In plain words

Take the leg from the inside as you guide the body.

Why it helps

Guide the body as you block or hook the leg.

FAQ

Do inside trips work when skipping upper-body control?

An inside trip blocks or hooks a partner’s leg from the inside. You guide their upper body.

When are inside trips most available?

They commonly appear from clinch ties and body-lock style exchanges.
