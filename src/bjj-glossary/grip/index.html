Grip

A grip is a hand connection on your partner or their clothing that helps you control, pull, steer, or slow their movement.

Foundational concept Basics Common Grip Fighting Gi Collar Grip

Quick definition

A grip is a hand connection on your partner or their clothing that helps you control, pull, steer, or slow their movement.

Beginner translation

A grip gives your technique something real to connect to instead of reaching at empty space.

Why it matters

Beginners often discover that a good grip changes a technique more than trying to use extra strength does.

What beginners should know

A common mistake with grip is using it passively instead of with structure and timing.

Common class phrases

  • You see grips everywhere in gi training
  • and in no-gi you see the same idea through wrists
  • head ties
  • and body connections

Beginner Safety Cue

Safety cue: Move with control and communicate early while training grip.
