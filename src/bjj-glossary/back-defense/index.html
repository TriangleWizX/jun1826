Back Defense

Quick meaning

Back defense helps keep your neck safe and lets you turn to face a partner.

In plain words

Stay safe when a partner is on your back. Guard your neck.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how back defense connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Guard your neck first. Then work to face your partner.

It gives beginners a clear survival structure under one of the strongest attacking positions in BJJ. Builds control and clearer decisions during back defense.. Improves timing and structure for safer training..

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Cover your neck. Use your hands to block. Turn with care.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working back defense.

Coach says

Back defense connects to seatbelt. back control. rear naked choke

Stay safe

Move with care. Guard your neck. Reset if you need.
