Basics · Beginner

Safety

Quick meaning

Safety in BJJ means clear taps, calm moves, partner care, and coach help.

In plain words

Move with care. Speak up when you need help.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how safety connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It helps everyone learn with trust.

Safety gives beginners permission to reset early with complete composure. It helps partners train with control instead of surprise force. It makes class feel predictable enough to keep learning.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Listen to your coach. Tap when you need to stop.

Coach cue: Tap early, move with control, and ask for a reset before you feel stuck.

Coach says

Tell your coach if you need help.

Stay safe

Use clear taps. Move with care. Follow your coach.

Coached rounds begin at a controlled pace so everyone trains safely.
