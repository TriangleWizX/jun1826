What is a sweep single?

A sweep single is a takedown that starts with one leg.

You use an angle to move your partner off balance.

In plain words

You hold one leg. Then you move to the side and help your partner fall with care.

Where you see it

You may see it in standing drills and takedown work.

What to learn

Learn the set-up with a coach. Move at a calm pace.

Stop if your partner taps or asks to pause.
