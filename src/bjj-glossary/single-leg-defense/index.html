Single Leg Defense

Quick meaning

Single-leg defense uses stance, balance, hands, and hips to stop a one-leg takedown.

In plain words

Keep your base. Move your hips. Use your hands.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how single leg defense connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It can help you stay on your feet.

Single leg defense gives students a framework when a leg gets captured. It combines balance recovery. hip positioning. and upper-body control so they can stay safe and counter.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.
