Penetration Step

Quick meaning

A penetration step is the lead step that drives a shot entry forward under control to connect to legs or hips with better angle and balance.

In plain words

Bend your knees. Step in with care.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how penetration step connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It helps you learn how to move in with care.

It helps students reach connection with structure instead of diving from too far away. Builds control and clearer decisions during penetration step.. Improves timing and structure for safer training..

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

FAQ

What does a good penetration step do?

It carries your body into contact with posture, angle, and balance.

Why do beginners struggle with penetration step?

They overreach or lose posture instead of stepping with control.
