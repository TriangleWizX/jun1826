Sprawl Defense

Quick meaning

Sprawl defense uses your hips, legs, arms, and angle to stop a takedown.

In plain words

Move your hips back and down to make a shot hard to finish.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how sprawl defense connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Use your hips and arms to stop the shot.

Sprawl defense teaches beginners how to respond when someone attacks the legs. It helps defend against double legs and single legs. and often leads into front headlocks. go behinds. or resets to safer standing control.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Move your hips back. Use your hands. Turn at an angle.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working sprawl defense.

Coach says

Sprawl defense connects to sprawl. double leg. single leg

Stay safe

Move with care. Follow your coach. Reset if you need.
