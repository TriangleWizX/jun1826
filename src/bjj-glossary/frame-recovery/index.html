Frame Recovery

Quick meaning

Frame recovery means rebuilding useful frames after pressure has collapsed or weakened your structure.

Frame recovery is the moment when you rebuild structure before trying to escape. Beginners often think the answer is to move harder, but many escapes only start working once the frames come back first.

In plain words

Use your arms or legs to make space, then move.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how frame recovery connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It can help you get back to a safe spot.

Frame recovery helps manage incoming pressure and creates the first opening for escapes. guard recovery. or hip movement.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

FAQ

Is frame recovery the same as an escape?

Not exactly. It is often the first part of an escape. You rebuild structure first, then use that structure to improve position.

Why do coaches talk about frames so much?

Frames help prevent pressure from collapsing everything at once. In their absence, it is much harder to breathe, turn, or bring the knees back in.

Does frame recovery only matter on bottom?

It matters most there, because bottom players rely on structure to stay safe and create movement under pressure.
