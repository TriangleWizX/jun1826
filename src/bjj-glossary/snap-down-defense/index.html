What snap down defense means in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Snap down defense helps you stay on your feet when a partner pulls your head down.

In plain words

Stand tall. Use your feet to find your base.

What it does

It can help you keep your balance and neck safe.

Where you may see it

You may hear this in stand-up drills or when a partner uses a collar tie.

Why it matters

Good head and foot place can help you keep your balance.

More words

  • Snap Down
  • Head Position
  • Collar Tie
  • Front Headlock

Stay safe as you learn

Learn to move your feet and stand tall with a coach. Use care and stop if your partner taps.
