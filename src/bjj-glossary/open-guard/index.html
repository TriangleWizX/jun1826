Open Guard

Quick meaning

Open guard is a bottom position.

In plain words

Your legs are free to push, hook, block, and guide.

Why it helps

Your legs can help you move and make space.

Start here

Use your legs with care. Ask your coach to show you.

Coach says

  • You often see open guard after closed guard opens
  • during guard recovery

Stay safe

Move with care.

FAQ

What does open guard mean in BJJ?

Open guard is a bottom position. Your legs stay free to block or guide a partner.

How should beginners train open guard safely?

Train open guard with control, follow coach cues, and tap or reset early when needed.
