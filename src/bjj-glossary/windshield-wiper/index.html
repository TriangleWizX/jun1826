Windshield Wiper

Quick meaning

A windshield wiper is a leg movement where the lower body swings or rotates from one angle to another to clear hooks, switch positions, or stabilize movement.

In plain words

Move your legs from side to side as your coach shows you.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how windshield wiper connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It can help you move past the legs.

This movement appears in passing. top transitions. and some guard adjustments. It helps change angle and remove obstacles while preserving your momentum.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Go slow. Ask your coach to guide you.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working windshield wiper.

Coach says

Move your legs with care.

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training windshield wiper.

FAQ

Where is windshield wiper most useful?

In passing and top transitions where hips need to change angle while staying connected.

What makes windshield wiper feel loose?

Leg motion separated from coordinated hip and upper-body connection.
