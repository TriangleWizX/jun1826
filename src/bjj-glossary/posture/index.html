Posture

Posture is the alignment of your body so you can stay balanced, strong, and harder to break down or control.

Foundational concept Basics Common Base Guard Armbar

Quick definition

Posture is the alignment of your body so you can stay balanced, strong, and harder to break down or control.

Beginner translation

Posture is how you hold yourself so you do not get folded, pulled apart, or tipped over easily.

Why it matters

Posture is one of the quiet details that changes everything. Beginners with better posture usually feel safer and more effective fast.

What beginners should know

A common mistake with posture is using it passively instead of with structure and timing.

Common class phrases

  • You hear about posture when someone is inside guard
  • trying to stand
  • trying to pass
  • or resisting chokes and arm attacks

Beginner Safety Cue

Safety cue: Move with control and communicate early while training posture.
