BJJ Foundations & Practice Methods

Drilling in BJJ

Drilling means practicing a specific movement or sequence with a partner to build feel and confidence.

Quick Meaning

Drilling is structured repetition. In class, students practice movements like sweeps, passes, and escapes with cooperative resistance so the body learns the mechanics naturally.

In Plain Words

Think of drilling like practicing scales in music. You repeat the movement smoothly, step by step, so your muscles and balance remember what to do when training live.

Why Drilling Helps Beginners

When you start jiu-jitsu, learning everything during live rounds can feel fast. Drilling slows the pace down. It gives you time to feel body weight, refine hand placement, and ask questions.

How We Drill in Class

The coach shows one key concept with clear cues. Partners take turns performing five or ten calm repetitions each. As you gain familiarity, your partner provides light, realistic feedback.

Safety and Cooperative Movement

Move with care and respect your partner's pace. Drilling is a team activity where both students help each other learn. Communicate openly and rest whenever you need.

Related Glossary Terms

  • Roll · Live practice rounds guided by coach structure.
  • Open Mat · Dedicated mat time for drilling and questions.
  • Tap · The universal signal to pause, reset, and learn.
  • Escape · Fundamental defensive movements practiced in drills.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is drilling different from rolling?

Yes. Drilling focuses on repeating one specific technique with partner support. Rolling is a continuous round where both practitioners try different ideas.

Do beginners need experience to start drilling?

Not at all. In our beginner lane, coaches walk you through each step alongside experienced, patient partners.
