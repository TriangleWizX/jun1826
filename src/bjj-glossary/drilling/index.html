Drilling in BJJ

Quick definition

Drilling means practicing a move again and again.

In class

You repeat a move to learn its steps and timing.

Safety cue

Move with care. Follow the coach. Ask for help if you feel unsure.

Related terms

  • Roll
  • Open Mat
  • Tap

FAQ

Is drilling a live roll?

Drilling repeats a move. A roll is live practice.
