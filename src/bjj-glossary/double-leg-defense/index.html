Double Leg Defense

Quick meaning

Double leg defense helps stop a partner from grabbing both legs and finishing a takedown.

In plain words

Stop the shot before your partner gets to your hips.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how double leg defense connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Use your hands, hips, and angle to stop the shot.

Students need to know that defending a double leg is not only about throwing the legs back.. It also involves head position, hand control, hip pressure, and creating a better angle.. It turns reactive defense into structured anti-takedown control..

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Sprawl. Block with your hands. Turn to an angle.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working double leg defense.

Coach says

Double leg defense connects to sprawl defense, level change, shot entry, head position, and double leg.

Stay safe

Move with care. Follow your coach. Reset if you need.
