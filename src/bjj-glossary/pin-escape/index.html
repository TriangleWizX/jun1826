Pin Escape

Quick meaning

A pin escape is the act of getting out from underneath a controlling top position where your movement has been restricted.

In plain words

Make space, then turn to a safe spot.

Why it helps

It can help you move and feel less stuck.

Start here

Stay calm. Ask your coach for one cue.

Coach says

Ask your coach to show you.

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training pin escape.
