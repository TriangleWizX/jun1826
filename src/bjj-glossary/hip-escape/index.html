Hip Escape

Quick meaning

A hip escape makes space to move away.

In plain words

A hip escape makes space to move away.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how hip escape connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It helps you move with care.

The hip escape helps with guard recovery. escapes. and preventing top pressure from collapsing fully onto you.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.
