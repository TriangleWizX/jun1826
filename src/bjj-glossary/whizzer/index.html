Whizzer

Quick meaning

A whizzer is a strong overhook used to counter underhooks, disrupt posture, and make takedowns or body-lock entries harder to finish.

In plain words

Wrap one arm over your partner’s arm.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how whizzer connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It can help you hold your ground.

Whizzers are important in standing grappling. takedown defense. and transitions where someone is trying to connect tightly to your body. Beginners who learn the whizzer understand how to recover posture and balance with composure when an underhook gets deep.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

FAQ

Is a whizzer simply any overhook?

No. A whizzer is an overhook used with stronger defensive pressure and angle.

Does the arm alone make the whizzer work?

No. Hips, feet, posture, and angle make the whizzer effective.
