Torreando Defense

Quick meaning

Torreando defense helps you turn with the legs and bring them back in before a partner passes.

In plain words

Turn with your partner. Bring your legs back in.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how torreando defense connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It can help you keep your guard.

Torreando defense teaches guard retention. hip movement. turning with the passer. and rebuilding frames before side control gets settled.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.
