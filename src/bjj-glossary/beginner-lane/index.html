Beginner class

Quick meaning

New to class? Sandy shows you the rules. You learn how to move with care. You work with a partner at your pace.

In plain words

Sandy guides your first class at a calm pace.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how beginner lane connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Your first class starts with a room tour and safety steps. Sandy picks a partner and pace. You learn with coached partner drills.

Beginner Lane lowers the fear barrier before the first visit. It gives new students a safer order for learning class words and movement. It helps coaches match pace, partner, and expectations.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.
