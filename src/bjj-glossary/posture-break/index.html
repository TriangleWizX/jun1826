Posture Break

Quick meaning

A posture break pulls or guides your partner so they lose an upright stance.

In plain words

Make your partner less steady and easier to guide.

Why it helps

It can make your partner less steady.

Start here

Use your legs and hips. Do not pull with your arms alone.

Coach says

  • Posture break connects to guard
  • wrist control

Stay safe

Move with care.

What makes posture breaks stronger?

A posture break pulls or guides your partner so they lose an upright stance.

Why does posture breaking help guard?

It limits balance and passing pressure, making control and attacks easier to build.
