Knee Cut

Quick meaning

A knee cut is a guard pass where the top player slices the knee across the bottom player's legs to move toward side control or another strong top position.

Beginners learn that passes work better when the top and bottom halves of the body are controlled together.

In plain words

You use your knee like a wedge to cut through the guard instead of trying to jump all the way around it.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how knee cut connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Beginners learn that passes work better when the top and bottom halves of the body are controlled together.

The knee cut combines pressure. angle. and upper-body control to help the top player pass efficiently.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

A common mistake with knee cut is using it passively instead of with structure and timing.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working knee cut.

Coach says

You see knee cuts against open guard. half guard. and situations where the top player can pin the upper body while slicing through

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training knee cut.

Coached rounds begin at a controlled pace so everyone trains safely.
