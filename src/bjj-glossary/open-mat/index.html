What is open mat in BJJ?

Open mat is practice time with more choice. Students can drill, ask a coach, or work on a skill.

Open mat in plain English

At open mat, the coach may give less set class work. You can ask a question, repeat a move, or work with a partner. Each school runs this time in its own way.

More choice still calls for care and respect.

What students do

Students may drill a move, watch a round, or ask for help. The coach can help you pick a safe task for your skill level.

When it takes place

Open mat times vary by school. Check the current schedule before you plan a visit.

Why it matters

Open mat gives you time to repeat a skill. You can learn at your pace and ask for help when you need it.
