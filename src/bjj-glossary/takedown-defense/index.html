Takedown Defense

Quick meaning

Takedown defense uses stance, footwork, hips, and arm control to stop or counter a takedown.

In plain words

You stay on your feet and block a partner’s takedown.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how takedown defense connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It helps you stay safe while you learn to stand.

It helps beginners stay safe and organized in standing exchanges while preventing easy positional losses. Builds control and clearer decisions during takedown defense.. Improves timing and structure for safer training..

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.
