What front headlock series means in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

A front headlock series links a head hold to the next move.

In plain words

It is a chain of moves from a front head hold.

What it does

It can link snap downs, go-behinds, spin-behinds, and chokes.

Where you may see it

It can start after a sprawl, a missed shot, or a turn to turtle.

Why it matters

One hold can lead to the next move.

More words

  • Front Headlock
  • Snap Down
  • Spin Behind
  • Go Behind

Stay safe as you learn

A coach can teach one move at a time. Go slow and follow each cue.
