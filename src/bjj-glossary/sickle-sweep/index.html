Sickle Sweep

Quick meaning

A sickle sweep is a sweep against a standing opponent where the bottom player disrupts the upper body and cuts the standing leg out with the foot.

Beginners learn that many sweeps work by removing the right support point at the right time instead of trying to launch the whole person.

In plain words

You pull the person one way and cut their standing leg the other way so their balance disappears.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how sickle sweep connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Beginners learn that many sweeps work by removing the right support point at the right time instead of trying to launch the whole person.

The sickle sweep teaches off-balancing. timing. and how small foot-placement details can completely change the direction of a standing opponent.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

A common mistake with sickle sweep is using it passively instead of with structure and timing.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working sickle sweep.

Coach says

You see it from open-guard situations against standing passers when the bottom player gets strong sleeve or collar control and an opening near the ankle line. Sickle Sweep in drills. Sickle Sweep during positional rounds

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training sickle sweep.

Coached rounds begin at a controlled pace so everyone trains safely.
