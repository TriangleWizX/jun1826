Roll in BJJ

Quick definition

A roll is a practice round with a partner.

In class

You use moves while your partner responds. The coach sets the task and pace.

Safety cue

Follow the coach. Tap or ask for help if you feel unsure.

Related terms

  • Drilling
  • Open Mat
  • Tap
  • Escape

FAQ

Is a roll a fight?

No. It is a coached practice round.
