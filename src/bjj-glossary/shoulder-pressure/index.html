Shoulder Pressure

Quick meaning

Shoulder pressure uses your shoulder to limit a partner’s movement or block frames.

In plain words

Your shoulder acts like a soft block to make space hard to use.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how shoulder pressure connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It can help you learn safe, steady control.

This concept helps beginners understand that pressure is more than body weight. A well-placed shoulder can turn decent control into strong control. by affecting posture. head line, and the direction a partner can move.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Go slow. Keep your weight off the neck.
