Body-Lock Pass

Quick meaning

A body-lock pass uses a close hold to move past the legs.

In plain words

Hug the body. Keep your hips close. Move around the legs.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how body-lock pass connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It helps you pass with close control.

This pass teaches students that strong passing often comes from connection and hip control.. It is especially useful for creating pressure and reducing space.. It limits guard movement by controlling torso alignment..

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.
