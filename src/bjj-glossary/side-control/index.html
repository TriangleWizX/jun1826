What side control means in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Quick meaning

Side control is a top hold across your partner’s upper body.

In plain words

Your partner is beside you, not between your legs.

Why it helps

Side control teaches top holds and how to get free from them.

Start here

Use your arms to make space. Move your hips. Breathe slow.

Coach says

  • Frame first.
  • Recover guard.

Stay safe

Move with care.
