What side control means in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Side control is a top position where one person controls from across the other person’s upper body.

Foundational concept Positions Common Frame Shrimp Guard

Quick definition

Side control is a top position where one person controls from across the other person’s upper body.

Beginner translation

The top person is beside you, not between your legs. Your job on bottom is to make space and get your guard back.

Why it matters

Side control teaches pressure on top and survival on bottom. It is one of the first positions beginners learn to escape from.

What beginners should know

The bottom player should avoid pushing with straight arms. Better defense usually starts with frames, hip movement, and calm breathing.

Common class phrases

  • Frame first.
  • Recover guard.
  • Keep chest pressure.
  • Win the underhook.
  • Do not bench press the person.

Beginner Safety Cue

Safety cue: Move with control and communicate early while training side control.
