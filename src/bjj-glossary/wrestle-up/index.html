Wrestle Up

Quick meaning

Wrestle up means rising from guard or a lower position into a takedown, top control, or standing attack instead of staying underneath.

In plain words

Move up from guard and stand with care.

Why it helps

It can help you get back to your feet.

Start here

Go slow. Ask your coach to guide you.

Coach says

Ask your coach to show you.

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training wrestle up.
