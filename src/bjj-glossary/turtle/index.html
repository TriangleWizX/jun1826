Turtle

Quick meaning

Turtle is a defense pose on your knees and hands or elbows. Keep your arms close and head safe.

In plain words

Turtle is a shell pose. Stay small so you can move again.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how turtle connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Turtle can help you stay calm and find a way to move.

Turtle can protect space during scrambles and failed escapes. but only if it leads to movement. base recovery. and safer transitions.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Keep your arms close. Cover your head. Listen to your coach.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working turtle.

Coach says

You see turtle during scrambles. failed shots. guard passes

Stay safe

Move with care. Keep your head safe. Reset if you need.

Coached rounds begin at a controlled pace so everyone trains safely.
