How to Tap in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Tapping is your safety button. Tap to reset and keep learning.

Quick meaning

Tapping is the universal safety habit in grappling. You tap your partner or say the word tap. Your partner releases the hold right away.

Tapping keeps training safe, friendly, and sustainable for every student.

Two Clear Ways to Tap

Students use two simple methods to signal a reset:

  • Physical Tap: Tap twice on your partner's body with your hand. If their body is out of reach, tap twice on the mat.
  • Verbal Tap: Say the word "tap" out loud. Use your voice whenever your hands are trapped.

Both methods work the same way. As soon as you tap, your partner releases pressure and resets the position.

In plain words

A tap is a friendly signal. It tells your partner to release and reset. Everyone in class respects the tap right away.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how tapping connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Tapping protects your joints and muscles. Early taps keep your body fresh for regular practice. Coaches celebrate students who tap early. Tapping enables lifelong training with friends and family.

Practicing this safety habit gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Tap as soon as a hold feels snug. Tap when you feel stuck or tight. Tapping is how smart students learn and improve each week.

Coach cue: Tap early, smile, reset, and keep training.

Coach says

Tap early.. Say tap out loud.. Release on the first tap.. Reset with a fist bump.. Respect your partner.
