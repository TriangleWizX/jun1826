Frames

Quick definition

A frame is a body structure that helps you create space and block pressure.

Beginner translation

Use your arms or legs to make space.

Why it matters

It can help keep weight off you.

What beginners should know

Ask your coach where to place your hands.

Common class phrases

  • Make space with your arms.

Safety cue

Move with care. Stop if your partner feels pain.
