Guard Recovery

Quick meaning

Guard recovery means you build your guard back after it starts to open.

In plain words

Move your legs back between you and your partner.

Why it helps

It can help you get back to a safe spot.

Start here

Go slow. Ask your coach to show you.

Coach says

  • Bring your legs back in.

Stay safe

Move with care.
