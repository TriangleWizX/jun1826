Arm Drag

Quick meaning

An arm drag is a movement that guides your partner's arm across your center line to create an angle and expose the side or back.

Beginners learn that making the other person turn is often more useful than meeting them straight on.

In plain words

You move one arm out of the way so you can get around the person instead of staying square in front of them.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how arm drag connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Beginners learn that making the other person turn is often more useful than meeting them straight on.

The arm drag creates angles for back takes. takedowns. sweeps. and cleaner positional entries using balance and leverage.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

A common mistake with arm drag is using it passively instead of with structure and timing.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working arm drag.

Coach says

You see arm drags from standing. seated guard. butterfly-style exchanges

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training arm drag.
