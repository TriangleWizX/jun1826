Waiter Sweep

Quick meaning

A waiter sweep tips a partner from guard.

In plain words

A waiter sweep tips a partner from guard.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how waiter sweep connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It helps you move with care.

Waiter sweep helps students understand that sweeps do not always come from pushing sideways or exploding. Sometimes the best answer is getting under the base. lifting at the right time. and managing direction.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.
