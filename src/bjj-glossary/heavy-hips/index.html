Heavy Hips

Quick meaning

Heavy hips uses hip place and body weight to limit a partner’s move.

In plain words

Put your hips where your pressure is hard to move.

Why it helps

Good hip place helps you hold a safe spot.

Start here

Use balance. Do not drop your weight with force.

Coach says

  • Heavy hips connects to sprawl defense, pressure, top position, hip switch, and guard pass.

Stay safe

Move with care.
