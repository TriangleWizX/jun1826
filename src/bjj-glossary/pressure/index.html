Basics · Beginner

Pressure

Quick meaning

Pressure uses weight and body shape to limit a partner’s space.

In plain words

Use your body shape to make space hard to use.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how pressure connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Good pressure helps you hold a safe spot.

Pressure helps top players control movement. flatten frames. slow escapes. and make passing more effective.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Use shape and balance, not force.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working pressure.

Coach says

You see pressure in chest-to-chest control. pinning exchanges. side control

Stay safe

Move with care. Keep weight off the neck.

Coached rounds begin at a controlled pace so everyone trains safely.
