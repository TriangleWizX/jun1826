What a triangle means in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Quick meaning

A triangle is a submission where the legs trap the head and one arm to create a choke.

In plain words

You use your legs to make a triangle shape around your partner’s neck and arm, then apply controlled pressure until they tap.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how triangle connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

The triangle shows how Jiu Jitsu uses legs as powerful tools for control and submissions, even from the bottom.

The triangle turns guard into a direct submission threat and often chains naturally with armbars and sweeps. Builds control and clearer decisions during triangle.. Improves timing and structure for safer training..

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

A triangle is more than squeezing. Good triangles use angle, posture control, leg position, and patience.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working triangle.

Coach says

Break posture.. Cut the angle.. Lock the triangle.. Control the head.. Respect the tap.

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training triangle.
