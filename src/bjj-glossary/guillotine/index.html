Guillotine

Quick meaning

A guillotine is a front choke around the neck when a partner’s head is low.

In plain words

You hold the head and neck from the front.

Why it helps

This move shows why head position matters.

Start here

Move slow. Let go when your partner taps.

Coach says

  • You see guillotines after failed shots
  • front headlock situations

Stay safe

Move with care.
