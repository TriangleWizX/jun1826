Lapel Grip

Quick meaning

A lapel grip is a hold on the gi jacket.

In plain words

Hold the jacket to guide the body or slow a move.

Why it helps

A lapel grip can help you guide a partner.

Use a light grip. Ask your coach to show you.

FAQ

What does lapel grip mean in BJJ?

A lapel grip is a hold on the gi jacket. It can guide your partner or change their posture.

Are lapel grips only for submissions?

No. They also help with control, passing efficiency, and posture management.
