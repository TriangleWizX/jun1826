Top Position

Quick meaning

Top position means you are above your partner and usually have a better chance to apply pressure, pass, and control movement.

In plain words

You are on top and can move with care.

Why it helps

It can help you keep a safe, strong base.

Start here

Stay calm. Follow your coach’s cue.

Coach says

Ask your coach to show you.

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training top position.
