Granby Roll

Quick meaning

A Granby roll uses a shoulder roll to get free or change your angle.

In plain words

Roll over a shoulder to get free or face a new way.

Why it helps

A roll can help you get free and face a new way.

Start here

Roll over your shoulder. Do not roll on your neck.

Coach says

  • Granby roll connects to inversion
  • turtle

Stay safe

Roll with care over your shoulder.

FAQ

What is the purpose of a Granby roll?

A Granby roll uses a shoulder roll to get free or change your angle.

What is the main safety detail for Granby roll?

Roll across the shoulder path with control, not flat across the neck or back.
