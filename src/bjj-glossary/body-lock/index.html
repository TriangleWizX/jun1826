Body Lock

Quick meaning

A body lock wraps both arms around a partner’s torso.

In plain words

Wrap the body as your coach shows you.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how body lock connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It can help you stay close as you move.

The body lock helps with standing control. takedowns. passing pressure. and stopping the other person from turning away easily.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Use care. Ask your coach to show you.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working body lock.

Coach says

Stay close. Keep your base.

Stay safe

Move with care. Let go when your partner taps.
