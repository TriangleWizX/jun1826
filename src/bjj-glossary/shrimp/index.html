What shrimp means in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Quick meaning

Shrimp is a hip escape movement used to create space and improve position.

In plain words

You move your hips away from your partner so you can make room to escape or put your guard back.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how shrimp connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

Shrimping is one of the first movements beginners learn because it shows how hips, rather than arms, create space.

The shrimp creates room for frames. guard recovery. and escapes from side control or mount pressure.

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

The goal is not to slide randomly. The goal is to push, move your hips, and rebuild useful space.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working shrimp.

Coach says

Shrimp away.. Move your hips.. Recover guard.. Frame first, then shrimp.. Make space.

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training shrimp.

Coached rounds begin at a controlled pace so everyone trains safely.
