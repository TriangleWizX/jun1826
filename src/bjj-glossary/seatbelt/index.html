Seatbelt

Quick meaning

Seatbelt is a back hold: one arm goes over a shoulder, one under an arm.

In plain words

Your arms link around a partner from behind, like a seatbelt.

Why it helps

Stay linked to your partner before you try a move.

Start here

Keep your arms in place. Follow your coach’s cue.

Coach says

Ask your coach to show you.

Stay safe

Move with care.
