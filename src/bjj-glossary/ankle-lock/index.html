Ankle Lock

Quick meaning

An ankle lock is a submission that attacks the ankle and lower leg, typically from a leg-entanglement position with controlled alignment.

It teaches students that safe, structured control and knee-line awareness come before finishing mechanics.

In plain words

A coach can show you how the hold works.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how ankle lock connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

It helps you learn to move with care.

It gives a direct lower-body submission option when control and alignment are in place. Builds control and clearer decisions during ankle lock.. Improves timing and structure for safer training..

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

Go slow. Tap at once if it hurts.

Coach says

Stay safe

Move with control and communicate early while training ankle lock.
