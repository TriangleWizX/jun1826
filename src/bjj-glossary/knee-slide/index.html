Knee Slide

Quick meaning

A knee slide is a guard pass where the top player slides the knee through the guard while controlling the upper body and hips to move toward side control or mount.

In plain words

Knee slide means cutting the knee through the space in the guard so you can get past the legs and settle on top.

In Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, understanding how knee slide connects to balance and leverage helps you make smart choices during partner drills.

Why it helps

A knee slide is a guard pass where the top player slides the knee through the guard while controlling the upper body and hips to move toward side control or mount.

This is one of the clearest passing terms for beginners because it combines posture, angle, and pressure.. It shows that passing is about clearing the legs while staying connected enough to complete the pass.. It links upper-body control to reliable finishing positions..

Practicing this movement gives you confidence during partner movement. You learn when to build frames, when to create space, and how to stay calm.

Start here

A common mistake is trying to slide through before controlling the upper body well enough to keep the guard from recovering.

Coach cue: Stay calm and keep your structure while working knee slide.

Coach says

Knee slide connects to knee cut, crossface pressure, top position, mount transition, and side control.

Stay safe

FAQ

Is knee slide the same as knee cut?

They are closely related; many gyms use the terms interchangeably in passing context.

Why does knee slide fail for beginners?

They often move the knee before establishing enough upper-body control.
