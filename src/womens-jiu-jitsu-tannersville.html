Six useful problems to solve.

Each session uses short games, simple goals, and guided rounds. You learn by moving, trying, and adjusting.

  1. Stay safe and stand up.
  2. Escape a pin.
  3. Keep your guard.
  4. Reverse position.
  5. Hold the top position.
  6. Link the skills.

The proposed cohort syllabus

One shared problem each week.

The group builds from safety and space toward linking escapes, reversals, and control. Every class has a beginner lane and a more challenging variation for experienced students.

Proposed dates: November 7, 14, and 21; December 5, 12, and 19. No class November 28.

01 · Nov 7 — Stay safe and stand upLearn space, frames, and a safe way to get back to your feet. Experienced students add a partner who blocks the stand-up.
02 · Nov 14 — Escape a bottom pinWork from mount or side control. Beginners recover guard or get free; experienced students solve the same problem with tighter limits.
03 · Nov 21 — Keep your guardUse your legs and frames to keep a partner from passing. Add pace and pressure only when the pair is ready.
04 · Dec 5 — Reverse the positionFind a sweep or stand-up from guard. Experienced students connect the first attempt to a second option.
05 · Dec 12 — Hold the top positionPass the legs and settle into control while your partner works to escape.
06 · Dec 19 — Link the skillsPut the pieces together in guided rounds: escape, recover, reverse, and hold position.
