Six skills to learn.

Each class has short games and clear goals. You learn by moving, trying, and making small changes.

  1. Stay safe and stand up.
  2. Escape a pin.
  3. Keep your guard.
  4. Reverse position.
  5. Hold the top position.
  6. Link the skills.

Six-class skill path

One class, three ways to learn.

Each class starts from a new spot: chest to chest, chest to back, or neutral guard. Try the day's skill from each spot.

Planned dates: November 7, 14, and 21; December 5, 12, and 19. The group rests November 28.

Nov 7Escape: make space and standLearn a frame, get free, and stand up.

Chest to chest: Make space and find guard.

Chest to back: Turn in and face your partner.

Neutral guard: Frame, stand, and make space.

Nov 14Escape: get free from a pinStart under mount or side control. Find guard.

Chest to chest: Bridge, turn, and find guard.

Chest to back: Turn your shoulders to the mat.

Neutral guard: Make space and stand up.

Nov 21Guard: keep your spaceUse your legs and arms to keep guard.

Chest to chest: Frame and bring your knees in.

Chest to back: Turn in and face your partner.

Neutral guard: Keep guard as your partner moves.

Neutral guard: Sweep or stand up.

Dec 12Pass: hold the top spotPass the legs and hold side control.

Chest to back: Follow their turn and hold.

Neutral guard: Pass the legs and hold the top.
