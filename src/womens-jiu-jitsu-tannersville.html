Six skills to learn.

Each class has short games and clear goals. You learn by moving, trying, and making small changes.

  1. Stay safe and stand up.
  2. Escape a pin.
  3. Keep your guard.
  4. Reverse position.
  5. Hold the top position.
  6. Link the skills.

The proposed cohort syllabus

One shared skill each week.

We start with safe movement. Then we learn escapes, sweeps, and control. Each class has a path for new and returning students.

Planned dates: November 7, 14, and 21; December 5, 12, and 19. The group rests November 28.

01 · Nov 7 — Make space and standLearn frames and a safe way to get up. Returning students add light partner pressure.
02 · Nov 14 — Get free from a pinStart under mount or side control. Work to get free or find guard.
03 · Nov 21 — Keep your guardUse your legs and frames to keep your partner from passing.
04 · Dec 5 — Turn the spot aroundLearn a sweep or stand-up from guard. Then try a second move.
05 · Dec 12 — Hold the top spotPass the legs and hold side control as your partner works to get free.
06 · Dec 19 — Put it all togetherLink the skills in guided rounds: get free, find guard, sweep, and hold.
