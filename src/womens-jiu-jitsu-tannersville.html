Tannersville, NY · Proposed Saturday pilot

Women's Jiu-Jitsu Foundations

Learn Jiu-Jitsu. Find your training partners. Build your skills.

This proposed six-session No-Gi group is designed for women who want a calm, coached place to learn Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Sandy leads instruction. A female co-lead is planned to support welcome, partner matching, and student comfort.

What you will work on

  1. Stay safe and stand up.
  2. Escape bottom pins.
  3. Keep guard.
  1. Reverse position.
  2. Hold top position.
  3. Link the skills.

Each class uses short games, clear goals, matched partners, and guided positional rounds. You can pause or stop a round. No forced full-intensity rounds, uncontrolled takedowns, neck cranks, or twisting leg submissions.

Before you join

Start with a Free First Visit. Meet Sandy, see the room, and ask whether the proposed pilot fits your goals. The pilot needs a confirmed co-lead, reviewed safety and admissions procedures, enough paid reservations, and suitable training partners before it can launch.

There is no childcare provided. Photo and video are optional. Share only training needs that help with partner matching; you do not need to disclose medical or trauma history.
