HOW WE TRAIN TOGETHER

What a good Friday round looks like

Listen

Hear the rules and the starting spot before each round begins.

Stay Engaged

Try your best move. If it slips, take a breath and try again.

Care for Your Partner

Move with control. Help each other learn and stay safe.

When a partner taps or Sandy calls a pause, let go at once. Then reset.
