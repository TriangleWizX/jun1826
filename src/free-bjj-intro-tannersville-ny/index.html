What your Free First Visit includes

Start with a 15-minute Free First Visit. Talk with Sandy about your goals. Adults can choose to train that day, after the visit. Sandy must confirm the class first. Your first class is free. Kids and teens use their own class plan.

  1. Reserve your first visit. Choose the lane that fits who is starting.
  2. See the room. Learn the rules and talk about your goals.
  3. Choose the next step. Sandy helps you choose a class and pace.
