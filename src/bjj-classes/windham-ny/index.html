Where is the studio from Windham?

The studio is located at 6045 Main Street in Tannersville, NY. Take Route 296 south to Route 23A. The 11-mile drive takes 14 minutes. See route and parking details.

Can weekend visitors drop in from Windham Mountain Club?

Yes. Second-home owners and weekend skiers visiting Windham can maintain their training routine. We welcome visiting practitioners for cooperative, coached sessions. Check the schedule or text Sandy before you visit.

Which class is best for new students from Windham?

Adults can begin in the Beginner Lane, featuring slow partner drills and guided safety. Families with kids and teens can explore dedicated youth programs. Explore the Beginner Lane.

How do family classes work for Windham residents?

Parents and youth can train under one roof. Kids build body control and balance while parents improve core strength. Read about family martial arts.
