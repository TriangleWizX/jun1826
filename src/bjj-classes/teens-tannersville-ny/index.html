What Happens During Class?

Students do not spend class watching long demonstrations or repeating movements without resistance.

Each class progresses through:

  1. Movement connected to the day’s problem
  2. Chest-to-chest control and escapes
  3. Chest-to-back control and escapes
  4. A neutral guard game
  5. Coach-controlled resistance rounds
  6. A brief review

Sandy changes the partners, starting positions, scoring rules, and resistance level so beginners and experienced students can learn safely.

Each standard enrollment includes three planned classes per week (up to 36 scheduled classes over 12 weeks). Students select three recurring home-class reservations to keep training consistent and manage room capacity.

If your availability changes during a week, we offer open-seat rescheduling by text. Rescheduling depends on space and partner fit in that session. Text Sandy directly to check open seats.
