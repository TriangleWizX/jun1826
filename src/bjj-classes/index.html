The First-Visit Experience

Your First Class Is a Coached Learning Experience

Starting martial arts should feel welcoming, not intimidating. Here is how your Free First Visit works from the moment you walk through the door.

01

Connect & Reserve

Choose Kids, Teens, or Adults. Pick a date online in under a minute, or text Coach Sandy with your goals and schedule questions.

02

Studio Tour & Fitting

Arrive 10 minutes early. Sandy shows you the mats, locker space, and gets you fitted with a clean loaner uniform or training guidelines.

03

Coached Beginner Lane

Step onto the mats for a guided, gentle introduction. You partner with a cooperative teammate who helps you practice fundamental movements at your pace.

04

Zero-Pressure Review

Sit down with Sandy after class to chat about how it felt. No sign-up pressure, no contracts, and you can reschedule or pause anytime by text.
