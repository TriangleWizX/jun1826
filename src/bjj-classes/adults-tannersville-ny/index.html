Beginner Lane Structure

Adult Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu for Beginners: Start at Your Own Pace

Most adults worry about being out of shape or feeling clumsy on day one. At Sensei Sandy BJJ, every adult starts in our structured Beginner Lane.

1. Room Tour & Goal Mapping

Before ever stepping on the mat, you tour the studio, meet Sandy, and map your training goals in regular street clothes.

2. Mat Walkthrough & Safety Rules

Learn our core safety rules: tap early, communicate with your partner, breathe calmly, and control your own pace.

3. Coached Movement Rounds

Train in small groups capped at 12 students. Partners are matched for height, weight, and cooperative focus.
