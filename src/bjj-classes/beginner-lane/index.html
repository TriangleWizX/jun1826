Structured Onboarding

Our 3-Step Beginner Trust Protocol

Every new student starts with structured guidance. You learn safety rules first and move with confidence.

Step 01

Guided Room Tour & Equipment Walkthrough

Meet Sandy at the entrance. Walk through our clean studio space. See where students store shoes and gear. Ask every question in street clothes. Learn about clean training uniforms before touching the mats.

Step 02

Safety & Tap Walkthrough

Learn our core safety rules first. Practice how to tap with your hand. Tap your partner or tap the mat. Tapping signals an immediate reset. Partners pause and reset with care. You control your own pace.

Step 03

Coached Partner Drilling with Matched Teammates

Sandy pairs you with an experienced teammate. Partners match your size and calm tempo. Practice structured movements with cooperative resistance. Everyone moves with control and mutual respect.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Here is what new adult students ask before starting.

What is the Beginner Lane at Sensei Sandy BJJ?

The Beginner Lane is our calm onboarding system for adults. You learn safety rules, tour the room, and practice cooperative partner drills with experienced teammates.

Do I need previous martial arts training?

You start right where you are. Classes build strength, balance, and mobility through steady step-by-step guidance.

What should I wear to my first visit?

Wear clean athletic clothes such as sweatpants or shorts and a t-shirt. Bring a water bottle.

How do partners practice together?

Sandy pairs new students with trusted teammates. Partners share a cooperative tempo and respect every tap.
