Gentle Pacing Warmup Science

Why move before class?

BJJ uses your hips, back, arms, legs, and neck. These easy moves help you get set for class. Move slow. Take easy breaths. Stay in a range that feels comfortable. Then you can learn with care.

Safety note: Move with care. Stay in a range that feels good. Ask your doctor if you have health needs before trying new physical routines.

The 5 to 10 Minute BJJ Mobility Routine

Do each move 5 to 10 times. Go slow. Keep breathing smoothly as you move.

1 Hips

Hip Circles

Stand tall with feet apart. Move your hips in a small circle. Go both ways.

Helps you move on the mat and get up.
2 Pelvis

Pelvic Tilts

Bend your knees a bit. Tip your hips front, back, and side to side.

Helps you feel your back and hips before class.
3 Spine

Forward Fold to Stand

Bend at your hips. Let your back rest. Stand up slow.

Helps wake up your back and legs.
4 Torso

Side Bends

Lift one arm. Lean to the other side. Then switch.

Helps your sides and arms get set for class.
5 Shoulders

Shoulder Rolls

Lift your shoulders, roll them back, then down. Go both ways.

Helps your arms and shoulders get set.
6 Arms

Arm Circles

Make small arm circles. Go both ways.

Helps your arms get warm for partner drills.
7 Neck

Slow Neck Turns

Turn your head left and right. Look up and down. Move slow.

Helps you check how your neck feels and protect head posture.
8 Legs

Flat-Foot Squats

Keep your heels down if you can. Bend your knees. Stand up slow.

Helps you bend, guard base, and stand with care.
9 Hips

World’s Easiest Lunge

Step one foot back. Reach up. Take a breath. Switch sides.

Opens hip flexors after sitting or driving.
10 Spine

Cat-Cow

Get on hands and knees. Round your back, then lift it. Breathe.

Helps your back get set for mat work and bridges.
11 Core

Downward Dog to Plank

Move slow from a light V shape to a plank. Keep it smooth.

Connects arms, shoulders, hips, and core.
12 BJJ Skill

Technical Stand-Up

Sit down. Put one hand back. Cover your head with the other. Step back and stand.

Fundamental BJJ self-defense way to stand up safely.
Timing Guide

Before class vs after class

Before Class: Dynamic Movement

Use easy moves that warm your joints. Try hip circles, arm rolls, bodyweight squats, and safe technical standups. Keep your breath steady.

After Class: Calm Static Reset

Move slow while your muscles are warm. Rest in gentle forward folds or seated hip stretches. Let your heart rate return to baseline.

First class tip: Flexibility comes with gentle practice. We shape each class to match your current range of motion.
