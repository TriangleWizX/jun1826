# Pricing — Sensei Sandy BJJ

## Starting Point

* **Free Intro Class:** $0. Includes a room tour, safety walkthrough, Goal Mapping, and a coached learning experience for first-time local students.

## Core Culture — First 12-Week Term

Core Culture is one calm starting lane: three recurring home-class reservations per week, up to 36 scheduled classes, with classes capped at 12 students. Rescheduling is by text only when an appropriate open seat and partner fit are available. The academy gi is included with first-time Core Culture enrollment.

* **Youth (ages 5–17):** $550
* **Adult (ages 18+):** $715
* **Community-Service Adult:** $600 for teachers, healthcare workers, law enforcement, veterans, firefighters, EMS, and other first responders.

Core Culture includes a 30-day fit guarantee: attend at least four classes in the first 30 days and request within 30 days; first Core term only.

## After the First Term

Annual renewal is reviewed after the first term:

* **Youth:** $2,100
* **Adult:** $2,650
* **Community-Service Adult:** $2,250

Elite Concierge is a separate adult service at $1,050 per 12-week term and is not the normal first-term choice.

## Visitors

* **Day Pass:** $35
* **Vacation Week:** $99 for up to three classes in seven days
* **10-Class Pack:** $300, valid for six months
* **20-Class Pack:** $550, valid for 12 months

Capacity rules apply to every option; no plan overrides the 12-student class cap.

## Private Coaching

* **Price:** $195 per session
* **Schedule:** Generally morning hours, by request.
