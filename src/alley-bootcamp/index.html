Two Weekly Workouts

Tuesday Strength and Thursday Engine

Every class uses four outdoor stations beside the studio. Classes last forty minutes. Tuesday builds muscle and protects joints. Thursday builds lung power and mountain stamina.

Station 1

Bulgarian Bag

Leather bags with curved handles. You build core strength, shoulder health, and balance.

Station 2

Kettlebells

Solid cast iron bells. Sandy guides your lifts, hip drive, and flat back.

Station 3

Jump Rope

Smooth ropes for quick feet. You build strong calves, ankles, and trail rhythm.

Station 4

Sprint & Steps

19 wooden steps on four landings. You build strong climbing legs for mountain hills.

Tuesday Track

Strength & Joint Armor

60s work · 30s rest · 4 full rounds

Focus on steady lifts and joint health. You lift steady weights and rest between stations.

  • Bulgarian Bag: Lunges build strong legs and core balance.
  • Kettlebell: Deadlifts and squats build a strong back.
  • Jump Rope: Single-leg hops build quiet balance.
  • Sprint & Steps: Weighted step climbs build climbing power.

How you feel: Strong, tall, and calm.

Thursday Track

Engine & Stamina

40s work · 20s rest · 5 full rounds

Focus on heart rate and lung power. You move at a steady pace that burns energy.

  • Bulgarian Bag: Quick bag snatches build power.
  • Kettlebell: Smooth hip swings burn body fat.
  • Jump Rope: Fast boxer skips build quick feet.
  • Sprint & Steps: Quick steps build fast climbing legs.

How you feel: Light, fast, and full of energy.
