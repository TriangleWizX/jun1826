Two Weekly Workouts

Tuesday Strength and Thursday Engine

Every 40-minute class uses four outdoor stations beside the studio. Tuesday builds muscle and joint armor. Thursday expands your lung power and mountain stamina.

Station 1

Bulgarian Bag

Leather bags with curved horn handles. You build rotational core power, shoulder health, and balance.

Station 2

Kettlebells

Solid cast iron bells. Sandy coaches your hip drive, flat back, and leg power with kettlebell lifts.

Station 3

Jump Rope

Smooth ropes for quick feet. You strengthen your ankles, calves, and rhythm for hiking Catskills trails.

Station 4

Sprint & Steps

19 wooden steps across four alley landings. You build strong climbing legs and mountain stamina.

Tuesday Track

Strength & Joint Armor

60s work · 30s rest · 4 full rounds

Focus on controlled lifts, muscle tension, and joint health. You lift steady and rest between stations.

  • Bulgarian Bag: Rotational lunges and good mornings for core balance.
  • Kettlebell: Suitcase deadlifts and goblet squats with a deep pause.
  • Jump Rope: Single-leg balance hops and slow high-knee marches.
  • Sprint & Steps: Weighted step power climbs with kettlebell carries.

How you feel: Grounded, strong, and tall with calm physical confidence.

Thursday Track

Engine & Stamina

40s work · 20s rest · 5 full rounds

Focus on heart rate, continuous rhythm, and lung capacity. You move at a steady cadence that burns energy.

  • Bulgarian Bag: High-speed bag snatches and smooth rotational swings.
  • Kettlebell: Fluid two-handed hip swings for metabolic burn.
  • Jump Rope: Fast boxer skips and high-tempo footwork.
  • Sprint & Steps: High-speed alley sprints and quick stair ascents.

How you feel: Energized, light on your feet, and clear-headed all day.
