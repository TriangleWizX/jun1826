How a session flows

Warm up

Start with easy moves and stretches at the middle landing.

Try four stations

Use blocks, a balance ball, kettlebells, and a Bulgarian bag.

Move on the steps

Walk up 19 steps, do a set at the top, then walk back down.

Cool down

Finish with a foam roller, stretches, and slow breaths.

Why train in the alley

Eight spots, tops. Sandy sees every rep and sets each move to fit you.
The same coach each week. Sandy leads every session himself.
Fresh air and real steps. Four landings and 19 steps beside the studio.
Strong legs for ski season. Steps and carries build the legs you use at Hunter and Windham.
Gear is here. Kettlebells, a Bulgarian bag, a balance ball, and blocks wait at the studio.
Pairs with your BJJ week. Morning strength beside evening classes.
