Choose Your Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Program

Kids Jiu-Jitsu · Ages 5–9

Focus, confidence, and follow-through

Kids learn through age-appropriate movement, structured partner activities, clear boundaries, and positive coaching.

Parents can watch the process and receive useful progress observations. Classes help children practice listening, body awareness, communication, problem-solving, and staying composed when an activity becomes challenging.

Best for:

  • Children who need an active after-school routine
  • Beginners who benefit from clear structure
  • Families looking for confidence and healthy physical activity
  • Children learning to work safely with partners

Teen Jiu-Jitsu · Ages 10–17

Discipline, composure, and meaningful challenge

Teen classes provide a mature training path built around physical skill, problem-solving, respectful communication, and steady progress.

Students train in coach-led after-school classes. Each teen receives appropriate challenges, direct feedback, and opportunities to build confidence through real capability.

Best for:

  • Teens who want an engaging physical activity
  • Beginners who need patient coaching
  • Students developing confidence and self-control
  • Athletes looking for a new problem-solving sport

Adult Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu · Ages 18+

Strength, mobility, stress relief, and practical skill

Adult classes create a sustainable training practice for first-time beginners, returning martial artists, and experienced grapplers.

Students begin with controlled partner work and skill-based resistance activities. Coaching emphasizes movement, positional understanding, composure, and training methods that support long-term participation.

Best for:

  • Adults starting with no experience
  • People looking for a dependable fitness routine
  • Returning students rebuilding their skills
  • Adults who value technical, joint-aware training

Law Enforcement and First Responders

Tactical longevity for people who carry responsibility

Law-enforcement officers, corrections professionals, firefighters, EMS personnel, and veterans can train inside the regular adult program with responsibility-aware coaching.

Training develops balance, frames, escapes, pressure management, standing recovery, positional control, communication, and calm decision-making.

Best for:

  • Law-enforcement and corrections professionals
  • Firefighters and EMS personnel
  • Veterans
  • Departments evaluating private group training

More Ways to Train

Private Jiu-Jitsu Coaching

A premium alternative built around your pace

Private coaching provides focused one-on-one attention for beginners, current students, children, adults, and experienced grapplers.

Each session begins with a clear goal. Sandy then builds the class around your movement, questions, technical gaps, and desired result.

Choose private coaching when you want the fastest path to focused feedback, greater scheduling privacy, or a session designed entirely around you.

Catskills Home Base

Flexible training for visitors and seasonal families

Keep training while staying near Hunter, Windham, Tannersville, or the surrounding Catskills.

Catskills Home Base supports traveling grapplers, second-home families, seasonal residents, and repeat visitors with day passes, vacation-week access, and flexible class packs.

Birthday Parties

A private, active party led by the coach

Host a structured mat party where children move, play, laugh, and learn simple confidence-building skills.

Sandy leads the activities so parents can welcome guests, take photos, and enjoy the event without having to run the room.
