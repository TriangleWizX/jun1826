Instead of commuting off the mountain, our 6 PM adult classes and Saturday 10:30 AM Adult No-Gi provide a calm, technical environment to build fitness and skill right in town.

Parking on Main Street

Use Main Street spots first, nearby side streets if needed, and arrive 5–10 minutes early. Look for Studios on Main, and head upstairs to the second-floor training room.

Youth + Adult Recreation

Outdoor town. Indoor weekly discipline.

Around Tannersville and Hunter, families already build life around movement: skiing, snowboarding, hiking, golf, cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, lakes, trails, and school activities. BJJ gives kids, teens, and adults a repeatable indoor practice that works in every season.

Surrounding Ecosystem

We design classes to anchor your active lifestyle. Hunter Mountain has 320 skiable acres, 13 lifts, and 67 trails. Mountain Top Arboretum includes 200 acres of trails and meadows. Colonial Golf Course offers local bike trails and summer events. BJJ is your weekly anchor between them.

For kids

Confidence, body control, safe boundaries, and listening under pressure.

For teens

Calm self-defense, discipline, athletic identity, and structured challenge.

For adults

Fitness, stress relief, practical skill, and a reliable reason to move.
