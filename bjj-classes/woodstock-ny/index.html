The Woodstock-to-Tannersville weekly rhythm

Plan the drive around a class time that works.

The studio is roughly a 25-minute drive from Woodstock, depending on your starting point and road conditions. The best fit is the one you can repeat: after school, after work, or on a Saturday morning.

Arrival: Use the Free Intro to review the route, parking, and the second-floor studio entrance before you commit to a weekly rhythm.
