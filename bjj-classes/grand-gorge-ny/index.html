Why the Drive from Grand Gorge Works

A 35-minute drive east across the mountaintop to our four-season studio.

Grand Gorge sits right at the crossroads of Route 23 and 30, but finding a highly structured, calm athletic space often means heading east. For local families, the 35-minute drive across the mountaintop connects you to a consistent routine not found at the intersection. One local parent uses the drive back west as a dedicated time to talk with their teenager after class.

Weather & Arrival: During winter storms, we know the drive across the open stretches of Route 23 from the gorge requires care. We keep an eye on the weather and send text updates by 2:00 PM for evening classes so you can safely plan your trip east. When you arrive in Tannersville, you can usually find parking right on Main Street or in the municipal lot behind the theater. Walk up the wooden stairs to the second floor—you’ll see the mats right away.
