Sustainable Jiu-Jitsu Overview

Can I start Jiu-Jitsu with no experience?

Yes. We built this program for adult beginners. You start skill-based resistance activities at the right pace from day one. We start you with controlled partner work, maps of control, and movement before intensity increases.

How our curriculum supports joint-aware training and long-term participation.

We prioritize longevity over short-term intensity. We teach sustainable training methods like flow drilling and the Cali Roll sparring style. These emphasize positional awareness and controlled resistance. You build strength and mobility without unnecessary wear and tear on your body.

What Happens During Class?

Students do not spend class watching long demonstrations or repeating movements without resistance.

Each class progresses through:

  1. Movement connected to the day’s problem
  2. Chest-to-chest control and escapes
  3. Chest-to-back control and escapes
  4. A neutral guard game
  5. Coach-controlled resistance rounds
  6. A brief review

Sandy changes the partners, starting positions, scoring rules, and resistance level so beginners and experienced students can learn safely.

6. Adult Beginner FAQ

Adult Beginner FAQ

Can I start from my current fitness level?
Yes. Your first visit is paced so you can learn the room, safety rules, and basic movement comfortably.
What kind of training happens in the first small-group class?
A complete adult class usually gives students a safe look at the main Jiu-Jitsu situations: coached, beginner-appropriate partner activities. Sandy adjusts the position, partner, pace, and resistance.
What should adults wear to a first small-group class?
Wear clean athletic clothes you can move in, such as a fitted shirt and shorts or leggings without zippers. Bring a gi if you already have one. If you are starting fresh, come simple and we will guide you before class.
What happens after the Free Intro?
Most adults choose the 12-week Core Culture track after their first visit because it gives them structure, consistency, and a clear training rhythm.
Can I reschedule?
Yes. Text Sandy if plans change and we’ll help you find your next best class.
How do I ease into starting?
Start with the first-timer guide, then use the Free Intro to tour the room, learn safety basics, and choose the right small-group class lane.
