Sensei Sandy BJJ policy

30-Day Confidence Guarantee

Starting Jiu-Jitsu requires trust. Your first 12-Week Core Culture Program includes our 30-Day Confidence Guarantee.

Who qualifies

The guarantee applies to each student’s first Youth or Adult Core Culture term.

What you need to do

  1. Attend at least four semi-private classes during the first 30 calendar days of your program.
  2. Text Sandy within those first 30 calendar days if the program is not the right fit.
  3. Give us the opportunity to adjust your schedule, semi-private class lane, partner matching, or starting plan.

Your refund

If you still prefer to leave, we will refund the amount paid for your first Core Culture program term in full.

What is not included

The guarantee does not apply to renewed Core Culture terms, annual plans, visitor passes, vacation passes, semi-private class packs, private lessons, merchandise, or optional add-ons.
