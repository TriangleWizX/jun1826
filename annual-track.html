Transition Eligibility

To upgrade to the Annual Track, students must meet the following requirements:

  • Completed at least one 12-week Core Culture term
  • Account in good standing (no outstanding balances)
  • Safe training record and clean mat hygiene
  • Completed a schedule and progress review with Sandy
  • Home classes available and agreed upon

Terms and Limits

Please note the following operational policies for the Annual Track:

  • Open-Seat Rescheduling: Request replacement classes with space by text. Unrestricted attendance is not included.
  • Gis: Annual Track pricing does not include another academy gi (students use their Core Culture gi).
  • Medical-Pause: Up to two pauses, maximum 30 days total, per annual term.
  • Non-Transferable: Accounts cannot be shared or transferred.
