What the First Two Weeks Look Like

We eliminate the friction of starting something new. Here is how your family transitions into a comfortable after-school BJJ rhythm:

Visit 1

Room Tour & Gi Fitting

Tour the Tannersville studio, meet Sandy, fit your child for their academy gi, and review safe mat rules together.

Week 1

Beginner Lane Pacing

Your child is paired with a patient partner for fundamental movement, breakfalls, and basic positioning at their comfort level.

Week 2

Establishing the Rhythm

Recurring home-class days become a steady, anticipated highlight of your family’s weekly calendar.
