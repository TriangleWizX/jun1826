### **06:35 – 06:47: Phase A — Demian Maia Side-Control Armbar Detail**
*Source: Demian Maia & Bernardo Faria Transcript Breakdown*

- **The Problem**: When transitioning to an armbar from side control, explosive opponents bend their arm, pull down, and slip their elbow out during the leg step-over.
- **Demian Maia’s Micro-Adjustment**:
  1. **Biceps Grip & Forearm Closure**: Catch opponent's biceps and close your forearm firmly across their chest.
  2. **Hip-Bone Weight Transfer (The Game-Changer)**: Transfer ALL your weight off your knees directly onto opponent's hip bone and sprawl out with your legs. This immobilizes their hips and locks their arm completely in place.
  3. **Chin Control**: If opponent attempts to sit up or explode, place your free hand directly on their chin to push them back down to the mat.
  4. **Tight Knee Switch**: Slide your knee under their arm while keeping 100% of your weight glued low to their body throughout the transition. Never lift high or give up chest pressure.

---

### **06:47 – 07:00: Phase B — Declan Moody Lasso-Counter North-South Pass**
*Source: Declan Moody European Trials Transcript Breakdown*

- **The Problem**: When split-squatting or joint-pointing, opponent shoots a deep lasso guard and places their foot flat on your chest to push you away.
- **Declan Moody’s North-South Pass Mechanics**:
  1. **Blading the Body**: Do not stay square. Blade your torso to block the foot from stepping flat on your chest, using your chest angle to direct their foot across your body.
  2. **Lower-Ankle Scoop & Shin Block**: Scoop right at their lower ankle while blocking the top of their shin.
  3. **Fist Punch & Chest Opening**: Punch the hand that was on their ribs flat to the mat. Open your chest as you slide through.
  4. **Hip-Cross & Bicep Ride**: Drive your hip across to the far side, landing in North-South with a bicep ride at their hip post to clear knees and solidify the pass.
  5. **Back-Step Variation (Low Leg Hook Defense)**: If opponent maintains a low hip hook, take a synchronized back-step while blading your body to persist with the North-South passing angle.
