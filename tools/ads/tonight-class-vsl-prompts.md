# SSBJJ Evening Studio Video Prompts: Class Member VSL & Testimonial Guide

**Setting:** Sensei Sandy BJJ Studio (6045 Main St, 2nd Floor, Tannersville, NY)  
**Goal:** Capture 15–30 second genuine, unscripted video soundbites from class attendees before, during, or after evening class for Video Sales Letters (VSLs), Meta ads, and organic social reels.

---

## 🎬 Director's Fast Rules (How to get real answers in 2 minutes)

1. **Don't ask them to "give a testimonial":** People freeze up or sound robotic. Instead, ask them about their **past fears** versus their **actual experience**.
2. **Tell them to repeat the question in their answer:** 
   - *Weak:* "It was great."
   - *Strong:* "Before my first class, I was terrified I'd get hurt or look stupid. But the second I stepped on the mats..."
3. **Studio Framing:**
   - Stand on the clean training mats with the yellow/black wall logo or the open room in the background.
   - Keep the camera at eye level, 2–3 feet away for crisp phone audio.
   - Capture them in their gi or training rashguard while their energy is still up right after cooldown.

---

## 1. 🧔 For Adult Beginners (Beginner Ben Angle)
*Targeting adults (25–50) who have wanted to try martial arts for years but feared being out of shape, getting injured, or walking into a meathead gym.*

### Prompt 1: The Hesitation
> **Ask:** *"What almost stopped you from coming in for your first class?"*  
> **What you're listening for:** Fears about being out of shape, age, past injuries, or intimidation.

### Prompt 2: The First Class Reality Check
> **Ask:** *"What actually happened during your first class with Sandy?"*  
> **What you're listening for:** How calm it was, the coached walkthrough, partner control, no live sparring on day one.

### Prompt 3: The Message to Hesitant Neighbors
> **Ask:** *"What would you say to someone in Tannersville, Hunter, or Windham who is sitting on their couch thinking they need to get in shape before starting?"*  
> **Ideal Soundbite:** *"Don't wait to get in shape. You get in shape by starting. Everyone in this room started in the exact same spot."*

---

## 2. 👩👧👦 For Parents (Coach-Mom Carla Angle)
*Targeting moms and dads looking for a safe, structured, screen-free activity that builds real confidence without aggression.*

### Prompt 1: The Parent Concern
> **Ask:** *"Before you brought your child here, what was your biggest worry about martial arts or jiu-jitsu?"*  
> **What you're listening for:** Aggression, getting hurt, chaos, feeling out of place.

### Prompt 2: The Kid's Transformation (Independent Ian)
> **Ask:** *"How does your child feel about coming to class, and what differences have you noticed at home or school?"*  
> **What you're listening for:** Eagerness to attend, better focus, physical confidence, body awareness, safe falling.

### Prompt 3: Community & Environment
> **Ask:** *"How would you describe the atmosphere and Sandy’s coaching style for families?"*  
> **What you're listening for:** Calm, patient, small groups, intentional partner pairing, safe and friendly.

---

## 3. 🧑🎓 For Teens & High School Athletes (Cross-Training Tyler Angle)
*Targeting teenagers who play other sports (soccer, wrestling, skiing, football) or want physical self-defense competence.*

### Prompt 1: Athlete Cross-Training
> **Ask:** *"How does training jiu-jitsu here help you in your other sports or your day-to-day life?"*  
> **What you're listening for:** Balance, grip strength, body control, staying calm under pressure.

### Prompt 2: The Culture Check
> **Ask:** *"What's the vibe like here compared to school sports or other gyms?"*  
> **What you're listening for:** No egos, coaches actually teach, you learn real problem-solving rather than just doing drills in lines.

---

## 4. 👩🏫 For Public Service & Community Adults (Community Casey Angle)
*Targeting local teachers, nurses, EMTs, firefighters, police, and hospitality workers.*

### Prompt 1: Stress Relief & Decompression
> **Ask:** *"After a long shift or a stressful week, what does coming onto these mats do for your head?"*  
> **What you're listening for:** "I can't think about work when I'm on the mats," stress reset, physical durability, great local people.

## 5. 🥋 Sandy’s On-Camera Anchor Lines (Coaching Bridges)
*Film Sandy delivering these 10-second pieces straight to camera while students train or stretch in the background:*

### Hook A (The Beginner Guarantee):
> *"If you've never stepped on a martial arts mat in your life, you are our favorite student to teach. Your first class isn't a test. It's a coached tour and learning experience."*

### Hook B (The Outcome vs. Jargon):
> *"We don't do choreographed line routines or ego-driven sparring. We play structured games where kids and adults learn to solve real physical problems with safe partners."*

### Hook C (The Invitation):
> *"Text me or tap the link below. Come visit the studio, meet the team, and take your free intro class. Start calm. Train smart."*
