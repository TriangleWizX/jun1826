### Block 2 (06:35 – 06:50): Game 1 — North-South Frame & Pass Exploration
- **Task Constraint (Top Player / Sandy)**: Pass to North-South and pin both shoulders flat to the mat for 5 continuous seconds.
- **Task Constraint (Bottom Player / Javan)**: Keep both hands framed on top player’s hip or neck; escape by getting to hip-switch/guard within 30 seconds.
- **Rules / Restrictions**: Top player cannot use brute force or lift bottom player off the mat; must use body alignment and angle shifts.
- **Coaching Cues (Affordance Awareness)**:
  * *"Feel where Javan's weight is shifted—if his frame pushes left, step right."*
  * *"Don't memorize a pass; look for the space his elbow opens."*

---

### Block 3 (06:50 – 07:05): Game 2 — The Armlock Isolation Game
- **Task Constraint (Attacker)**: Isolate one arm and secure an elbow tap or chest-level control without losing top position.
- **Task Constraint (Defender)**: Keep hands connected (gable grip / rear-clasp) and turn chest toward attacker to clear elbow space.
- **Constraints Manipulation**:
  * *Level 1*: Defender starts with hands locked; attacker must break connection using body posture, not arm pulling.
  * *Level 2*: Attacker is forbidden from leaning backward; must maintain chest-to-chest contact throughout the transition.
- **Coaching Cues**:
  * *"Jiu-Jitsu is affordance perception—find where his elbow is unsupported."*

---

### Block 4 (07:05 – 07:20): Progressive Task Sparring (Live CLA Flow)
- **Game 3: North-South to Armlock Constraint Match**
  * 3x 3-minute rounds.
  * *Win Condition (Top)*: Advance from Side Control → North-South → Armlock isolation.
  * *Win Condition (Bottom)*: Re-guard or recover underhook before arm isolation occurs.
