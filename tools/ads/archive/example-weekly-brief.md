## Retention review
- Check missed expected sessions against closures and known absences.
- Ask what changed; record a reason without medical or personal details in creative files.
- Choose one operational fix before creating a promotion.

## Experiment decision
- Keep a productive control. Change one message variable for the same buyer and offer.
- Record dates, spend, booked visits, completed visits, enrollments, and attribution limits.
- Separate paid acquisition, organic engagement, visitor attendance, and returning-member outcomes.
- With little conversion evidence, mark the result inconclusive; do not declare a winner from likes.
