## Platform playbooks

Use the vertical video source ideas:

1. Pre-ski balance and hip-position drill.
2. Rear-naked-choke demonstration.
3. Closed-guard breakdown.
4. Visitor invitation for Saturday No-Gi, only after availability check.
5. Calm angle-step technical loop.

Do not upload static Instagram ads as ordinary videos. Turn them into an honest photo-mode slideshow or use them on the platforms where static posts are native.
