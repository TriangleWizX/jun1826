First visit walkthrough

What happens at your Free Intro

Starting martial arts shouldn't mean being thrown into the deep end. We guide every new teen through three calm, structured steps.

Step 1

Tour & Meet Coach Sandy

Arrive in athletic clothes. We introduce you to the space, answer any questions, and walk through the layout before class starts.

Step 2

Safety Rules & Matched Partner

Learn how to tap, protect your neck and joints, and practice with a calm, experienced partner who matches your pace.

Step 3

Coached Positional Practice

Test simple answers against controlled resistance. No aggressive sparring, no ego battles. Coached problem solving from day one.

Questions parents & teens ask

Before the first class

Does a teen need prior experience?

No. Every student starts with fundamental movement, safe falling, and matched partner pacing.

Is this striking or fighting?

No punches or kicks. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is 100% submission grappling, leverage, and positional control.

Can parents watch?

Yes. The studio is open and parents are welcome to observe.
