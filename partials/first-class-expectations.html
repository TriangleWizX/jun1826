Day one

Your semi-private class is guided from the first minute.

You do not have to know what to wear, where to stand, or how Jiu-Jitsu works. A complete semi-private class usually gives students a safe look at the main Jiu-Jitsu situations: a neutral exchange where one partner's hips are grounded and the other's are elevated, chest-to-chest control, chest-to-back control, and standing engagement when skill level makes it appropriate.

Sandy adjusts partners, resistance, and pace for youth semi-private classes, adult semi-private classes, and private lessons.
