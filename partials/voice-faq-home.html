Questions people ask before booking

Is Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu safe for beginners?

Yes. Training starts with a studio tour, a safety walkthrough, and clear rules. Every drill is coach-led with carefully matched partners, controlled movement, and mutual tapping so you set your own pace from day one.

Do I need to be physically fit before starting?

You can start at any fitness level. Training is how you build stamina, strength, and mobility. Classes begin with calm, coach-guided pacing, and Coach Sandy adjusts each activity to fit your current comfort.

At what age can children start BJJ?

Children can start at age 5. Our Youth classes welcome kids ages 5 to 9, and Teen classes serve ages 10 to 17. Younger children learn through active movement games that build balance, focus, and body control.

Where can I take BJJ classes near Hunter or Tannersville?

Sensei Sandy BJJ is located at 6045 Main Street (2nd Floor Studio) in Tannersville, NY, just 4 miles from Hunter and easily accessible from Windham, Haines Falls, and surrounding Catskills towns.

Yes. You can coordinate makeups or change your class times by sending a text directly to Sandy at +1 (917) 736-8649.
