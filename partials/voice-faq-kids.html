Questions parents ask before booking

Can my child start if they are shy or nervous?

Yes. We pace the first semi-private class slowly and keep the room calm and structured.

Is this striking or grappling?

Grappling only. No punches or kicks in the kids lane.

Can parents watch semi-private class?

Yes. Parents can watch and see exactly how semi-private class is run.

What semi-private class do most kids try first?

Most kids start in the Youth Semi-Private Class. We group partners by size, comfort, and experience.
