First visit walkthrough

How adults start on the mats

Starting as an adult beginner is intimidating when you don't know what to expect. Here is how your first visit works:

Step 1

15-Min Consultation & Tour

Come in normal clothes. Meet Coach Sandy, view the studio, and talk through any previous injuries, physical goals, or concerns with zero pressure.

Step 2

Thoughtfully Matched Partner

You won't be thrown to wolves or aggressive sparrers. You are paired with a calm, seasoned partner whose job is to keep you safe and help you learn.

Step 3

Coached Positional Grappling

Practice fundamental escapes and positional control with controlled resistance. Coach Sandy is right there to guide and answer questions.
