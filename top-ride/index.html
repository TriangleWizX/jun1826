Top Ride

A top ride is a style of top control where the top player follows, steers, and stays attached to a partner who is trying to move, rise, or scramble free.

Intermediate concept Positions Turtle Mat Return Chest to Back

Quick definition

A top ride is a style of top control where the top player follows, steers, and stays attached to a partner who is trying to move, rise, or scramble free.

Beginner translation

Top ride means staying on top of someone in a way that tracks their movement instead of letting them get away clean.

Why it matters

A top ride is a style of top control where the top player follows, steers, and stays attached to a partner who is trying to move, rise, or scramble free.

What beginners should know

A common mistake with top ride is using it passively instead of with structure and timing.

Common semi-private class phrases

Beginner Safety Cue

Safety cue: Move with control and communicate early while training top ride.

FAQ

How is a top ride different from a static pin?

A top ride emphasizes following movement rather than freezing one position.

Where do top rides appear most?

Turtle exchanges, stand-up attempts, and scramble-heavy transitions.
