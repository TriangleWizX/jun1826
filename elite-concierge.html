Best Fit

  • Adult beginners who want gear handling and laundry services included
  • Students looking for priority scheduling and replacement class seats

Elite Concierge is an adults-only program. Youth students are not eligible.

Not A Fit

  • Youth or teen students (adults only)

Operational Limits

Clear Service Policies

Elite Concierge students receive first consideration when a suitable replacement seat opens. Sandy will still enforce the class cap and placement requirements.

  1. Planned schedule
    Covers three planned classes per week. It does not guarantee extra classes or a replacement seat.
  2. laundry limits
    Covers one bag and three training sets per week. Excludes same-day service, personal clothing, and home delivery.
  3. Gear storage
    Storage remains assigned, and loaner equipment depends on availability. Unclaimed gear moved to lost and found after 30 days.
