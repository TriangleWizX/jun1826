Gi

Foundational concept Gi / No-Gi Common No-Gi Guard Tap

Quick definition

A gi is the traditional Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu uniform, with a jacket, pants, and belt that can all be used for grips.

Beginner translation

It is the training uniform that slows things down a little and gives both people more handles to hold.

Why it matters

Beginners often wonder whether they need special knowledge before trying gi class. They do not. Understanding the uniform reduces that friction.

What beginners should know

A common mistake with gi is using it passively instead of with structure and timing.
