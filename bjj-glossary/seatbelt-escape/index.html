Seatbelt Escape

A seatbelt escape is the process of defending and loosening the seatbelt grip from back control so you can turn, hide the choking side, and improve your position.

Intermediate concept Movements Seatbelt Back Defense Back Control

Quick definition

A seatbelt escape is the process of defending and loosening the seatbelt grip from back control so you can turn, hide the choking side, and improve your position.

Beginner translation

Seatbelt escape means breaking down the grip that helps your partner stay attached to your back and attack your neck.

Why it matters

A seatbelt escape is the process of defending and loosening the seatbelt grip from back control so you can turn, hide the choking side, and improve your position.

What beginners should know

A common mistake with seatbelt escape is using it passively instead of with structure and timing.

Common semi-private class phrases

  • Seatbelt escape connects to seatbelt
  • back defense

Beginner Safety Cue

Safety cue: Move with control and communicate early while training seatbelt escape.

FAQ

What is the first priority in seatbelt escape?

Protect the neck and manage the choking-side hand before bigger movement.

Can explosive turning alone solve it?

Usually no. Without hand fighting and positioning, explosive movement makes control worse.
