Wrestle Up

Wrestle up means rising from guard or a lower position into a takedown, top control, or standing attack instead of staying underneath.

Foundational concept Movements Common Technical Stand Up Single Leg Sweep

Quick definition

Wrestle up means rising from guard or a lower position into a takedown, top control, or standing attack instead of staying underneath.

Beginner translation

Wrestle up means using the moment to come up and attack instead of accepting bottom position.

Why it matters

Wrestle up means rising from guard or a lower position into a takedown, top control, or standing attack instead of staying underneath.

What beginners should know

A common mistake is trying to stand with no control first. Good wrestle-ups usually start after off-balancing, gripping, or forcing a reaction.

Common semi-private class phrases

  • Wrestle up connects to technical stand up
  • single leg

  • and base

Beginner Safety Cue

Safety cue: Move with control and communicate early while training wrestle up.

FAQ

Can you wrestle up without controlling first?

You can try, but it is lower percentage. Off-balancing and grips usually make the entry cleaner.

What does wrestle up connect most to?

Technical stand up, single-leg attacks, sweeps, and scramble transitions.
