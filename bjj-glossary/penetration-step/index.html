Penetration Step

A penetration step is the lead step that drives a shot entry forward under control to connect to legs or hips with better angle and balance.

Foundational concept Movements Common Shot Entry Level Change Double Leg

Quick definition

A penetration step is the lead step that drives a shot entry forward under control to connect to legs or hips with better angle and balance.

Beginner translation

Penetration step is the key step that gets your body into the takedown safely.

Why it matters

A penetration step is the lead step that drives a shot entry forward under control to connect to legs or hips with better angle and balance.

What beginners should know

A common mistake with penetration step is using it passively instead of with structure and timing.

Beginner Safety Cue

Shot Entry

A shot entry is the movement used to enter safely and efficiently into a takedown attempt, usually by lowering level, closing distance, and connecting to the legs or hips.

Level Change

Level change is lowering your base and body height with control so you can enter takedowns safely and efficiently.

Read more →

Double Leg

A double leg is a takedown where you attack both of your partner's legs to drive, turn, or lift them into a grounded position.

Read more →

Single Leg

A single leg is a takedown where you attack and control one of your partner's legs to bring them to the mat or force them off balance.

Head Position

Head position means where you place your head relative to your partner and how that placement affects balance, pressure, posture, and control.

FAQ

What does a good penetration step do?

It carries your body into contact with posture, angle, and balance.

Why do beginners struggle with penetration step?

They overreach or lose posture instead of stepping with control.
