Eligibility Criteria

Who Qualifies for the Pilot

To ensure class quality and fair seat access for local families, applicants must meet the following criteria:

    First-Time Student: Has not previously enrolled or taken classes at Sensei Sandy BJJ.
    Primary Local Resident: Primary resident of Tannersville, Hunter, Windham, Haines Falls, or nearby Mountaintop communities.
    Age 5 or Older: Available for Kids (5-9), Teens (10-17), or Adults (18+).
    10-Day Attendance Window: Able to attend three reserved classes within ten days of the first class.
    Prerequisite Steps: Willing to complete Goal Mapping and the standard safety waiver first.
