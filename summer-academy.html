Give Them Two Active Mornings to Look Forward to Each Week

The Summer Academy gives children a reliable rhythm without filling every weekday. They move, solve problems, work with partners and learn skills that require attention, patience and controlled effort.

Confidence

Children experience manageable challenges and practice continuing when an answer is not immediate.

Coordination

Movement games, safe falling and partner activities develop balance, timing and body awareness.

Self-Control

Children learn to manage their pace, listen for stop signals and protect their training partners.

Friendship

A small recurring group gives children time to become comfortable, cooperate and feel known.

Our goal is to help each child become more capable, calm and confident through purposeful play and skill-based jiu-jitsu.

Is This a Good Fit for My Child?

The Summer Academy may fit a child who:

  • Needs a constructive summer routine
  • Enjoys learning through movement and games
  • Would benefit from a smaller group
  • Wants to try martial arts without competition pressure
  • Needs practice with focus or confidence
  • Likes solving physical problems
  • Has never trained jiu-jitsu

Parents should know:

  • Activities include supervised physical contact.
  • Children must follow stop signals and partner-safety instructions.
  • The program is not all-day childcare.
  • The academy uses games, but each game has a teaching objective.
  • Accommodations should be discussed before enrollment.

A Clear Rhythm for Every Morning

9:00 — Arrival and Readiness

Belongings, water, bathroom and a brief emotional check-in.

9:10 — Movement Game

Running, crawling, balancing, reacting and coordinating.

9:30 — Jiu-Jitsu Class

One clear problem and one or two practical solutions.

10:05 — Snack and Reset

A supervised break for water, food and regrouping.

10:20 — Partner Resistance Games

Short rounds with a specific goal, clear limits and active coaching.

10:55 — Team Challenge

Communication, leadership or structured problem-solving.

11:20 — Cooldown and Progress Passport

Children reflect on what they practiced and record progress.

11:30 — Authorized Pickup

Children spend more time moving and solving problems than listening to long explanations.

Six Weeks of Real, Age-Appropriate Jiu-Jitsu

This is not a collection of unrelated activities. Each week builds on the movement, safety and partner habits introduced before it.

Week 1 — Ready Body

Safe falling, mat movement, personal space and partner habits.

Week 2 — Balance and Takedowns

Stance, posture, push-pull balance and controlled takedown games.

Week 3 — Escapes and Boundaries

Creating space, standing safely, speaking clearly and finding help.

Week 4 — Guard and Problem-Solving

Frames, distance, sweeping games and staying calm while stuck.

Week 5 — Top Control and Leadership

Safe pressure, positional control, partner coaching and leadership.

Week 6 — Submission Safety and Challenge Week

Tapping early, releasing immediately, team challenges and the family showcase.
