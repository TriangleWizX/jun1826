How does a class work?

Each reservation occupies a 60-minute schedule window with approximately 45 minutes of structured, coach-led training. Classes progress through movement mastery, chest-to-chest and chest-to-back positional games, a neutral guard game, resistance rounds, and a brief review.

See the full minute-by-minute class structure

What happens at my first small-group class?

You will arrive, meet your coach, and learn basic concepts and strategies of the gentle art. Beginners start with safe, coached, beginner-appropriate partner activities where Sandy adjusts the position, partner, pace, and resistance.

  • We explain the room before training starts.
  • You can watch first, ask questions, and step in when you feel ready.
  • You can text Sandy before arrival with any concern.
What should I wear?

Wear clean athletic clothes without zippers or pockets. A fitted shirt and shorts or leggings work well. We help with uniform choices after your first visit.

Do I need experience?

No. The room is built for beginners. You will learn positions, movement, tapping, and partner safety before intensity increases.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes, because we structure the room. We use supervised rounds, clear rules, tapping, partner matching, and coach-controlled intensity.

What ages are kids classes?

Kids classes serve ages 5-9. Teen classes serve ages 10-17. Adult classes start at 18+. Placement may vary based on size, maturity, experience, and family schedule.

Will this help with bullying?

We teach posture, boundaries, escape skills, calm communication, and confidence under pressure. The goal is not aggression. The goal is self-control. Read our bullying and self-defense FAQ for details.

Can parents watch?

Yes, where space allows. Parents can watch from the lounge area while we keep the training space calm and focused.

Will BJJ make my teen more aggressive?

No. Done correctly, Jiu-Jitsu teaches restraint, respect, consequences, and self-regulation. Teens learn how to stay calm instead of escalate.

What ages are teens classes?

Teens classes serve ages 10-17. Placement may vary based on size, maturity, experience, and family schedule.

How does teen training build skill safely?

Designed for beginners. Teens start with structured classes, cooperative partner drills, and a focus on control rather than aggression. No prior martial arts experience needed.

Can teen athletes train during their sports season?

Yes. Start with a manageable schedule and adjust intensity around practices, games, and recovery.

Do I have to spar?

No. We focus on the gentle art of control and leverage. Beginners build skill through controlled drills, positional games, and safe partner work, free of big egos.

What is Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu?

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is grappling based on control, escapes, and submissions. There is grappling-only training.

Can I start at my current level?

Yes. You start calmly, learn the room, and build fitness through consistent training.

Do you offer private coaching?

Yes. Private classes are useful for confidence, specific problems, injury-sensitive training, or faster beginner onboarding.

How do you keep beginners safe?

We prioritize safety so smaller grapplers and first-timers feel safe and welcomed. We use partner matching, dedicated beginner lanes, clear stop signals, tapping culture, and coach-controlled intensity.

What does tapping mean?

Tapping is the safety signal. It means pause immediately. We teach it early so students feel safe asking for a reset.

Is there striking?

No. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu at Sensei Sandy BJJ is grappling, positioning, control, and escapes.
