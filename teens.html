6. Teen Beginner FAQ

Do I need any experience?

No. Most teens start with zero experience. Sandy introduces safety rules, partner care, neutral work, chest-to-chest control, chest-to-back control, and standing engagement when skill level makes it appropriate.

Is it safe?

Yes. Sandy coaches grappling-only training with clear boundaries, partner matching, chest-to-chest control, chest-to-back control, and standing engagement when skill level makes it appropriate.

What should I bring?

Just comfortable athletic clothes (like shorts and a t-shirt). We provide a clean loaner uniform (Gi) for the Free Intro. You do not need special gear to start.

What if my teen needs a calm start or misses a semi-private class?

Consistent days build the best progress, but families can reschedule without stress. Just send Sandy a text to let him know, and we'll find a makeup time that works. Sandy will walk them through the room and let them go at their own pace.

Can parents watch?

Yes. Parents are always welcome to stay, sit on the sideline, watch the entire semi-private class, and talk with Sandy after training.

How do I get started?

The best first step is to book a Free Intro. We'll show you around the room, walk through the safety basics, and help your teen choose the next step.
