Fair Starting Positions

Rounds start from connected, fair positions so kids work right away without chasing, stalling, or rough mismatches. The goal is skill development and growth, not winning a trophy.

Starting examples may include:
  • Shoulder to shoulder on the knees
  • Seated guard connection
  • Double octopus guard
  • Other coach-selected positions

Coaches review age, weight, experience, size, attributes, temperament, and comfort level before building matchups. Every pairing is chosen with care.

Most kids get 2 to 3 coached game rounds. The exact number depends on attendance, pairings, age groups, and safety.

Rounds last 2 to 3 minutes. The goal is short, focused work that keeps kids moving, learning, and resetting well. The whole class runs about one hour in and out. Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes early (4:45 PM to 4:55 PM) so check-in is calm.
