What Happens

In smaller mountain towns, most dedicated Brazilian Jiu Jitsu programs are centralized. For Windham families, that location is Tannersville.

Students from Windham, Hunter, and Haines Falls train together in one structured program rather than fragmented small groups.

This creates:

  • More consistent training partners
  • Clear progression for beginners
  • Better coaching structure
  • Reliable class times

How Far Is Training From Windham?

Tannersville is typically about a 10-minute drive from Windham, depending on your starting point.

For most students, that short drive is what makes consistent training possible instead of waiting for limited or inconsistent options.

What Classes Look Like

Small-group classes are organized by age and level so you know exactly where to start:

  • Kids: Youth Class, 5:00–6:00 PM
  • Teens: Youth + Teen Class, 5:00–6:00 PM
  • Adults: 6:00–7:00 PM

Beginner Friendly Matters More Than Location

Most people don’t quit because of distance. They quit because the first experience is overwhelming.

A good first class should include:

  • A quick tour of the space
  • A safety walkthrough
  • A clear first class
  • Train at your own pace

This is often called a “Beginner Lane” approach. It allows new students to build confidence before intensity increases. Browse the beginner BJJ glossary.
