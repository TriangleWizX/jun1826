# DIY AI SEO Tracking Template — Sensei Sandy BJJ

Use this template to manually track your website's visibility and citations in AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot) month-over-month.

---

## 1. How to Use This Template

1.  **Select Your Queries:** Track 10–20 search queries that matter most to your business (e.g. "kids martial arts Tannersville", "beginner jiu jitsu Catskills").
2.  **Run Monthly Audits:** Open a clean browser window (ideally incognito/private mode to avoid personalized bias) and query each AI engine.
3.  **Log the Results:** Fill in the tracking table below. Note whether you were cited, which URL was cited, and which competitors were mentioned.
4.  **Identify Action Items:** If a competitor is cited instead of you, analyze their page structure and update your content structure or schema markup to match.

## 4. Key AI SEO Factors to Remember

*   **Google AI Overviews:** Strong correlation with Google Maps/Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization and reviews. Keep GBP active.
*   **ChatGPT & Perplexity:** Highly sensitive to structural formatting. Direct 40-60 word answer blocks, FAQ schema, and markdown lists are rewarded.
*   **Claude:** Brave Search-based. Ensure your pages are indexable and correct on `search.brave.com`.
*   **Freshness:** Re-update key pages (like schedule and pricing) at least quarterly and display the `Last Updated` date to trigger ChatGPT recency bonuses.
