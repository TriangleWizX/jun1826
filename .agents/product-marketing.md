### Outcome vs. Pedagogy Rule
Never sell the underlying pedagogy ("constraints-led ecological dynamics", "differential learning") in top-of-funnel messaging. That is how the coaching is delivered. Always sell the outcome the avatar desires:
- **Parent (Carla)**: "I want my kid to become more confident, disciplined, and capable without aggression."
- **Child (Ian)**: "You'll play games, wrestle, escape, attack, and figure things out."
- **Adult (Ben)**: "You don't need to get in shape before starting. Small classes. Controlled resistance. Beginner-friendly partners."
- **Teen (Tyler)**: "Another sport doesn't have to replace your sport. Become harder to control."
- **Service Adult (Casey)**: "Train with the people who keep the Catskills running."
- **Visitor (Wendy)**: "Visiting the Catskills? Keep training."
- **Family (Frankie)**: "Something your family can learn together."

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Traditional BJJ and martial arts schools feel intimidating, chaotic, and high-ego. Large class sizes and aggressive rolling on day one lead to injuries and early dropout.
**Why alternatives fall short:** Traditional gyms throw beginners into live sparring on day one, lack structured beginner pacing, or enforce rigid attendance rules.
**What it costs them:** Time, money, injuries, and lost confidence to maintain a fitness routine.
**Emotional tension:** Stress, intimidation, fear of injury, and feeling out of shape.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Traditional BJJ and MMA schools (down the mountain, e.g., in Kingston or Catskill). They prioritize competitive sparring over beginner safety, lack class caps, and foster a high-ego culture.
**Secondary:** Generic martial arts (Taekwondo, Karate) or fitness gyms. They focus on forms/striking rather than practical grappling self-defense, or lack coached group structure.
**Indirect:** Home workouts, apps, or physical inactivity. These options lack hands-on coaching, accountability, and partner training.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Small-group classes capped at 12 students.
- Dedicated "Beginner Lane" structure.
- Coached learning experience (Goal Mapping, tour, coached first class).
- Open-seat rescheduling by text.
**How we do it differently:** We focus on calm, guided coaching. Your first class is a coached learning experience, avoiding live sparring.
**Why that's better:** This structure reduces injury rates, lowers entry friction, welcomes new students, and fits busy schedules with text-based coordination.
**Why customers choose us:** Safety, high-quality instruction (Sandy earned a black belt under Josh Griffiths), calm environment, and convenience on Tannersville Main Street.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| **"I'm not in shape or have no experience"** | We welcome beginners of all fitness levels without past experience. Classes use coach-led instruction, small groups, and skill-based resistance activities that begin at the right pace from day one. |
| **"I'm worried about getting hurt"** | We start with a room tour and safety walkthrough. The Beginner Lane ensures you train at the right pace with matched partners, focused on control, not striking. |
| **"My schedule is too busy / unpredictable"** | We make rescheduling simple. You can coordinate makeups or schedule changes by text message. |

**Anti-persona:** Someone seeking high-intensity cage fighting, aggressive MMA sparring from day one, or a competitive fight-camp atmosphere.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with high-ego martial arts gyms, crowded classes, or boring fitness routines.
**Pull:** Small-group attention, calm pacing, clean facility on Tannersville Main Street, and clear onboarding path (Free Intro and Goal Mapping).
**Habit:** Physical inactivity or staying with ineffective home workouts.
**Anxiety:** Fear of injury, looking foolish, or committing to long-term plans before testing the experience.
