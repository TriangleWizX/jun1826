## Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Map

### Segment 1: Parents (Kids and Teens)
- **Functional Job:** Enroll children in structured, safe, screen-free physical activities.
- **Emotional Job:** Gain peace of mind that their child learns self-defense in a calm environment.
- **Social Job:** Demonstrate family commitment to active development and local community values.

### Segment 2: Adult Beginners
- **Functional Job:** Restore physical fitness, learn self-defense, and relieve stress.
- **Emotional Job:** Build confidence in physical capabilities and reduce work tension.
- **Social Job:** Train in a local group free of ego and aggressive posturing.
