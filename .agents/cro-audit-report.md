# CRO & Copy Audit Report

*Last updated: 2026-07-12*

This report audits the primary pages of `https://senseisandy.com` against the customer research insights mined from reviews, success stories, and class transcripts.

---

## 1. Homepage (index.html)

### Assessment
The current hero section focuses on calm, small-group classes. However, it misses key student vocabulary.

### Quick Wins (Implement Now)
- Add "the gentle art" to the hero small-print text: "Your first class is a coached, beginner-friendly introduction to the gentle art."
- Mention "no big egos" in the community section to target beginner intimidation.

### High-Impact Changes (Prioritize)
- Frame the introductory experience around learning "concepts and strategies" instead of pure movements. This sets clear expectations for nervous visitors.

### Copy Alternatives
- **Alternative 1 (Concept-focused):** "Calm, Coach-Led Jiu-Jitsu: Learn Concepts and Strategies at Your Own Pace" (Focuses on structured safety).
- **Alternative 2 (Beginner-focused):** "Learn the Gentle Art: Small-Group Classes Capped at 12, with Zero Ego" (Focuses on community comfort).
