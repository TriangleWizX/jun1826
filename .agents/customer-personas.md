# Customer Personas

*Last updated: 2026-07-12*

These profiles represent the core members at Sensei Sandy BJJ, constructed from mined customer reviews and transcripts.

---

## 1. Mountaintop Parent (Kids and Teens)

**Profile**
- Target audience: Parents of children aged 5 to 17.
- Location: Tannersville, Hunter, Windham, Haines Falls, and surrounding Catskills towns.
- Primary goal: Build screen-free physical routine and focus.

**Primary Job to Be Done**
Enroll children in structured, safe, after-school activities that develop confidence and behavior patterns.

**Trigger Events**
- Child spends too much time on screens.
- Child needs school focus improvement.
- Parent seeks a local sport option that fits busy schedules.

**Top Pains**
- Fear of aggressive martial arts environments (striking, kicks, bullying).
- Inflexible schedule policies in local sports.
- Lack of clear focus development in standard gym classes.

**Desired Outcomes**
- Child looks forward to going after school.
- Focus at home and school improves.
- Safe, non-striking grappling classes fit the family routine.

**Objections and Fears**
- "Will my child get hurt?" (Answered by safety-first room tours and partner matching).
- "What if we miss a class?" (Answered by text message rescheduling).

**Key Vocabulary**
- "Family routine"
- "Focus at home and school"
- "After school"

## 2. Adult First-Timer (Later-in-Life Practitioner)

**Profile**
- Target audience: Adults aged 25 to 55 (often starting BJJ at age 40 or older).
- Location: Local Mountaintop residents and weekend visitors.
- Primary goal: Restore fitness, relieve stress, and learn self-defense.

**Primary Job to Be Done**
Reclaim mobility, improve mental health, and learn grappling fundamentals in a welcoming environment.

**Trigger Events**
- Stress levels from work rise.
- Standard fitness gym routines feel boring.
- Individual wants to learn self-defense but fears injury in high-ego sparring classes.

**Top Pains**
- Intimidation when walking into traditional fight gyms.
- Fear of joint injuries or rib injuries during sparring.
- Inability to follow complex, fast-paced techniques without clear fundamental breakdowns.

**Desired Outcomes**
- Walk away from lessons with skills under the belt.
- Improve body and mind coordination.
- Feel comfortable on the mats from day one.

**Objections and Fears**
- "Am I too old or out of shape?" (Answered by the Beginner Lane pacing).
- "Will I feel out of place?" (Answered by the small class size capped at 12).

**Key Vocabulary**
- "Gentle art"
- "Helped my body and my mind"
- "No big egos"
- "Skills under my belt"
