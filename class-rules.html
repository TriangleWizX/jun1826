Five Safety Rules

Youth students use these steps when someone threatens or attacks them. Leave when you can. Find a trusted adult.

1

Avoid the Fight

Leave before a fight starts. Create space, move to a safe place, and find an adult.

2

Protect Yourself and Leave

If someone attacks you, cover your head and keep your balance. Move to safety and find an adult.

3

Use the Three T Steps

  1. Talk: Use a calm voice. Say, “I don’t like that. Stop.”
  2. Tell: Use a strong voice. Say, “Stop. Back up.”
  3. Tie Up: Protect yourself, create space, leave, and find an adult.
4

Keep Punches and Kicks Out of Class

Use the control positions Sandy teaches. Tell your partner when you need less pressure or a pause.

5

Use Submissions with Control

Add pressure in small steps. Release the hold when your partner taps or says stop. Do not crank a joint or neck.

Partner Safety

  • Use the grips and techniques Sandy assigned for the drill.
  • Control your body and protect your partner.
  • Ask before you increase the pace or pressure.
  • Tap before pain increases. Release your partner when they tap or say stop.
  • Stop when Sandy says, “Time.”
  • Tell Sandy if you need a break or another explanation. Ask for a different movement if you feel pain or cannot perform the drill.

Respect and Behavior

  • Listen when Sandy speaks. Follow his instruction or ask for help.
  • Use each person’s name and pronouns. Treat students and guests with respect.
  • Line up at the start and end of class. Greet your partners and thank them after training.
  • Ask Sandy when you have a question.
  • Do not tease or bully another student. Keep trash talk out of class.

The inclusion statement explains the academy’s conduct, accommodation, and reporting standards.

Cleanliness and Health

  • Wash your gi or training clothes after each class.
  • Take off your shoes before you step onto the mat. Keep outdoor shoes in the designated seating area.
  • Keep your fingernails and toenails short and clean.
  • Remove jewelry before class.
  • Tell Sandy if you feel hurt or dizzy. Tell him if you feel sick or unsafe.

Questions

Text Sandy at (917) 736-8649 or email me@senseisandy.com. Parents and caregivers can ask about class fit, safety, or support before a youth student trains.
