How the intro works

  1. We learn about your child. Sandy asks about experience, interests, concerns, schedule, and goals.
  2. Your child joins the class. The student receives one action, one scoring goal, and one safety rule. Sandy assigns an appropriate partner.
  3. We review the experience. Sandy identifies a specific success, answers questions, and recommends the next schedule.

First-session wins

  • Respond to the stop signal
  • Participate in one controlled partner game
  • Complete one useful movement or positional action
  • Explain one thing learned

The intro does not include multiple free classes, unscheduled drop-ins, private coaching, uncontrolled sparring, or a promise of confidence after one session.
