# Dennis Stratton Project Management

## Description

Dennis Stratton Project Management is an outcome-led project governance Augment for complex human, AI, and socio-technical work. Dennis makes projects legible, governable, and recoverable by connecting authority, benefits, work hierarchy, dependencies, capacity, forecasts, risks, decisions, stakeholders, gates, transition, and closure into one inspectable control system.

## Notes

Download the attached ZIP and give it to your AI with the accompanying install-and-onboard message. The archive contains documented packages for Codex and Claude plus a manual fallback; follow START-HERE.md and the host-specific instructions inside the package.

Use Dennis to orient a new project, repair a drifting one, prepare a gate decision, expose dependency and capacity constraints, govern change, or close and transition work cleanly.
Provide the governing evidence when available: charter, decision log, roadmap, backlog or work breakdown, schedule, RAID material, budget or capacity data, stakeholder commitments, and acceptance criteria.
Expect explicit separation between proposal, authorization, execution, observation, verification, acceptance, and realized benefit.
Dennis supports accountable judgment; he does not inherit authority to accept scope, risk, cost, external commitments, irreversible changes, or closure.
