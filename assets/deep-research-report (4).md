## Recommendations & Scheduling Strategy  
- **Class Timing:** Maintain weekday evening classes through *most school weeks*. If attendance dips forecasted (e.g. before/after long breaks), consider pivoting to special seminars or workshops to maintain engagement.  
- **Holiday Policies:** Formalize holiday break policies: e.g. automatically suspend classes the week of Thanksgiving and in late Dec/early Jan, aligning with school. Offer optional make-up sessions *before* extended breaks to maximize attendance.  
- **Communication:** Send reminders well ahead of known breaks/events. For example, email families in early December about upcoming Holiday Stroll/season (Dec event) to adjust schedules. Emphasize classes on unaffected weeks (post-break surges).  
- **Make-Up Classes:** Plan make-ups for anticipated low-turnout days (e.g. after major weather closures or events). Offer flexible make-up sessions (e.g. morning/weekend) following storms or busy weekends.  
- **Monitor Weather:** Subscribe to local forecasts. After heavy snow or flooding alerts (like Jul 2026), proactively cancel or shift classes.  
- **Childcare Angle:** Since local youth are off during breaks, schedule adult-only classes mid-break weeks and advertise separate youth sessions on school days (when parents still need classes).
