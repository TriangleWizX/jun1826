## 〔Final〕 Product and Offer Strategy

### Objective

Give every offer one defined role, audience, price, call to action, page destination, and search treatment.

Writers should know what to promote. Developers should know what to index. Staff should know what to sell.

---

## STRAT-01 — Approve the Public Offer Hierarchy

**Owner:** Business owner
**Contributors:** Product strategist, marketing lead, web lead
**Required before:** Copywriting, pricing changes, navigation changes, page redesign, redirects, structured data

### Approved Funnel

**Free Intro → Goal Mapping Visit → Core Culture**

Core Culture remains the flagship first-term offer.

The website should direct new local students toward this path. Visitor passes, private coaching, annual plans, and access upgrades should not compete with it.

### Visibility Rules

| Level                | Offers                                               | Public treatment                                         |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary              | Free Intro, Goal Mapping Visit, Core Culture         | Main navigation, homepage funnel, program pages          |
| Secondary            | Day Pass, Vacation Week, Private Coaching            | Visitor section or secondary CTA                         |
| Restricted           | 10-Class Pack, 20-Class Pack                         | Available to approved visitors or existing relationships |
| Premium              | Keyholder, Concierge, Annual Track                   | Member upsell or qualified landing page                  |
| Business development | Law enforcement, hospitality, community partnerships | Separate campaign pages                                  |
| Archived             | 6-Week Test Drive, expired seasonal promotions       | Redirect and remove from navigation                      |

## Recommended Offer Decisions

### Core Culture

Approve Core Culture as the only standard first-term enrollment product.

Each Core Culture student receives:

* A 12-week term
* Three planned home classes each week
* Up to 36 scheduled classes
* Partner matching
* Progress tracking
* Text-based rescheduling when space permits
* One academy gi for first-time Core Culture enrollment
* A 30-day fit guarantee after completing four classes

Do not describe Core Culture as unlimited access.

### Visitor Products

Keep these products active:

* **Day Pass:** $35
* **Vacation Week:** $99 for up to three classes within seven days

Keep these products restricted:

* **10-Class Pack:** $300 with a six-month expiration
* **20-Class Pack:** $550 with a twelve-month expiration

Do not place class packs in the primary navigation. Public class-pack promotion would give local prospects a way to avoid Core Culture.

### Premium Products

Keep Annual Track and Keyholder available only after qualification.

Do not promote them as first-term alternatives.

Keep Concierge hidden until the business defines:

* Included services
* Eligibility
* Capacity
* Price
* Fulfillment standard

### Christmas in July

Set **July 31, 2026** as the final campaign date.

On **August 1, 2026**:

* Remove promotional banners
* Remove campaign navigation
* Disable promotional codes
* Redirect the campaign URL
* Remove campaign schema
* Replace campaign CTAs with “Reserve Your Free Intro”

### Price Control

Use one approved pricing source for website copy, sales scripts, schema, forms, and payment links.

Do not copy prices manually across independent pages.

### Indexing Control

Use these rules:

* **Index:** Stable public offers with distinct search value
* **Noindex:** Member upgrades, restricted products, thin qualification pages
* **Redirect:** Retired offers with a clear replacement
* **Remove:** Expired pages without backlinks, traffic, or useful replacement intent

### STRAT-01 Completion Standard

STRAT-01 is complete when:

* The owner approves every price
* Every offer has one status
* Every offer has one destination URL
* Every indexed page supports a defined search intent
* Every archived offer redirects or returns an intentional status
* Navigation displays only primary and approved secondary offers
* Writers no longer need to infer which product to promote
