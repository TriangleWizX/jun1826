# Location Page Policy — SenseiSandy.com

## Policy Directives
1. Standalone location pages MUST provide distinct, accurate, local value (real route/travel context, local testimonials, studio proximity).
2. Standalone pages are retained for primary service towns:
   - Tannersville (`/bjj-classes/tannersville-ny/`)
   - Hunter (`/bjj-classes/hunter-ny/`)
   - Windham (`/bjj-classes/windham-ny/`)
   - Haines Falls (`/bjj-classes/haines-falls-ny/`)
   - Palenville (`/bjj-classes/palenville-ny/`)
   - Catskill (`/bjj-classes/catskill-ny/`)
   - Cairo (`/bjj-classes/cairo-ny/`)
   - Elka Park (`/bjj-classes/elka-park-ny/`)
3. Thin secondary town pages with zero unique local content are consolidated into `/bjj-classes/tannersville-ny/` via 301 redirects.
4. Under NO circumstances are fake travel times, fabricated local claims, or doorway keyword pages created.
