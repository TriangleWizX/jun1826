# Movement class at school, with Coach Sandy

A free after-school class for CROP kids in grades 1-6. Active games on soft mats, with school teachers in the room.

## What it is

Sensei Sandy Community Jiu Jitsu Inc. brings a free movement class to CROP at Hunter-Tannersville schools. Coach Sandy volunteers his time. Kids play, move, and learn calm focus with friends after school.

## Who it is for

Kids in grades 1-6, in a younger group and an older group. A school teacher stays with each group. Beginners are welcome. Each child moves at their own pace.

## What a session looks like

A calm, coach-led session built on games. We call it Move, Play, Stop.

**Welcome and pause practice.**
Kids learn three rules: care for your friend, freeze means look and listen, and tap or say "Stop" to pause right away. Every child practices letting go before any game starts.

**Balance games.**
Bear walks, soft bridges, and quick freeze games. A wobble just means reset and keep playing.

**Safe rolls and stand-up.**
Kids learn to roll like a ball, chin tucked, and a simple safe stand-up with a shield hand.

**Partner games.**
Gentle, cooperative games about balance and space: Sticky Side, Face Your Friend, and Guard the Gate. Play stays kind and controlled.

**Calm close.**
Three calm belly breaths, one recall question, and tidy-up. Each session has a word of the week.

## What to wear

Wear comfy school clothes and clean socks. The class is barefoot on soft mats.

## How to join

Families join through CROP at school. Ask your CROP coordinator about a spot.

## Questions

**What if my child feels shy?**
Every child moves at their own pace. Coach Sandy guides each child, and kids can pause at any time.

**Does my child need experience?**
Beginners are welcome. The class starts with simple games.
