### 3. Who it is for

### 4. What a session looks like — "Move, Play, Stop"
A calm, coach-led session built on games:
- **Welcome and pause practice.** Kids learn three rules: care for your friend, freeze means look and listen, and tap or say "Stop" to pause right away. Every child practices letting go before any game starts.
- **Balance games.** Bear walks, soft bridges, and quick freeze games. A wobble just means reset and keep playing.
- **Safe rolls and stand-up.** Kids learn to roll like a ball, chin tucked, and a simple safe stand-up with a shield hand.
- **Partner games.** Gentle, cooperative games about balance and space: Sticky Side, Face Your Friend, and Guard the Gate. Play stays kind and controlled.
- **Calm close.** Three calm belly breaths, one recall question, and tidy-up. Each session has a word of the week.
*(Safety-language note: "tap or say 'Stop' to pause" is the class's actual safety cue, kept verbatim for safety clarity. It is instructional language, not a marketing claim — flagged here as a deliberate exception to the banned-token list. Owner call if he wants it softened.)*

### 5. What to wear
