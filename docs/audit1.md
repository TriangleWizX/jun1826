1. Standardize the Free Intro

The homepage calls it a two-visit process: a 15-minute Goal Mapping appointment, followed by a reserved class. The booking page also says “15 minutes,” “normal clothes,” and “no workout required.” However, global CTA blocks promise that the visitor will “begin guided partner practice,” which sounds like it happens during the same appointment.

Use this exact global model:

Reserve Your Free Intro
Step 1: Meet Sandy for a 15-minute Goal Mapping Visit, studio tour, and safety walkthrough.
Step 2: Return for your reserved beginner-guided class.

Use Reserve Your Free Intro as the primary CTA everywhere. Use Reserve Your Free Intro only as the explanatory label inside the booking flow.

2. Fix the Age-System Conflict

Current program structure:

Kids: 5–9
Teens: 10–17
Adults: 18+

Current booking structure:

Kids: 6–11
Teens: 12–15
Adults: 16+

Pricing groups youth as ages 5–17.

Set one global taxonomy:

Kids: ages 5–9
Teens: ages 10–17
Adults: ages 18+
Add: “Placement may also consider size, maturity, experience, and family schedule.”

An older teen training with adults should be described as an individual placement decision, not a conflicting age definition.

3. Decide Whether These Are “Small-Group Classes”

The new homepage, program hub, local pages, pricing page, and youth pages now use “small-group” repeatedly. Yet the existing business model has historically described classes capped at up to 12 students. Visitors normally interpret small-group training as approximately two to four people.

Best wording:

Small-group, coach-led Jiu-Jitsu

Then support it with:

Class sizes are capped so every student receives direct coaching.

Reserve small-group for a clearly defined smaller product, or publish the exact maximum class size beside every small-group claim.

4. Restore Homepage Clarity

The live homepage currently opens with:

“Sustainable Combat Athletics in Tannersville, NY”

It then presents Adults, Youth, and LEO as the three principal starting lanes. Teens disappear as a distinct lane, while “BJJ/Judo” and “combat athletics” introduce terminology that the rest of the website does not consistently support.

Replace the hero with:

Build Confidence Through Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in Tannersville

Small-group, coach-led classes for kids, teens, and adults. Begin with a studio tour, safety walkthrough, and a training plan built around your goals.

Primary CTA: Reserve Your Free Intro
Secondary CTA: Text Sandy First

Restore the three principal cards:

Kids 5–9
Teens 10–17
Adults 18+

Place LEO training, private coaching, birthday parties, and Catskills Home Base below the primary paths.
