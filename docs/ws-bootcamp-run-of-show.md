# Alley Bootcamp: Two Runs of Show Deepdive (Strength vs. Engine)

**Document ID**: `docs/ws-bootcamp-run-of-show.md`  
**Academy**: Sensei Sandy BJJ (6045 Main Street, Tannersville, NY)  
**Session Times**: Tuesday & Thursday 6:30 AM – 7:10 AM (40 Minutes)  
**Cohort Capacity**: Maximum 8 adults (2 students per station across 4 stations)  
**Head Coach**: Sandy (1st-degree BJJ Black Belt)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Periodization Philosophy

Alley Bootcamp is designed around a dual-track periodization model tailored for adults living in the Catskills mountains. Instead of repeating the same generic workout every morning, the program separates weekly training into two complementary physiological adaptations:

1. **Tuesday: STRENGTH (Structural Resilience & Joint Armor)**
   - **Primary Objective**: Mechanical tension, posterior chain strength, spinal stability, and bone density.
   - **Metabolic Pathway**: Phosphagen (ATP-PC) and aerobic recovery; slow, controlled time-under-tension (TUT).
   - **Mountain Benefit**: Builds the resilient quads, hamstrings, and glutes required to ascend steep trails, carry heavy gear, and protect knees during downhill mountain terrain.

2. **Thursday: ENGINE (Aerobic Capacity & Metabolic Work Rate)**
   - **Primary Objective**: Cardiovascular output, mitochondrial density, lactic buffering, and work capacity.
   - **Metabolic Pathway**: Glycolytic and high-flux oxidative systems; higher cadence with fixed rest intervals.
   - **Mountain Benefit**: Expands lung capacity, increases all-day stamina on the slopes or hiking trails, and accelerates calorie burn to support steady fat loss on Sandy's scale.

## 2. The 4 Core Modality Stations

With an 8-person cohort cap, the alley is divided into 4 distinct stations, with exactly **2 students per station**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        ALLEY STATION ARCHITECTURE                      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [Station 1: Bulgarian Bag] ──► 2 Students (Rotational Core & Carries)  │
│ [Station 2: Kettlebell]    ──► 2 Students (Posterior Chain & Leverage) │
│ [Station 3: Jump Rope]     ──► 2 Students (Footwork & Elastic Bounce)  │
│ [Station 4: Alley Steps]   ──► 2 Students (Elevation Power & Sprints)  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Station 1: The Bulgarian Bag Station
- **Equipment**: Heavy and medium leather Bulgarian bags with flexible horn handles.
- **Physiological Role**: Multi-planar rotational strength, shoulder girdle stabilization, and thoracic mobility.
- **Tuesday Strength Exercise**:
  - *Movement*: Rotational Bag Lunges & Heavy Good Mornings.
  - *Cadence*: 8–10 slow, deliberate reps per side. 3-second eccentric lower, 1-second pause.
  - *Coaching Focus*: Upright torso, strong core brace, active shoulder pack.
- **Thursday Engine Exercise**:
  - *Movement*: Bag Snatches & High-Speed Lamb Swings.
  - *Cadence*: 20–25 rhythmic cycles per minute. Continuous fluid momentum.
  - *Coaching Focus*: Powerful hip drive, explosive breathing on extension, soft knee catch.

### Station 2: The Kettlebell Station
- **Equipment**: Competition and cast iron kettlebells ranging from 12 kg (26 lbs) to 32 kg (70 lbs).
- **Physiological Role**: Pure mechanical leverage, hip hinge mastery, and full-body grip engagement.
- **Tuesday Strength Exercise**:
  - *Movement*: Double Kettlebell Suitcase Deadlifts & Strict Goblet Squats with pause.
  - *Cadence*: 6–8 reps per set. 2-second pause at bottom, full hip lockout at top.
  - *Coaching Focus*: Flat spine, lat engagement, driving feet through the wooden boardwalk.
- **Thursday Engine Exercise**:
  - *Movement*: Two-Handed Russian Kettlebell Swings.
  - *Cadence*: 20–24 powerful hip snaps per 40-second work bout.
  - *Coaching Focus*: Crisp hip snap, floating the bell at chest height, sharp exhales at peak.

### Station 3: The Jump Rope Station
- **Equipment**: Licorice speed ropes and weighted PVC conditioning ropes.
- **Physiological Role**: Achilles tendon stiffness, ankle stabilization, foot-eye coordination, and rhythmic pacing.
- **Tuesday Strength Exercise**:
  - *Movement*: Heavy Rope Single-Leg Balance Hops & Slow High-Knee Marches.
  - *Cadence*: Slow, controlled rhythm with deliberate ground contact.
  - *Coaching Focus*: Landing softly on balls of feet, keeping arches active, absorbing force quietly.
- **Thursday Engine Exercise**:
  - *Movement*: Speed Boxer Skips & Fast Cadence Turns.
  - *Cadence*: 120–140 rotations per minute.
  - *Coaching Focus*: Low wrists, minimal ground contact time, steady nasal-diaphragmatic breath.

### Station 4: The Sprint & Step Station
- **Equipment**: 19 wooden alley steps across 4 landings, plus flat alley straightaway.
- **Physiological Role**: Vertical climbing power, quad deceleration, and maximal cardiovascular recruitment.
- **Tuesday Strength Exercise**:
  - *Movement*: Weighted Step Power Climb (Farmer's carry up 19 steps) + Controlled Walk Descent.
  - *Cadence*: Heavy step-by-step ascent, single step per stride, zero skipping steps.
  - *Coaching Focus*: Weight through the heel, full hip extension on every riser, slow descent to protect joints.
- **Thursday Engine Exercise**:
  - *Movement*: High-Velocity Boardwalk Sprint & Fast Step Ascents + Jog Descent.
  - *Cadence*: Rapid turnover, light feet on stairs, explosive acceleration out of the turn.
  - *Coaching Focus*: Forward chest lean, quick foot turnover, smooth deceleration at the landing.

## 3. Hour-by-Hour Timeline: 40-Minute Run of Show

### Minute 0:00 – 7:00 | Dynamic Warm-Up & Joint Mobility
Sandy gathers the 8 students at the middle alley landing.
- **Minute 0–2**: Breathing sync (4-second inhale, 4-second exhale) and spinal rolls.
- **Minute 2–4**: Hip openers, world's greatest stretch, ankle circles, and calf wall pumps.
- **Minute 4–7**: Movement pattern rehearsal (bodyweight squats, light jump rope bounces, step taps).

### Minute 7:00 – 10:00 | Station Instruction & Personal Scaling
- Sandy demonstrates the 4 station movements for the morning.
- Confirms weight selection for each student based on their fitness level and joint comfort.
- Assigns 2 students per station (Stations 1, 2, 3, 4).

### Minute 10:00 – 34:00 | The Main Work Circuit (24 Minutes)

#### Tuesday STRENGTH Protocol:
- **Work Interval**: 60 seconds work.
- **Rest Interval**: 30 seconds transition & rest.
- **Cycle Time**: 90 seconds per station × 4 stations = 6 minutes per round.
- **Volume**: 4 full rounds (24 minutes total work capacity).
- **Pacing**: Deliberate, high-tension, heavy weight. Students complete 8–12 controlled reps per station.

#### Thursday ENGINE Protocol:
- **Work Interval**: 40 seconds high-output work.
- **Rest Interval**: 20 seconds transition.
- **Cycle Time**: 60 seconds per station × 4 stations = 4 minutes per round.
- **Volume**: 5 full rounds + 4-minute team step relay (24 minutes total).
- **Pacing**: High density, steady cadence, low rest. Students sustain unbroken movement throughout the 40 seconds.

### Minute 34:00 – 40:00 | Coached Cool-Down & Weekly Progress Check
- **Minute 34–37**: Coached stretching, foam roller decompression, and diaphragmatic breathing.
- **Minute 37–40**: Private weigh-ins on Sandy's gym scale for students on the 8-session goal guarantee.
- **7:10 AM**: Class dismissed on schedule. Students leave refreshed and ready for their day.

## 4. Run of Show Comparison Matrix

| Phase | Tuesday (Strength Focus) | Thursday (Engine Focus) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Goal** | Muscle tone, posterior chain, joint armor | Lung capacity, metabolic burn, mountain stamina |
| **Circuit Interval** | 60s work / 30s rest (2:1 strength ratio) | 40s work / 20s rest (2:1 density ratio) |
| **Rounds** | 4 complete rounds (6 min/round) | 5 complete rounds (4 min/round) + 4-min finisher |
| **Bulgarian Bag** | Rotational lunges & heavy good mornings | Speed snatches & continuous lamb swings |
| **Kettlebell** | Strict suitcase deadlifts & paused squats | Explosive Russian hip-snap swings |
| **Jump Rope** | Heavy rope single-leg balance hops | Fast boxer skip cadence & speed turns |
| **Sprint / Steps** | Weighted step power climbs (heavy carry) | High-speed boardwalk sprints & quick-step climbs |
| **Post-Workout Feel** | Strong, grounded, muscularly engaged | Energized, light, high-metabolism glow |

## 5. Safety, Weather Contingency & Coaching Standards

1. **Weather Backup**: If morning mountain rain or snow falls, the entire 4-station circuit seamlessly transfers inside the heated, clean BJJ studio mat room.
2. **Coach-to-Student Ratio**: Strict 1:8 coach-to-student ratio ensures Sandy monitors every single repetition, prevents fatigue-induced form breakdown, and enforces safe joint alignments.
3. **Beginner Lane Adjustments**: Every station offers a ground-level regression (e.g. flat ground marching instead of jump rope, bodyweight step-ups instead of weighted carries).
