All three reviewers agreed on the central correction: **private lessons are not included in the current Adult Core Culture front-end offer, and the $7,150 / 10× concept is not an approved product.**

## 2. Executive fixes

### Fix 1 — Keep the front-end offer simple

Adult Core Culture remains the first adult decision:

- **$715 for 12 weeks**;
- three planned home classes each week;
- up to 36 scheduled classes;
- placement based on fit and capacity;
- coached partner practice and safety management;
- progress tracking and term review;
- one academy gi with first qualifying enrollment; and
- the existing 30-Day Training Fit Guarantee, subject to its approved terms.

The page should sell the coached system, not a pile of speculative bonuses.

### Fix 2 — Separate private lessons from Adult Core Culture

The current offer does **not** include private lessons. Current evidence supports:

- Tannersville private coaching at **$195 for one 60-minute session**;
- private coaching by request, with fit and availability confirmed before booking;
- a `$1,050` six-pack in `src/_data/pricing.json`; and
- public private-page copy that currently treats additional pack pricing as “By request” / “To confirm.”

The six-pack conflict must be reconciled before it is used as public offer math.

Recommended public boundary:

> **Need more individual coaching?** Private coaching is a separate request-based path. Sandy confirms fit, availability, session details, and pricing before enrollment.

### Fix 3 — Feature the supported premium path correctly

The current supported adult premium path is **Elite Concierge**, not the hypothetical $7,150 product.

Current authority describes Elite Concierge as:

- adults only;
- continuation-only after Core Culture;
- $1,050 for 12 weeks;
- three planned classes per week;
- one private coaching session;
- one weekly laundry bag with up to three training sets;
- assigned gear storage;
- direct scheduling help;
- first consideration for a suitable replacement seat;
- subject to class caps, placement, and availability; and
- excluded from the 30-Day Training Fit Guarantee.

On `/options-pricing/`, show Elite Concierge below first-term offers and label it **continuation-only**. Do not make it an equal first-click alternative to Adult Core Culture.

### Fix 4 — Quarantine the $7,150 / 10× concept

Ten times the current adult front-end price is mathematically `$7,150`, but no approved product currently supports that price.

The opportunity report should describe it as:

> **Proposed Adult Private-Intensive Concept — $7,150 paid in full, subject to approval.**

It must remain internal until Sandy approves:

1. product name and audience;
2. first-term versus continuation eligibility;
3. number and duration of private sessions;
4. private-session capacity and protected schedule blocks;
5. group-training inclusions and limits;
6. payment terms;
7. rescheduling, expiration, pause, cancellation, and refund rules;
8. guarantee treatment;
9. maximum enrollment; and
10. fulfillment cost and owner.

Do not publish `$71,500 value`, `10× value`, `save $64,350`, crossed-out anchor pricing, or fake scarcity.

## 5. Corrected 10× pricing model

### What is factual

`$715 × 10 = $7,150`.

That establishes a comparison price, not a valid product or value claim.

Known current price references include:

- Adult Annual Track: $2,650;
- Private Coaching: $195 single session;
- pricing-data six-pack: $1,050, but public packaging remains to confirm;
- Gear Laundry & Storage Add-On: $195 / 12 weeks for eligible adults; and
- Elite Concierge: $1,050 / 12 weeks, continuation-only.

These references do not prove that a $7,150 bundle delivers $71,500 of value.

### Internal design test

If the owner wants to test a 10× concept, use this internal model:

| Scenario | Amount | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| Proposed price | **$7,150 paid in full** | Mathematical scenario; not approved |
| Target perceived value | **$71,500** | Design target only |
| Required value components | Private coaching, group training, reviews, logistics, practice support | Must be specified and delivered |
| Unresolved value needed after the current model | **$4,000** | `to confirm`; not a benefit |

The unresolved `$4,000` must represent a real, owner-approved component—such as a defined private curriculum with preparation, review, and support—not a decorative line item. Until it has a scope, cost, capacity, proof, and approved value, the public phrase should be **premium coaching intensive**, not **10× value**.
