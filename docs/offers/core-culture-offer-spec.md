# 2. Core Culture

## Public description

> **Core Culture is a 12-week small-group program with three planned classes each week. During your Free Intro, you will choose three recurring home classes. When your schedule changes, text Sandy to request another appropriate class with space.**

## Each enrollment includes

* A 12-week term.
* Three planned classes per week.
* Three recurring home classes.
* Up to 36 scheduled classes.
* A 12-student class cap.
* Placement based on age, size, experience, and partner fit.
* Progress tracking.
* Partner matching.
* One academy gi with first-time enrollment.
* A 30-day fit guarantee.
* A renewal review during weeks 10 and 11.

## Student path

1. Reserve a Free Intro.
2. Complete Goal Mapping.
3. Choose three home classes.
4. Start Core Culture.
5. Review progress during weeks 10 and 11.
6. Continue through another Core term or Annual Track.

Core Culture leads the local sales path. Private Coaching, Elite Concierge, and Visitor Options support specific needs.

# 4. Operating definitions

## Planned class

A planned class is one assigned home class or one replacement class approved by Sandy.

## Home class

A home class gives the student a recurring reservation during the active term.

Sandy may change the reservation when:

* The academy changes its schedule.
* The class reaches capacity.
* The student needs different partners.
* Another class offers a safer placement.
* The student’s attendance pattern no longer fits the assigned time.

The student does not own the time slot after the term ends.

## Missed class

A missed home class does not create a stored credit.

The student may request a replacement when the term remains active, the requested class has space, and Sandy approves the placement.

Unused classes expire at the end of the term unless Sandy approves a medical pause or written exception.

## Rescheduling copy

Use:

> Text Sandy to request another appropriate class with space.

Remove:

* Make up anytime.
* Attend any class.
* Unlimited makeup classes.
* Flexible access to all classes.
* Roll over unused classes.
* Train as often as you want.

# 5. Youth Core Culture

**$550 for 12 weeks**

Youth students receive three home classes and up to 36 scheduled classes.

Most students attend:

* Wednesday No-Gi.
* Two gi classes from Monday, Tuesday, and Friday.

Sandy may approve another schedule based on maturity, size, experience, transportation, partner availability, and class capacity.

## Teen placement

Sandy may place a teen in a Youth or Adult class. He considers safety, maturity, experience, size, and available partners. Age alone does not qualify a teen for Adult classes.

# 8. Annual Track

Annual Track covers four 12-week training cycles within a 12-month enrollment window.

It includes:

* 48 programmed training weeks.
* Three planned classes per week.
* Up to 144 planned classes.
* Three home classes.
* A schedule and progress review after each cycle.

Do not describe Annual Track as 52 guaranteed training weeks.

## Eligibility

Sandy may offer Annual Track after the student completes one Core Culture term.

Before approval, Sandy confirms:

* The account remains in good standing.
* The program remains safe for the student.
* Three suitable home classes remain available.
* The student or parent completes a progress review.
* The student or parent accepts the Annual Track terms.

The student does not need to attend all 36 first-term classes.

## Terms

* The student may request a replacement class with space.
* Annual Track does not include unrestricted attendance.
* Annual Track does not include another gi.
* The student may not transfer the enrollment.
* The medical-pause policy applies.
* Sandy may change home classes when the academy schedule changes.
* Annual Track does not renew without approval.

Before renewal, Sandy reviews progress, attendance, schedule fit, partner availability, and account standing. The student or parent must approve the next enrollment before payment.

# 9. Elite Concierge

**$1,050 for 12 weeks**
**Adults only**
**Pilot limit: three students**

Elite Concierge includes Adult Core Culture plus:

* One Private Coaching session.
* One academy laundry bag per week.
* Up to three training sets per bag.
* Assigned gear storage.
* Direct scheduling help.
* First consideration for suitable replacement seats.
* A detailed term review.
* Loaner gear when available.
* One academy gi with first-time enrollment.

## Limits

Elite Concierge covers three planned classes per week. It does not guarantee extra classes or a replacement seat.

Laundry covers one bag and three training sets per week. It excludes same-day service, personal clothing, and home delivery.

The plan includes one Private Coaching session. Storage remains assigned, and loaner equipment depends on availability.

Use:

> Elite Concierge students receive first consideration when a suitable replacement seat opens. Sandy will still enforce the class cap and placement requirements.

Before adding a fourth student, Sandy must review laundry time, storage use, scheduling demand, Private Coaching capacity, service errors, and labor cost.

# 10. Medical pauses

## Core Culture

A student may receive one medical pause per term for up to 14 consecutive days.

The student should contact Sandy before the absence or within seven days after it begins. Sandy extends the term by the approved number of days.

The student does not need to disclose a diagnosis. Sandy may request confirmation that the student cannot train safely.

Sandy will preserve the student’s home classes when capacity allows. Longer interruptions require an academy-credit review.

Approved credit:

* Expires six months after the expected return date.
* Remains with the original student.
* Has no cash value after the fit-guarantee period, except where law requires a refund.

## Annual Track

A student may receive two medical pauses totaling no more than 30 days.

Sandy extends the enrollment window by the approved number of days. He may reassign home classes after a long absence.

## Qualifying reasons

* Injury or extended illness.
* Medical procedure.
* Physician-directed activity restriction.
* Pregnancy-related restriction.
* Recovery that prevents safe training.

Vacation, work travel, transportation problems, schedule conflicts, school events, and ordinary missed classes do not qualify.

# 11. Laundry

Elite Concierge includes one labeled academy bag per week with up to three training sets.

## One training set means

**Gi:** one jacket and one pair of pants.

**No-Gi:** one rashguard and one pair of grappling shorts or leggings.

## Turnaround

> Sandy will return the bag within 72 hours or by the student’s second scheduled home class after drop-off, whichever comes later.

## Excluded items

Sandy will not accept:

* Moldy gear.
* Gear contaminated with blood or bodily fluids.
* Shoes or personal clothing.
* Dry-clean-only items.
* Specialty equipment.
* Items that require special washing.
* Gear with sharp objects.
* Gear that presents a mildew risk.

Students must empty pockets, remove personal items, close the assigned bag, and use the collection area.

Sandy sends an abandoned-gear reminder after 14 days, moves the gear to lost and found after 30 days, and sends a final notice after 45 days. He may donate or dispose of the gear after 60 days when permitted.
