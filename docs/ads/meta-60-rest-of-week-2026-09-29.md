## Daily operating rhythm

Publish in three blocks where practical: discovery videos first, teaching carousels/static proof next, and the conversion unit last. The exact time should follow the available class footage rather than forcing a claim about a peak window. Capture the post’s Facebook and Instagram native URL or ID immediately after publication.

For each unit, mark one of: `planned`, `drafted`, `scheduled`, `published-owner-reported`, or `verified-native`. Credit the 60 only when the unit is a qualifying feed/Reel post and the same creative is not already counted. A scheduled item is not published evidence.
