# Revised VSL shoot order

October 6, 2026. Use the exact words below. Record two takes per block with two seconds of silence at each end. Speak at a calm pace; measure actual take duration. Targets are edit allocations, not timed speech guarantees.

## Setup

Use a stable phone at eye level, soft light, and a quiet room. Place the mic below the chin, record room tone, and play back a test. Obtain consent before filming identifiable people; youth need documented guardian permission. Keep personal booking data out of shots.

## Physical recording order

### 1. S05: How do we start? (45s edit target)

> You start in our Beginner Lane. I show you the room. We talk through the rules. You learn with a matched partner at your pace. You can ask me to show a move. We try it one step at a time. You can pause a round. I help you find your next step. You get time to learn how class feels.

### 2. S06: How does my first class work? (40s edit target)

> We plan a first class that fits you. Adults start with a visit. You can ask to train that day. I must confirm your class before you train. You can choose a later day. Kids and teens use their own class plan. A parent or guardian stays with them. I confirm the class and what to wear. We talk about your goals and find the next step together. Wear day clothes to visit. Wear clean sports clothes to train.

### 3. S04: Why learn with Sandy? (75s edit target)

> I am Sandy. I earned my black belt at Clockwork Jiu Jitsu in New York City. I taught kids there. I led adult classes in Brooklyn. I coach in Tannersville. We start by talking about your goals. You can tell me what you want to learn. We see the room and talk about the rules. I help you plan your first class. We find a pace and partner that fit you. You can ask me questions as we go. You get a clear next step. Come meet me and see how we train in the Catskills.

### 4. S09: What comes after the free class? (15s edit target)

> Your first class is free. If you choose to keep training, I show you the paid plans. You can ask me what each plan includes.

### 5. S07: What is the first step? (10s edit target)

> Meet me for fifteen minutes. See the room. I confirm your free first class.

### 6. S08: What happens after the visit? (10s edit target)

> You can ask questions. You choose your next step. I can help you plan a later class.

### 7. S10: Find a time that fits (10s edit target)

> Pick a visit time that fits your week. We can talk about your goals and plan your class.

### 8. S02: Calm coaching (6s edit target)

> I coach you step by step. We start calm.

### 9. S03: See how coaching works (15s edit target)

> See how I coach. I show a move. You try it with a partner. I watch and give you a clear tip. You try the move.

### 10. S01: Time for you (7s edit target)

> Want time for you? Come learn a new skill with us.

### 11. S11: Reserve your first visit (7s edit target)

> Tap the link for your free visit. Come meet me.
