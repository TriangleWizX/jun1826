### S05: How do we start?

> You start in our Beginner Lane. I show you the room. We talk through the rules. You learn with a matched partner at your pace. You can ask me to show a move. We try it one step at a time. You can pause a round. I help you find your next step. You get time to learn how class feels.

### S06: How does my first class work?

> We plan a first class that fits you. Adults start with a visit. You can ask to train that day. I must confirm your class before you train. You can choose a later day. Kids and teens use their own class plan. A parent or guardian stays with them. I confirm the class and what to wear. We talk about your goals and find the next step together. Wear day clothes to visit. Wear clean sports clothes to train.

### S04: Why learn with Sandy?

> I am Sandy. I earned my black belt at Clockwork Jiu Jitsu in New York City. I taught kids there. I led adult classes in Brooklyn. I coach in Tannersville. We start by talking about your goals. You can tell me what you want to learn. We see the room and talk about the rules. I help you plan your first class. We find a pace and partner that fit you. You can ask me questions as we go. You get a clear next step. Come meet me and see how we train in the Catskills.

### S09: What comes after the free class?
