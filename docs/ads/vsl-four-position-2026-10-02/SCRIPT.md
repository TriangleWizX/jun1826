## Learning checks

**Standing:** We are both on our feet. Find your base.

**Guard:** Use your legs to make space. Or work to get past the legs.

**Chest to chest:** Hold from the top. Or work to make space.

**Chest to back:** Hold from behind. Or turn to face your partner.

**After the four classes:** Show me the four spots. Name each one. What is your goal? Show me one thing you can try.

**If a step needs more help:** I will show you the step. We can try it with my help.
