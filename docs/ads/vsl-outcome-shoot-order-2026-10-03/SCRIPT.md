# Outcome-led VSL: Sandy recording copy

This draft replaces the October 2 curriculum-led VSL for recording and editing. It preserves the approved offer and four-position learning aim. S numbers are EDIT order, not shoot order. Final length depends on the actual read; budget roughly 4–5 minutes including shot holds.

## S01: The life Ben wants

> Want to feel strong and sure of yourself? Want a place to learn and fit in? Picture time each week that feels like yours. A skill to build. People to learn with. I'm Sandy at Mountaintop BJJ in Tannersville.

**On screen:** Time for you. Room to grow.

**Picture:** Start with Sandy in frame. Cut to a real adult welcome and a warm end-of-class moment. These illustrate the invitation, not a promised transformation.

## S02: The need beneath the purchase

> Work and home can fill your whole week. You want time to move and feel good. You want to learn and use your mind. If you have a child, you may want that for them too. A place to join in. A chance to feel proud.

**On screen:** Move. Learn. Join in.

**Picture:** Real adults learning; brief parent-and-child arrival if permitted. Avoid invented before-and-after scenes.

## S03: Proof and the video roadmap

> Here is what that first step looks like. I show one step. You give it a try. I watch and help. You have someone to learn with. Let me show you how we start. Then we can find a first class that fits.

**On screen:** See how your first step works.

**Picture:** Use one coherent cue → attempt → feedback exchange, not three unrelated students. This proves coaching only. If missing, film Sandy with a consenting adult and label it a demo.

## S04: A brief way to make learning feel clear

> We start with your goals. On the mat, we work on one clear task. Across four classes, our aim is to help you learn four main spots. Standing. The guard. Chest to chest. Chest to back. You learn where you are and what to try.

**On screen:** Standing / Guard / Chest to chest / Chest to back

**Picture:** Four matched position clips, about 3 seconds each, then return to a person learning. Keep this whole section near 25 seconds. No fixed one-position-per-class promise.

## S05: Readiness: start where you are

> You may want to feel fit before you start. You can start at your own pace. I match your partner and the pace. You can pause a round at any time. Your first task is to learn one step. We build from where you are.

**On screen:** Start at your pace.

**Picture:** Use actual matched practice and a reset. Avoid a hard competition montage under beginner reassurance.

## S06: Belonging: a place for a new learner

> You may wonder where a new person fits. Your first visit gives us time to meet. We talk about your goals. You see the room. In class, I help you with each task. You can ask me to show a step. You have help as you learn.

**On screen:** Meet Sandy. Find your first step.

**Picture:** Greeting, room orientation and Sandy answering a real question. Show the actual welcome rather than asserting everyone feels at home.

## S07: Family: support your child as they try

> You may want your child to feel brave as they try something new. Kids and teens meet me, then try a class. A parent or guardian stays with them. We can talk about your child's goals. We plan the right class for each of you.

**On screen:** A first step for your child, too.

**Picture:** Brief permission-cleared youth coaching, parent present. Separate adult and youth class shots; no suggestion of a shared mixed-age class. Sandy in frame is a usable fallback.

## S08: Time: a start that can fit real life

> You may need a start that fits your week. For adults, we start with a fifteen-minute goal chat. Then you can choose to train that day. I confirm the class first. You can choose a later day too. We talk through what fits your time.

**On screen:** Adult goal chat, then your class choice.

**Picture:** Sandy on camera for the required chat and elective class. Room footage can cover the last sentence. Same-day class follows the chat and requires confirmation.

## S09: Cost and choice: try, then decide

> You may want to try a class first. Your first class is free. Then you choose if you keep training. We go through the paid plans and prices. Your next three classes use the paid plan you choose. We aim for four classes in two weeks. We pick dates that fit your plan and have space.

**On screen:** First class free. Next three on your paid plan.

**Picture:** Keep Sandy visible for free/paid terms. Hold the complete overlay throughout. Four-class learning aim is not a free four-class product or an outcome guarantee.

## S10: Return to the desired life and invite

> Picture a skill you want to work on. People you look forward to seeing. A part of the week you choose for you. Come meet me and see how class feels. Tap to book your free first visit. Tell me who wants to start. I'll help you find the right first step.

**On screen:** Reserve Your Free First Visit

**Picture:** Sandy to camera, with a short authentic class-ending clip before the invitation. End on the single booking action for 6–8 seconds.
