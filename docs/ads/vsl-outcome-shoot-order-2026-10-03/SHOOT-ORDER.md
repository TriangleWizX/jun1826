# VSL shoot order: outcome-led revision

Current recording draft, October 3, 2026. This supersedes the October 2 VSL creative direction; it does not change academy policy. Use SCRIPT.md for viewer order. This sheet is physical recording order.

## What the video sells

Ben wants time for himself, a skill worth building, and people he looks forward to seeing. Parents want their child to join in and take pride in trying. These are aspirations, not promised results. The four positions explain how learning can become understandable. They occupy one short section, not the hook or four curriculum chapters.

## Set up once

Use one quiet room, a stable phone at eye height, and soft light on Sandy. Record a short sound test and listen before the session. Keep the same microphone distance. Record ten seconds of room sound. Speak to one new student. Two complete takes per block: one relaxed, one a little brighter. Leave two seconds quiet before and after each take. Use the talking-head audio as the voiceover under existing footage.

## Record in this order

### 1. S08: Time: a start that can fit real life

> You may need a start that fits your week. For adults, we start with a fifteen-minute goal chat. Then you can choose to train that day. I confirm the class first. You can choose a later day too. We talk through what fits your time.

### 2. S09: Cost and choice: try, then decide

> You may want to try a class first. Your first class is free. Then you choose if you keep training. We go through the paid plans and prices. Your next three classes use the paid plan you choose. We aim for four classes in two weeks. We pick dates that fit your plan and have space.

### 3. S04: A brief way to make learning feel clear

> We start with your goals. On the mat, we work on one clear task. Across four classes, our aim is to help you learn four main spots. Standing. The guard. Chest to chest. Chest to back. You learn where you are and what to try.

### 4. S05: Readiness: start where you are

> You may want to feel fit before you start. You can start at your own pace. I match your partner and the pace. You can pause a round at any time. Your first task is to learn one step. We build from where you are.

### 5. S06: Belonging: a place for a new learner

> You may wonder where a new person fits. Your first visit gives us time to meet. We talk about your goals. You see the room. In class, I help you with each task. You can ask me to show a step. You have help as you learn.

### 6. S07: Family: support your child as they try

> You may want your child to feel brave as they try something new. Kids and teens meet me, then try a class. A parent or guardian stays with them. We can talk about your child's goals. We plan the right class for each of you.

### 7. S03: Proof and the video roadmap

> Here is what that first step looks like. I show one step. You give it a try. I watch and help. You have someone to learn with. Let me show you how we start. Then we can find a first class that fits.

### 8. S02: The need beneath the purchase

> Work and home can fill your whole week. You want time to move and feel good. You want to learn and use your mind. If you have a child, you may want that for them too. A place to join in. A chance to feel proud.

### 9. S10: Return to the desired life and invite

> Picture a skill you want to work on. People you look forward to seeing. A part of the week you choose for you. Come meet me and see how class feels. Tap to book your free first visit. Tell me who wants to start. I'll help you find the right first step.

### 10. S01: The life Ben wants

> Want to feel strong and sure of yourself? Want a place to learn and fit in? Picture time each week that feels like yours. A skill to build. People to learn with. I'm Sandy at Mountaintop BJJ in Tannersville.

Record the alternate social ending after S10. Record S01 last while Sandy is warmed up. Hook and close should be usable on camera; the other takes can be covered with existing clips. Retake a sentence in full if it changes the adult/youth or free/paid terms.

## Assemble in viewer order

S01 desire → S02 daily need → S03 coaching proof and roadmap → S04 short method → S05 readiness → S06 belonging → S07 child fit → S08 time → S09 cost/choice → S10 desired week and invitation.

Estimated 4–5 minutes at a relaxed read with picture holds. Time the actual recording; do not pad it to hit a duration. S01–S03 form the opening promise, pain, proof and plan. S04 is the brief distinctive approach. S05–S09 answer five concerns. S10 closes with one action.

Keep real learning and welcome moments under the middle sections so the video continues to show the life the viewer wants. A permanent four-position progress bar would pull attention back to the curriculum; show labels only in S04. Use captions that match the recorded words. Music stays below Sandy's voice. Hold the final CTA 6–8 seconds.

## Five concern loops from the supplied format

These are editorial notes, not additional narration. The belief is introduced gently in each spoken block; the reframe stays positive. Proof must match the specific statement.

| Block | Belief | Why that belief need not prevent a start | Correct next step | Evidence to show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S05 | I need to get fit first | First practice can use a matched partner and pace | Start with one coached task | Real matched practice and reset |
| S06 | A new person may feel out of place | The first visit gives time to meet Sandy and see the room | Ask questions and get help | Actual greeting and coaching exchange |
| S07 | My child may need support to try | Youth meet Sandy and a parent stays | Discuss the child's goals and appropriate class | Permitted youth welcome with parent present |
| S08 | Starting takes time I cannot fit in | Adult visit and class can be on the same day, if confirmed | Pick the confirmed same-day or later class | Actual room/visit scene plus accurate policy text |
| S09 | I must buy before I can try | First class is free | Try, then choose whether to buy a paid plan | Large truthful first-free/next-paid overlay |

A policy overlay proves the stated terms, not emotional results. Coaching footage proves observable teaching. A genuine customer account can support that person's experience. Do not turn smiles into proof of lasting confidence or fitness.

The owner selects clips manually. No clip is marked verified yet. Fill in CLIP-MATCH.csv with the exact filename, in/out time, what is observable, and intended-use permission. Prefer an adult beginner for the first proof sequence. Youth are secondary; use Sandy on camera if cleared youth footage is unavailable.

Must-have coverage: adult welcome; connected cue → attempt → feedback; matched adult practice/reset; four clear positional examples; real room; optional cleared youth/parent moment; authentic end-of-class interaction. Existing footage should cover most of the edit.

Minimum new shoot: Sandy's ten voice blocks plus alternate CTA. Record pickups only after the clip sheet shows a gap. If the connected coaching sequence is missing, film Sandy teaching a consenting adult and label it a demo; do not present a staged scene as a first-timer result. Any missing position can likewise be shown by a demo. Keep both bodies and important limbs visible. A child never needs to be recruited for an emotional transformation scene.

Optional customer proof: the review bank and maintained adult/youth pages contain candidate accounts. The working script needs no testimonial. If adding one, verify exact words and intended-use permission and retain attribution; do not rewrite a quote to satisfy readability. Prefer an approved simple excerpt. A quote is that person's experience, not a universal result.

## Recording and edit acceptance

- The first 30 seconds invite time for self, learning and belonging.
- Four-position explanation stays near 25 seconds and is not a fixed four-lesson schedule.
- Adults hear required fifteen-minute chat, elective training afterward, Sandy confirmation, and later-day choice.
- Youth hear their own class path with parent/guardian staying.
- First free / next three paid remains audible and readable.
- The four-in-fourteen target is a learning aim under a suitable paid plan and available dates.
- Final action routes by who wants to start; it does not send all families through the adult chat.
- The user can locate proof clips or use the labeled demo/Sandy-on-camera fallbacks.
- Final caption and voice changes get another copy check. The draft is not a recorded or published video.
