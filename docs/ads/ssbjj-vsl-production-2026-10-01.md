### Opening

New to Jiu-Jitsu? Come meet me first.

I'm Sandy. I teach kids, teens, and adults here in Tannersville, on the mountaintop.

You might want to learn for yourself. You might want to find a class for your child. Come see how we start.

### The first step

Your first visit is free. We spend 15 minutes on your goals. Wear your day clothes. I'll show you the mats and walk you through safety.

We can talk about the days that fit your week. I'll help you choose and plan your first class.

### The class experience

In class, I coach you through each step. You learn how to move with a partner. We use games and practice to build your skills. You start at a pace that fits you.

You can ask me questions as you learn. I'll help you work through the next step.

If your child wants to learn, we can talk about their first class. I'll help you find the right start for each of you.

### The invitation

Your first class is free. Come meet me, see the space, and make a plan to start.

Tap the link to reserve your free first visit.

Start calm. Train smart.
