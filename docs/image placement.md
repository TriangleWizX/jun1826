Eyebrow:

### Video versions

**Short version:**
8–15 seconds, muted, looping, `playsinline`, no controls.

Suggested sequence:

1. Spraying
2. Lifting or separating mats
3. Sweeping underneath
4. Clean rebuilt room

**Full version:**
30–60 seconds, click-to-play below the short loop.

Button:

> Watch the Full Studio Reset

Add a poster frame showing the mats partially lifted. That frame communicates more operational care than an ordinary shot of clean mats.

Prepare:

* Wide building and parking images at approximately 16:9
* Door and hallway images at approximately 4:5
* Full-width images around 1,600 pixels wide
* Card images around 800 pixels wide
* WebP or AVIF images
* MP4 and WebM video versions
* A poster image for the video
* Muted video by default
* Native captions or a brief transcript
* Lazy loading below the fold
