# Plan to Update `/law-enforcement-bjj`

## Page Objective

Turn the page into the full landing page for the site’s new **Tactical Longevity** program lane.

The page should answer two questions:

> **Can I train here as an individual officer or first responder?**

> **Can my agency arrange a private training block?**

The primary conversion should be an individual booking a **15-minute Goal Mapping Session**. The department pilot becomes the secondary path.

The homepage already introduces this audience as **“LEO & First Responders: Tactical Longevity,”** while pricing places individual participants inside Adult Core Culture at the **$600 Community Service Rate**. The updated page should use the same hierarchy and language. ([Sensei Sandy BJJ][1])

---

# 1. Reposition the Page

## Current problem

The page begins with the right new name, but quickly turns into a long departmental arrest-control proposal. It contains:

* Two competing buyer paths above the fold
* A negative section titled “Why standard sport Jiu-Jitsu fails on duty”
* Departmental outcome statistics before strong local proof
* A complete 36-session tactical curriculum
* Three separate pricing tiers
* Repeated sections covering similar techniques
* A generic sitewide CTA written for kids, teens, and adults

This makes the page feel more like a training manual than the polished, personal, concierge-style program page used elsewhere on the site. ([Sensei Sandy BJJ][2])

## New positioning

**Tactical Longevity is the community-service version of the adult program.**

Lead with:

* Sustainable physical capability
* Composure under pressure
* Mobility and positional confidence
* A demanding outlet outside work
* Small-class coaching
* A schedule officers can realistically maintain

Treat cuffing transitions, team control, policy alignment, and private scenarios as features of the **agency pilot**, not the main promise to every individual visitor.

# 13. FAQ Replacement

Use six practical questions.

## Can I join without department approval?

Yes. Individual officers and first responders can join the regular adult program through the Community Service Rate.

## Do I need previous BJJ experience?

No. Your Goal Mapping Session identifies the appropriate starting pace and class.

## Is this a separate law-enforcement class?

Individual participants train inside the regular adult program. Private officer-specific blocks are arranged separately for agencies.

## Will I train with handcuffs or duty equipment?

Regular adult classes focus on Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu foundations. Agency pilots may include approved training equipment and role-specific exercises after a policy and safety review.

## Does this replace department training?

No. Tactical Longevity is supplemental training. Agency policy, New York requirements, departmental instruction, legal guidance, and medical restrictions control all on-duty applications.

## Can training work around rotating shifts?

Yes. Participants can use the available adult classes and coordinate changes with Sandy by text.
