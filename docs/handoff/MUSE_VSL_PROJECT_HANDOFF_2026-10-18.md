## 1. Executive Summary & VSL Project Mission

### 1.1. What is the VSL Project?
The **Sensei Sandy BJJ Video Sales Letter (VSL) Project** is a high-converting, direct-response video campaign designed to solve the primary conversion bottleneck for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in the Northern Catskills: **the intimidation barrier.**

For most everyday adults and parents in mountaintop towns like Tannersville, Hunter, Windham, Haines Falls, and Phoenicia, the idea of walking into a martial arts dojo brings anxiety:
* *"I am out of shape; will I get smashed or hurt?"*
* *"Is this a testosterone-heavy fight gym with an aggressive ego culture?"*
* *"Will I be locked into a multi-year gym contract before I even know if I like it?"*
* *"Can a total beginner survive day one?"*

The VSL addresses these concerns directly through founder **Sandy Jose Nunez's** authentic voice and calm presence. By showing real coaching on the mats, demystifying the first visit, and clearly explaining the **Beginner Lane**, the video turns passive, curious local residents into booked **Free First Visits**.

### 1.2. The Core Conversion Funnel
All video formats direct viewers into one unified, frictionless customer journey:

$$\text{Video Hook (Paid Ad / Organic Reel / Web Embed)} \longrightarrow \text{Request Free First Visit (SMS / Link)} \longrightarrow \text{15-Minute Goal Mapping Visit} \longrightarrow \text{Elective Coached First Class} \longrightarrow \text{12-Week Core Culture Enrollment}$$

* **Primary Call-to-Action (CTA):** **"Reserve Free Intro"** / **"Book your free visit"**
* **Universal Social Trigger:** **`Comment START for the link.`**
* **Direct Coordination Line:** `+1 (917) 736-8649` (Text Sandy)
* **Web Landing Page:** [`/free-bjj-intro-tannersville-ny#booking-flow`](https://senseisandy.com/free-bjj-intro-tannersville-ny#booking-flow)
* **Key Promise:** *"Start calm. Train smart. Your first class is a coached learning experience."*

## 5. Physical Filming & Production Order (For Sandy)

Filming an 11-stage script in sequential order often results in a tired voice and stiff delivery by the time the crucial hooks are filmed. 

To maximize vocal warmth and natural energy, the production team uses an **Energy-Calibrated Filming Order** (`SHOOT-ORDER.md`):

```
Step 1: The Chunky Middle Blocks (Vocal Warm-Up)
  • S05 (Beginner Lane - 45s target)
  • S06 (First-Class Logistics - 40s target)
  • S04 (Lineage & Coaching Background - 75s target)
  │ Sandy gets comfortable, speaks naturally, and establishes rhythm.
  ▼
Step 2: The Logistics & Reassurance Blocks (Conversational Ease)
  • S09 (Price Expectations - 15s target)
  • S07 (The 15-Minute Visit Deliverable - 10s target)
  • S08 (Student Choice & Next Step - 10s target)
  • S10 (Booking Calendar - 10s target)
  │ Steady, relaxed pacing; clear, practical instructions.
  ▼
Step 3: The High-Stakes Hooks & CTAs (Peak Energy & Warmth)
  • S02 (Academy Mission: Start calm - 6s target)
  • S03 (Coaching Proof: See a move - 15s target)
  • S01 (Audience Hook: Time for you - 7s target)
  • S11 (Final CTA: Book your free visit - 7s target)
  • social_ending (Comment START - 8s target)
  • discovery_cut (16-30s Fast Reel - 20s target)
  │ Sandy's vocal cords are fully warm; smiles and eye contact are natural.
```

### Physical Production Rules:
1. **Camera Position:** Smartphone camera mounted vertically (for social) or horizontally (for web master) at exact eye level, 2–3 feet from Sandy.
2. **Lighting:** Soft natural light or a diffuser ring facing Sandy; no harsh backlight.
3. **Audio Capture:** Lav mic pinned 6 inches below the chin. Record 5 seconds of silent room tone before speaking.
4. **Take Protocol:** Record two complete takes per block. Leave 2 seconds of silence before and after each take.

## 6. Mobile Video Assembly Protocol (InShot on Android)

The complete editing walkthrough is documented in `ANDROID-EDIT-FULL-GUIDE-2026-10-08.md`.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PIP LAYER (Cutaways & B-Roll): Volume set to 0%; trimmed to speech cues    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TEXT LAYER: Block Titles & Captions placed in lower-third safe zone        │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ MAIN TRACK (A-Roll): Sandy's talking-head takes (S01 to S11 in viewer order)│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Critical Editing Rules for Muse & Editor:
1. **Dialogue First:** Place all 11 talking takes onto the main timeline first. Trim head/tail pauses. Verify that the spoken narrative flows smoothly before adding any cutaway footage.
2. **Never Stretch or Rush Audio:** The 240-second number is an *edit allocation*, not a required runtime. If Sandy finishes speech in 90 seconds, use intentional visual holds of coaching demonstrations rather than artificial speed-ups.
3. **B-Roll via PIP:** Add class and dojo cutaways using the **PIP (Picture-in-Picture)** layer. **Set PIP volume to 0%** so Sandy's voiceover remains continuous and unobstructed.
4. **Title & Caption Typography:** Use a clean, sans-serif font with a subtle solid or semi-transparent backing plate for high contrast over moving mats.
5. **Captions Checked Muted:** Play back the entire export with the sound muted. Every single step (especially Sandy's confirmation and the free-class policy) must be legible.
