# Free First Visit: staff guide

Current entry policy, October 2, 2026. Authority: `src/_data/free-intro.json` and `src/_data/visit-preparation.json`. This guide replaces the earlier draft's show-rate forecasts, five-message cadence, and obsolete fourteen-day guarantee.

## Confirm the right start

1. Check who is starting, their goals, and days that fit.
2. Adults complete a fifteen-minute goal visit before training. They may elect their free first class that same day or on a later day. Sandy confirms the class, space, partner, gear, and arrival time.
3. For a visit alone, day clothes are fine. For training, use clean sports clothes plus Sandy's class-specific gear plan. A same-day guest arrives in time for the full goal visit before class; a generic ten-minute class arrival rule does not replace that visit.
4. Complete the waiver before adult training. Explain choices and match the partner and pace. Live partner practice and the Sandy Challenge remain elective under their current rules. A student may pause or end a round.
5. Youth use their own confirmed visit-plus-class workflow. A parent or guardian stays. Arrive twenty minutes before class; allow sixty to seventy-five minutes for the visit. A verified waiver is required before confirmation. Check partner fit and gear; a loaner depends on stock.
6. Treat a request, confirmed booking, attended visit, attended class, and paid choice as separate events. A calendar booking for the adult goal visit does not confirm class participation.

## Free and paid classes

The general local offer is one free first class. If a student continues, later classes use the paid plan they choose. Give consistent written price, term, frequency, and inclusions before enrollment. The fourteen-day learning aim is four attended classes covering standing, guard, chest to chest, and chest to back. The free class counts as class one. Attendance and understanding require separate checks.

The member-invited adult Shiney Ticket is a separate offer with its own four-free-class rules. Use its current playbook and records. The paid Training Fit Guarantee has separate eligibility and full terms; it is not a fourteen-day trial guarantee. See `/guarantee-terms`.

## Short message drafts

Send only through an agreed channel, after the stated details are true. Check actual names, dates, class gear, and links. Reply coverage determines timing; this guide sets no response-time promise and activates no automation.

**Visit only**

> Hi [name], this is Sandy. Your visit is set for [date] at [time]. We will talk for fifteen minutes. Wear your day clothes. Text me if your plans change.

**Adult visit and same-day class**

> Hi [name], your visit is set for [date] at [time]. We will talk for fifteen minutes. Then your free class starts at [class time]. Bring clean sports clothes. Follow this gear plan: [link]. Fill out your waiver before you train: [link].

**Youth visit and class**

> Hi [name], Sandy here. Your child’s class is set for [date] at [time]. Come at [arrival time] to meet first. A parent or guardian must stay. Follow this gear plan: [link]. Text me if your plans change.

Use that youth message only after the waiver is verified and Sandy confirms the class. The request receipt must keep its pending status.

**Reminder**

> Hi [name], I look forward to seeing you on [date]. Here is how to find us: [link]. Text me if you need help with the plan.

**After the free class**

> What felt clear? What would you like help with? If you want to keep training, we can review the paid plans. Then we can pick your next class.

**Missed class**

> Would a new class time help? Tell me what works. I will check for space.

Youth receive one complimentary change; a second missed appointment requires Sandy's review. Paid class changes follow the student's actual plan and space/partner fit.
