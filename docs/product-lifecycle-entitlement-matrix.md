# Product lifecycle and entitlement matrix

This matrix follows the canonical policy in `src/_data/pricing.json`.

| Product | Lifecycle stage | Term | Gi | Progress feedback | Rescheduling | Guarantee | Continuation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Youth Core Culture | First paid term | 12 weeks | One gi with first qualifying Core Culture enrollment | Coached progress feedback | Open-seat, capacity and suitable-partner fit required | Eligible on the first Youth Core Culture term | Review at term end |
| Adult Core Culture | First paid term | 12 weeks | One gi with first qualifying Core Culture enrollment | Coached progress feedback | Open-seat, capacity and suitable-partner fit required | Eligible on the first Adult Core Culture term | Review at term end |
| Community-Service Adult Core Culture | First paid term | 12 weeks | Core Culture gi rule applies | Coached progress feedback | Open-seat, capacity and suitable-partner fit required | Excluded by current guarantee terms | Review at term end |
| Renewed Core Culture | Continuation | 12 weeks | No additional gi by virtue of renewal | Coached progress feedback | Open-seat, capacity and suitable-partner fit required | Excluded as a renewed term | Yes, by choice |
| Elite Concierge | Continuation | 12 weeks | No additional gi; first qualifying Core Culture gi carries forward | Adult Core Culture progress model | First consideration for a suitable open replacement seat; capacity and placement still control | Excluded | Review and choose next fit |
| Annual Track | Continuation | 12 months / four 12-week cycles | No additional gi; use Core Culture gi | Progress review after each cycle | Text-based open-seat rescheduling; no unrestricted attendance | Excluded | Review before another term |
| Class packs and visitor options | Flexible / visitor | Product-specific | No Core Culture gi entitlement | Not a Core Culture progress path | Availability-based | Excluded | Product-specific |

Renewal is a review and choice. It is not automatic escalation, a rank promotion, or an attendance virtue test.
