## 6. Safety, Youth Privacy & Facility Protocols

### 1. Youth Privacy & Opt-In Photo Policy
- **Strict Consent Rule**: Studio staff do not publish or broadcast photos of attending children without explicit, signed, in-person parental consent.
- **Family Phone Priority**: Staff encourage parents and guardians to use their own smartphones to capture family memories.
- **Friendly Non-Verbal Sasquatch Cues**: The Sasquatch performer maintains a seated or kneeling posture when interacting with small children to prevent intimidation, offers gentle waves, and gives thumbs-up gestures.

### 2. Stairway & Traffic Safety Walk
- **Handrails & Illumination**: The 2nd-floor stairway must remain fully lit with 100-watt equivalent warm LED illumination from 4:00 PM to 8:30 PM.
- **Wet-Weather Mats**: High-traction moisture-absorbent rubber mats placed at the street door entrance and at the top of the stairs.
- **One-Way Foot Traffic Flow**: Clear arrows guide visitors in through the main studio entrance, past refreshments, to the photo station, and toward the exit corridor.

### 3. Studio Capacity & Environmental Control
- **Maximum Room Occupancy**: Capped at 25 concurrent guests inside the studio. If occupancy reaches 25, the street greeter gently holds incoming groups on the landing for 2–3 minutes.
- **Shoe Policy**: Street footwear is strictly removed at the entry cubbies before stepping onto the tatami mats.
- **Alcohol-Free Facility**: Strictly alcohol-free premises. No alcohol permitted inside the studio under any circumstances.
