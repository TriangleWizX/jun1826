## 3. Detailed Decision Briefs

### Gate 1: Event Go / No-Go
- **Context**: The Tannersville SPTO conducts its annual Trick or Truck along Main Street from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Sensei Sandy BJJ serves as an indoor hospitality and photo hub.
- **Criteria**: Studio availability verified; morning Saturday adult class concludes normally at 11:30 AM; afternoon transition window (11:30 AM – 4:00 PM) allows full cleaning and staging.
- **Decision**: **APPROVED (GO)**.

### Gate 2: Date & Operational Hours
- **Date**: Saturday, October 31, 2026.
- **Schedule**:
  - 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM: Regular Saturday Adult No-Gi (unaltered canonical schedule).
  - 4:00 PM – 4:45 PM: Activation staging, warm drink preparation, lighting setup.
  - 4:45 PM – 5:00 PM: Final costume check and team walk-through.
  - 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM: Public activation hours coinciding with SPTO Trick or Truck.
  - 8:00 PM – 8:45 PM: Sanitization, floor mopping, equipment teardown.
- **Decision**: **APPROVED**.

### Gate 3: Staffing & Backup Roles
- **Lead Host & Head Instructor**: Sandy Nunez
  - *Role*: Welcome families, introduce the academy, answer parent questions about kids/teen programs, manage room flow.
- **Sasquatch Costume Performer**: Assigned academy assistant / volunteer
  - *Role*: Friendly non-verbal photo poses, wave to passing trick-or-treaters, take scheduled breaks.
- **Backup Relief Performer**: Secondary roster member on standby for costume rotation if needed.
- **Refreshment & Greeting Host**: Floor Assistant
  - *Role*: Cider and coffee station maintenance, cup distribution, take-home palm card handoff, stairway monitoring.
- **Decision**: **APPROVED**.

### Gate 4: Sasquatch Operator Protocol & Hygiene
- **Sanitization**: Costume interior sanitized with antimicrobial garment spray before and after use.
- **Hydration & Temperature Control**: Mandatory 15-minute breaks scheduled at 6:15 PM and 7:15 PM in private back office. Cold bottled water and ice packs provided for performer.
- **Child-Friendly Body Language**:
  - Performer remains seated on the photo bench or kneeling on the photo mat.
  - No sudden movements, loud vocalizations, or spooky jumps.
  - Gentle open-hand waves and thumbs-up gestures only.
- **Decision**: **APPROVED**.

### Gate 5: Capacity, Stairway & Child Safety
- **Maximum Room Occupancy**: Strict limit of 25 people in the studio at any moment.
- **Stairway Protocol**:
  - Handrails checked for structural stability.
  - Warm 100W-equivalent LED lighting active along the full stair run.
  - Heavy-duty ribbed rubber moisture runners at base and landing.
- **Mat Hygiene**: Street footwear removed at entrance shoe racks. Mats mopped with hospital-grade disinfectant before doors open and immediately after closing.
- **Child Privacy**: Photography by studio staff is strictly restricted unless signed in-person parental consent is provided. Parents photograph their own children using personal phones.
- **Decision**: **APPROVED**.

### Gate 6: Weather Contingency Plan
- **Cold / Mountain Wind**: Studio thermostats preset to 68°F. Hot drinks offered immediately upon arrival to warm up chilly hands.
- **Rain / Sleet Contingency**: Large umbrella stand and boot drying trays staged at entry landing. Wet-floor warning signs placed at base of stairs.
- **Decision**: **APPROVED**.

### Gate 7: Marketing & Promotion Clearance
- **Web Anchor**: Published at `https://senseisandy.com/halloween-spooktacular` with valid Schema.org `Event` metadata and mobile-responsive layout.
- **Print Materials**: 4" x 6" Take-Home First-Visit palm cards printed; 11" x 17" street posters placed on community boards.
- **Digital Promotion**: Social Reels and static ad concepts aligned with 4-rubric quality standards, `#catskills #bjj #tannersvilleny #hudsonvalley` tags, and `START` comment trigger.
- **Decision**: **APPROVED**.
