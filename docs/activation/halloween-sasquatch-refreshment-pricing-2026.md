## 5. Food Safety & Operational Procedures
1. **Urn Temperature Settings**:
   - Electric cider urn maintained at 145°F–155°F (warm, pleasant drinking temperature; avoids scald hazards for small children).
   - Coffee airpot maintained at 160°F.
2. **Dispensing Protocol**:
   - Adult staff member dispenses hot cider and coffee behind the hospitality table.
   - Children are not permitted to operate the hot urn spigots directly.
3. **Cup Handling**:
   - Every hot beverage is handed directly to an adult or teen with a secure lid and protective heat sleeve attached.
4. **Allergen Transparency**:
   - Sign placed on the refreshment table: *"Fresh local apple cider brewed with whole cinnamon. Coffee with dairy and oat milk options. Packaged fruit snacks contain honey. Please inform our host of any food sensitivities."*
