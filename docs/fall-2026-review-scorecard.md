# Fall 2026 Practice Schedule Review

Review date: October 26, 2026  
Measurement window: September 14–October 23, 2026  
Treatment: Monday Youth/Teen Gi → No-Gi; class time remains 5:00 PM  
Capacity: 12 students per class

## Data-quality gate

Do not make a schedule decision until these are true:

- Six pilot Mondays are recorded, or the pilot is explicitly extended.
- Six comparable pre-pilot Monday opportunities are identified and closures are documented.
- Tuesday and Friday Gi control classes are complete enough to compare.
- Reservations, attendance, late arrivals, cancellations, and capacity are reconciled.
- Three intended home classes and actual home-class attendance are available.
- Campaign source, athlete status, intro stage, and Core Culture stage are joined without exposing PII in analytics.
- Time-conflict comments identify the affected day/time and whether the conflict recurs.

If any item is missing, choose **Extend the pilot** rather than treating missing data as a negative result.

## Metrics

- Median attendance: middle attendance result for the class slot.
- Fill rate: attendance ÷ 12-person capacity.
- Reservation show rate: attended ÷ reserved.
- Home-class adherence: attended assigned classes ÷ available assigned classes.
- Intro conversion: new Core Culture enrollments ÷ completed Intros.
- Fourteen-day repeat rate: Intro students returning within 14 days ÷ completed Intros.
- Time-conflict rate: families reporting a recurring timing problem ÷ families assigned to that time.
- Late-arrival rate: arrivals more than 10 minutes late ÷ total arrivals.

## Time-change rule

Do not change a time because of one empty class. Consider a time test only when at least two conditions are true:

- Weak median attendance over six or more opportunities.
- At least three separate families report the same recurring conflict.
- More than 25% of arrivals are consistently late.
- Leads repeatedly choose another day because of the time.
- The move does not collide with the next class, facility access, or instructor availability.

If a time test is approved, keep the Monday format unchanged during that test. Do not change format and time together.

## Decision

### Keep everything

Monday No-Gi performs well, Gi attendance remains stable, and families can protect 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM.

### Keep formats; test one time

Monday No-Gi works, but one slot has a documented transportation problem that meets the time-change rule.

### Return Monday to Gi

Monday attendance or adherence falls, athletes do not use the class, or Tuesday/Friday Gi demand exceeds viable Gi opportunities.

### Extend the pilot

Attendance records are incomplete, closures or illness reduced the sample, or the results remain too close to distinguish.

### Consolidate a class

The slot remains weak, families have viable alternatives, and consolidation improves partner matching without exceeding the 12-person cap.
