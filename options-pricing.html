First Step

Free First Visit & Goal Mapping Visit

Every new student starts with a 15-minute Goal Mapping visit to tour the studio, meet Sandy, and select the right class lane. The general path schedules class afterward in a separate visit; the specialized youth path can place Goal Mapping immediately before class. Normal clothes are fine for the general conversation.

Core Culture Memberships (12 Weeks)

Our primary 12-week learning blocks with three planned weekly home classes, up to 36 scheduled opportunities, and clear feedback on what is working and what comes next.

Core Culture is a structured training relationship, not anonymous access to a mat: Sandy matches the starting conditions, watches what changes, and helps choose the next useful challenge.

Youth Core Culture is one shared product for ages 5–17. Kids ages 5–9 and teens ages 10–17 use different developmental guidance while sharing the scheduled Youth + Teen class.

When life interrupts a home class, text Sandy. Open-seat rescheduling depends on room capacity and suitable partner fit; a replacement seat is not guaranteed.

Coached continuity

Progress you can understand.

Sandy watches what is becoming reliable in live training, identifies the next useful problem, and gives students and families clear feedback along the way.

One class can show where you are starting. Repeated training gives Sandy enough situations to see what is changing and what needs a different challenge. That is why Core Culture is organized as a 12-week first term rather than a collection of unrelated drop-ins.

  1. Notice: What is working?
  2. Coach: What problem comes next?
  3. Review: What has changed?
