30-Day Confidence Guarantee

Your first Core Culture term is protected. Attend at least four semi-private classes during your first 30 calendar days. If the program is not the right fit, text Sandy within those 30 days. We will help adjust your plan or, at your request, refund your first Core Culture program payment in full.

Applies to a student’s first Youth or Adult Core Culture term only. Annual plans, renewals, visitor options, semi-private class packs, private lessons, merchandise, and optional add-ons are excluded.
