How I Teach

Instructional Philosophy

Our approach is built on judgment, patience, and adapting to the student.

  • Constraints-Led Learning

    We adapt the drill to the student, not the student to the drill.

  • Guided Resistance

    Learning happens in the sweet spot between comfort and challenge.

  • Sustainable Progression

    Techniques are tools, but the goal is a practice you can keep for a lifetime.

What your first visit feels like

A calm first small-group class path for beginners

You do not need experience before you come in. First we help you get oriented, then we explain the safety basics, then we place you in the lane that fits your age and goals.
