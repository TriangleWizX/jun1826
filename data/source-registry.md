## Source Status Definitions

* **Verified**: Source returned successfully (HTTP 200) within the last 90 days.
* **Redirecting**: Source works but has changed location (HTTP 3xx). Internal pages should link to the final destination or internal summary.
* **Archived**: Original source disappeared; citation record remains with a captured backup summary.
* **Unavailable**: Source could not be independently reviewed or returns an error (HTTP 4xx/5xx).
