FAQ

Questions before you invite them.

The short version: your friend can be brand new, and Sandy will guide the first visit.

Is the Friend Pass free?

Yes. Your friend’s first class is complimentary.

Who can invite a friend?

Active Sensei Sandy BJJ students can invite a first-time guest.

Does my friend need experience?

No. Your friend can start as a beginner, and Sandy will guide the first visit.

Can kids bring a school friend?

Yes. Add the friend's name, age, and parent contact information in the form.

Does a parent need to attend?

Yes. If the guest is under 18, a parent or guardian must accompany them for the safety walkthrough.

What should my friend wear?

Comfortable athletic clothing without zippers or metal buttons, like a t-shirt and shorts or joggers.

Does my friend need a waiver?

Yes. Every guest must complete the digital waiver online before stepping onto the mats.

Can siblings come together?

Yes. Add each guest's name and age in the request form so Sandy can help choose the right class lane.

What happens after the first class?

After class, Sandy will check in by text. Guests who want to continue can complete Goal Mapping and choose three recurring home classes inside a 12-week Core Culture term. There is no obligation to enroll.
